A. Background 
The free-floating subdivision *--Congresses* is used under subjects for collections of 
conference proceedings on those subjects as well as for works about congresses on those 
subjects. This instruction sheet provides guidelines for the use of *--Congresses* as both a form 
and topical subdivision. For classification of congresses, see F 240. 
LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead 
assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. 
*Textbooks*. To replace a form subdivision that includes a demographic group, also assign 
Library of Congress Demographic Groups Terms (LCDGT) to bring out the audience of the 
resource being cataloged, e.g. *German speakers* or *Arabic, Students of*.

C. Congresses as a form subdivision 
Use *--Congresses* as a form subdivision under subjects of all types to represent either: (1) the 
collected papers delivered at, or published on the occasion of, one or several congresses, 
symposia, conferences, meetings, etc.; (2) condensations or abstracts of such papers; (3)

reports of the proceedings or discussions, preprints, program statements, lists of delegates, 
etc., of such congresses; or (4) combinations of these types of materials. 
LC practice: Record subject heading(s) for the topic of the conference(s). Use the LCGFT 
*Conference papers and proceedings* for published records of conferences, including 
abstracts, reports, and the papers presented. Use LCGFT *Conference materials* for ephemeral 
materials such as flyers, program books, daily newsletters, or newspapers that are distributed 
at conferences. 
D. Meetings of a society or institution or institution 
For the proceedings of a meeting or meetings of a society or institution on a specific subject, 
use the subdivision *--Congresses* under the subject. For the annual meeting of a corporate 
body or other proceedings dealing with the internal affairs of a society or institution, use the 
subdivision *--Congresses* under the name of the corporate body, society, etc. 
LC practice: Record subject heading(s) for the topic of the meeting or the corporate body that is 
hosting the conference for its internal affairs. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or 
genre of the resource in hand, e.g. *Conference papers and proceedings* and *Yearbooks*. 
E. Lectures published separately 
Use the form subdivision *--Congresses* for collections of two or more lectures or papers, or of 
contributions, essays, etc., that are based on papers, originally presented at a conference or 
meeting and identified with that meeting. Omit the subdivision *--Congresses* when one or 
several lectures, originally delivered on the occasion of an individual meeting, are later 
published under separate cover, and no longer identified with the original meeting. 
LC practice: Record subject heading(s) for the topic of the lectures, papers, etc., that are based 
on papers, originally presented at a conference or meeting and identified with that meeting. 
Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. *Conference 
papers and proceedings* and *Lectures* and/or *Essays*. 
F. Legislative hearings 
Do not use the subdivision *--Congresses* for the hearings of legislative bodies. 
LC practice: Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. 
*Legislative hearings*.

G. Use of form subdivisions after *--Congresses* 
Further subdivide *--Congresses* by the form subdivisions *--Calendars*, *--Directories*, or *-
-Juvenile literature*, if appropriate. Do not use the form subdivisions *--Abstracts* or *--
Periodicals* after the subdivision *--Congresses*. 
LC practice: Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. 
*Conference papers and proceedings* and *Directories*. 
E. --Congresses as a topical subdivision 
Use *--Congresses* as a topical subdivision under subjects for works about congresses, 
conferences, meetings, etc., on those subjects.  
Example: 
Title: The guide to writers conferences. 
650 #0 $a Authorship $x Congresses $v Directories. 
LC practice:  
650 #0 $a Authorship $x Congresses. 
655 #7 $a Directories. $2 lcgft 
Example: 
Title: ERIC calendar of education-related conferences. 
650 #0 $a Education $x Congresses $v Calendars. 
LC practice: 
650 #0 $a Education $x Congresses. 
655 #7 $a Calendars. $2 lcgft 
F. The subdivision –Congresses--Attendance 
Use the free-floating topical subdivision *--Congresses--Attendance* under subjects for works 
on attendance at or participation in congresses on those subjects.  
Example:  
Title: Conference attendance patterns among physicists. 
650 #0 $a Physics $x Congresses $x Attendance. 
650 #0 $a Physicists $z Great Britain.
