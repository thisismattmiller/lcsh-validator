A. Background 
LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead 
assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. 
*Catalogs*. 
For guidelines on catalogs, see H 1360. For guidelines on catalogs of library materials, see H 
1361. For guidelines on collections of objects, see H 1427. For guidelines on publishers’ 
catalogs, see H 1965. 
B. Recent revisions 
2026-02: Updated template. Added instructions relating to LCGFT implementation. 
C. The subdivision --Imprints 
Use the free-floating form subdivision *--Imprints* under individual regions, countries, cities, 
etc., for lists of works published in those places without regard to the language in which they 
were published, for example, *United States--Imprints*. 
LC practice: To bring out the place of imprints, assign*Publications--[place]*. Also assign LCGFT 
*Bibliographies* if appropriate to the resource in hand.

D. Phrase headings 
Use phrase headings of the type *[language] imprints* for lists of works published in a 
particular language. Use geographic subdivision to refer to the place of origin, for example, 
*English imprints--India*. 
E. Imprints from places where a single language predominates 
As a general rule, assign only the heading *[place]--Imprints* for lists of works published in 
places where a single language is predominant, for example, the United States. Since English is 
the predominant language in the United States, do not assign an additional heading of the type 
*English imprints--United States*. 
F. Assignment of additional heading with [language] imprints 
When assigning a heading of the type *[language] imprints* subdivided by place, assign an 
additional heading of the type *[place]--Imprints*. 
LC practice: To bring out the place of imprints, assign *Publications--[place]*. Also assign LCGFT 
*Bibliographies*. 
Example: 
Title: A list of books in English published in India. 
650 #0 $a English imprints $z India. 
651 #0 $a India $v Imprints. 
LC practice: 
650 #0 $a English imprints $z India. 
650 #0 $a Publications $z India. 
655 #7 $a Bibliographies. $2 lcgft 
G. Catalogs 
For catalogs listing imprints existing at designated localities or available from specified sources, 
assign as appropriate either *[place]--Imprints--Catalogs* or *[language] imprints--Catalogs*. 
Note: The place used with either heading refers to place of origin. 
LC practice: To bring out the place of imprints, assign *Publications--[place]*. Also assign LCGFT 
*Bibliographies* and *Catalogs*, or the more specific term. 
Example: 
Title: The Rajasthan University Library catalog of Indian imprints in 
English.

H. Bibliography not used 
Do not further subdivide headings of the type *[language] imprints* and *[place]--Imprints* 
by the subdivision *--Bibliography*. 
LC practice: Assign LCGFT *Bibliographies*. 
I. Imprints on a specific topic 
For lists of imprints on a specific topic, also assign a heading of the type *[topic]--
Bibliography*. 
LC practice: Assign a subject heading for the topic. Also assign LCGFT *Bibliographies*. 
Example: 
Title: A list of English-language books on medicine published in India. 
650 #0 $a Medicine $v Bibliography. 
650 #0 $a English imprints $z India. 
651 #0 $a India $v Imprints. 
LC practice: 
650 #0 $a Medicine. 
650 #0 $a English imprints $z India. 
650 #0 $a Publications $z India. 
655 #7 $a Bibliographies. $2 lcgft
