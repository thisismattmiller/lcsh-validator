Professional Ethics  H 1949 
 
 
 
Subject Headings Manual 
H 1949 Page 1 
 
June 2013 
BACKGROUND:  The heading and subdivision Professional ethics are assigned to works that 
discuss rules of conduct and moral principles as they apply to all types of occupations and 
professions and to activities carried out in the workplace.  This instruction sheet provides 
guidelines for assigning this heading and subdivision. 
 
 
 
 
1.  General.  Assign the heading Professional ethics (May Subd Geog) to general works not 
limited to a particular occupation that discuss the rules of conduct, moral principles, etc., to be 
followed by persons in performing their professional duties. 
 
 
2.  Occupational groups and types of employees.  In accordance with H 1100, assign the 
free-floating subdivision BProfessional ethics (May Subd Geog) under occupational groups and 
types of employees to works that discuss rules of conduct, questions of right and wrong, etc., to 
be considered by persons in carrying out their duties in a particular field of work.  Examples: 
 
ArchitectsBProfessional ethicsBFrance 
School librariansBProfessional ethicsBUnited States 
PlumbersBProfessional ethics 
 
 
3.  Moral and ethical aspects.  To works that discuss both the rules of professional conduct in a 
particular occupation and the broad moral and ethical principles that pertain to the discipline 
associated with the occupation, assign an additional heading for the discipline subdivided by the 
free-floating subdivision BMoral and ethical aspects (May Subd Geog), in accordance with H 
1998.  Example: 
 
Title:  Surgery : ethical dilemmas. 
650 #0 $a Surgeons $x Professional ethics. 
650 #0 $a Surgery $x Moral and ethical aspects. 
 
Do not use the subdivision BMoral and ethical aspects under a topic if a phrase heading has 
been provided instead.  New phrase headings of this type should not be established, but existing 
ones may still be used.  Examples: 
 
Business ethics 
Medical ethics
