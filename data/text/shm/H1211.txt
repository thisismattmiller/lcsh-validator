Airports  H 1211 
 
 
 
Subject Headings Manual 
H 1211 Page 1 
 
June 2013 
1.  General rule.  Assign the heading Airports (May Subd Geog) to works that discuss 
collectively the airports, airfields, etc., that serve the city or region.  Example: 
 
 
Title:  Air facilities, 1967, Washington metropolitan area. 
650 #0 $a Airports $z Washington Metropolitan Area. 
 
 
 
2.  Individual airports.  To works that discuss a specific airport, assign the corporate name 
heading for the individual airport.  If the work discusses the physical plant of the airport, 
including the terminal building(s), runways, etc., also assign the heading [city]BBuildings, 
structures, etc. for the major city (or cities) that the airport serves.  Examples: 
 
 
Title:  Gateway to the world : the illustrated story of the London Airport. 
610 20 $a Heathrow Airport. 
651 #0 $a London (England) $x Buildings, structures, etc. 
 
 
Title:  A giant in Texas : a history of the Dallas-Fort Worth Regional Airport 
controversy, 1911-1974.   
610 20 $a Dallas-Fort Worth Regional Airport $x History.   
651 #0 $a Dallas (Tex.) $x Buildings, structures, etc.  
            $x History.   
651 #0 $a Fort Worth (Tex.) $x Buildings, structures, etc. 
            $x History.
