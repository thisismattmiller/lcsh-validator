A. Background 
The heading *Aerial photography* is assigned to technical works on methods of taking 
photographs from conventional aircraft. Headings of the type *Aerial photography in [topic]* 
are assigned to works that discuss methods of taking aerial photographs and using them to 
carry out work or research in a particular field. The heading *Aerial photographs* is assigned to 
general works on the handling, maintenance, cataloging, etc., of collections of aerial 
photographs. The subdivision *--Aerial photographs* is used under topical headings and under 
names of countries, cities, etc., for discussions or collections of aerial photographs, or 
reproductions of them, of a subject or place. The form subdivision *--Aerial views* is used for 
maps depicting areas observed from the air. The form subdivision *--Photographs from space* 
is used under topical headings and under names of countries, cities, etc., for collections of 
photographs, or reproductions of them, taken from outer space. This instruction sheet provides 
guidelines for assigning these headings and subdivisions.

LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead 
assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. 
*Aerial photographs*. To replace a form subdivision that includes a demographic group, also 
assign Library of Congress Demographic Groups Terms (LCDGT) to bring out the audience of the 
resource being cataloged, e.g. *German speakers* or *Arabic, Students of*.

C. Definitions 
Aerial photographs: Photographs taken from the air. 
D. Aerial photography 
D.1. General rule 
Assign the heading *Aerial photography* to technical works on methods of taking photographs 
from conventional aircraft. 
D.2. By subject 
Assign headings of the type *Aerial photography in [topic]* to works that discuss methods of 
taking aerial photographs and using them as a tool in carrying out work or research in a 
particular field. 
Example: 
Title: Color aerial photography in the plant sciences and related 
fields. 
650 #0 $a Aerial photography in agriculture. 
650 #0 $a Aerial photography in forestry. 
650 #0 $a Aerial photography in botany. 
650 #0 $a Color photography. 
E. Aerial photographs 
E.1. General 
Assign the heading *Aerial photographs* to general works on the handling, maintenance, 
cataloging, etc., of collections of aerial photographs.

E.2. By subject 
Assign the free-floating subdivision *--Aerial photographs* under topical headings to 
collections or discussions of aerial photographs on these topics. 
LC practice: Assign the subject heading for the topic for collections of aerial photographs or 
reproductions of the photographs on the topic. Add the free-floating topical subdivision for 
discussions of aerial photographs of the topic. Do not use the form subdivision. Assign LCGFT 
appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Aerial 
photographs*. 
E.3. By place 
Assign the free-floating subdivision *--Aerial photographs* under names of countries, cities, 
etc., to discussions or collections of aerial photographs, including reproductions, of these 
places. 
LC practice: Assign the subject heading for the place for collections of aerial photographs or 
reproductions of the photographs of the place. Add the free-floating topical subdivision for 
discussions of aerial photographs of the place. Do not use the form subdivision. Assign LCGFT 
appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Aerial 
photographs*. 
Example: 
Title: Above Baton Rouge : a pilot's view then and now. 
651 #0 $a Baton Rouge (La.) $v Aerial photographs. 
651 #0 $a Baton Rouge (La.) $x History $v Sources. 
650 #0 $a Repeat photography. 
LC practice: 
651 #0 $a Baton Rouge (La.) $x History. 
650 #0 $a Repeat photography. 
655 #7 $a Aerial photographs $2 lcgft 
F. Aerial photographs 
Assign the free-floating form subdivision *--Aerial views* under names of countries, cities, etc., 
or under individual educational institutions, to maps or atlases depicting those places observed 
from the air. Do not further subdivide by *--Maps*. 
LC practice: Assign the subject heading for the place for collections of aerial photography of the 
named place. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. 
LCGFT *Photomaps*.

G. Photographs from space 
Assign the free-floating form subdivision *--Photographs from space* under topical headings 
and under names of countries, cities, etc., for collections of photographs, or reproductions of 
them, taken from outer space. Use the heading *Space photography* for technical works on 
the methods of taking photographs from outer space. 
LC practice: Assign the subject heading for the place for collections of photographs from space 
or reproductions of the photographs of the named place. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form 
and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Photographs*.
