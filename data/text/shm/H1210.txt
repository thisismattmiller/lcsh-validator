Addresses, Essays, Lectures  H 1210 
 
 
 
Subject Headings Manual 
H 1210 Page 1 
 
June 2013 
BACKGROUND:  Until early 1986, the subdivision BAddresses, essays, lectures was used as a 
form subdivision to distinguish works that consist of collected papers, miscellaneous articles, 
anthologies of readings, etc., from works that consist of continuous text.  Because this 
distinction was determined not to be sufficiently useful, the subdivision was discontinued.  
References to the subdivision were removed from the subject authority file.  For classification 
of addresses, essays, and lectures, see F 300. 
 
 
 
 
1.  General rule.  Do not use the subdivision BAddresses, essays, lectures under headings 
assigned to any new work being cataloged.  To any work that would formerly have been 
assigned this subdivision, assign the appropriate headings with no form subdivision. 
 
 
 
LC practice: 
2.  Bibliographic file maintenance.   
 
a.  General.  Do not make any systematic attempt to correct the subdivision on existing 
bibliographic records.  However, if the subject headings on an individual record are 
being revised or updated for other reasons, delete BAddresses, essays, lectures whenever 
it is encountered. 
 
b.  Editions.  If a new edition of a work is being cataloged, earlier editions of which 
have been assigned headings with the subdivision BAddresses, essays, lectures, update 
MARC records of the earlier editions so that the 6XX fields in the records for all editions 
match.
