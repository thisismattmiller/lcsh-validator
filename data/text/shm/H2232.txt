Vocational Guidance  H 2232 
 
 
 
Subject Headings Manual 
H 2232 Page 1 
 
June 2013 
BACKGROUND:  The free-floating subdivision BVocational guidance is used under 
occupations, fields of endeavor, industries, names of corporate bodies, and individual military 
services, for works describing careers in these fields or organizations and/or offering advice on 
how to prepare for, enter, and succeed in these careers.  This instruction sheet provides 
guidelines for assigning this subdivision. 
 
 
 
 
1.  Professions, fields of knowledge, industries, etc.  Assign the free-floating subdivision 
BVocational guidance (May Subd Geog) under occupations, fields of endeavor, types of 
industries, trades, etc., for works on careers in these fields.  Examples: 
 
 
650 #0 $a Automobiles $x Maintenance and repair $x Vocational 
            guidance. 
650 #0 $a Bioengineering $x Vocational guidance. 
650 #0 $a Dentistry $x Vocational guidance $z United States. 
 
 
The former practice of establishing phrase headings of the type [...] as a profession for 
vocational guidance in professional fields was discontinued in 1975. 
 
 
2.  Classes of persons.  Assign the free-floating subdivision BVocational guidance (May Subd 
Geog) under classes of persons or occupational groups only if the heading for the corresponding 
field or activity does not exist and cannot be established.  Example: 
 
 
650 #0 $a Allied health personnel $x Vocational guidance. 
 
 
3.  Corporate bodies and individual military services.  Assign the free-floating subdivision 
BVocational guidance (May Subd Geog) under names of individual corporate bodies and 
individual military services for works on careers in those organizations or services.  Examples:  
 
610 10 $a United States. $b National Aeronautics and Space 
            Administration $x Vocational guidance. 
610 10 $a United States. $b Army $x Vocational guidance.
