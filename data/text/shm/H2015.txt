Religious Denominations or Religions as Subdivisions  H 2015 
 
 
 
Subject Headings Manual 
H 2015 Page 1 
 
August 2020 
BACKGROUND: Provision has been made in numerous instances in the subject authority file 
for the use of names of individual religions, churches, denominations, etc., as subdivisions under 
inherently religious headings or under other topical headings with the subdivision –Religious 
aspects, as in the following examples of headings from bibliographic records: 
 
 
650 #0 $a Fasts and feasts $x Buddhism. 
650 #0 $a Birth control $x Religious aspects $x Catholic Church. 
650 #0 $a Ordination $x Methodist Church. 
 
 
This provision may appear in the subject authority file as a multiple subdivision, for example, 
 
 
150 ## $a Baptism $x Anglican Communion, [Catholic Church, etc.] 
150 ## $a Public worship $x Judaism, [Buddhism, etc.] 
 
 
This instruction sheet describes the use of these names as subdivisions and the requirements for 
assigning additional headings when a heading of this type is assigned. For more information 
about multiple subdivisions, see sec. 2 below and H 1090. 
 
 
 
Note: The provisions of this instruction sheet apply only to those situations where the 
names of religions or denominations are used as subdivisions.  Where other currently 
acceptable patterns exist, such as the use of adjectival qualifiers, each new heading must 
be established individually according to the established pattern, for example, 
 
150 ## $a Lutheran preaching 
150 ## $a Orthodox Eastern press 
 
 
 
1.  General rule.  As necessary in current cataloging, divide inherently religious headings by 
religions or denominations if they have not yet been divided, rather than by using parenthetical 
qualifiers.  If the heading represents a concept that is not limited to the field of religion, 
establish the subdivision –Religious aspects under the topic, and also establish the subdivision to 
bring out the specific religion or denomination.  For a fuller discussion of the subdivision 
–Religious aspects, see H 1998.

2. Multiple subdivisions.   
 
 
As of July 2018, proposals for new multiple subdivisions are not being accepted and 
individual strings that formerly would have been authorized by a multiple are 
individually established. Also in 2018, the Policy and Standards Division began a project 
to separately establish each valid heading string that was created based on a multiple 
subdivision and to cancel the multiple subdivisions. Multiples that already exist remain 
valid for use until they have been cancelled. For additional information, see H 1090. 
 
 
a. Multiple already established.  If an appropriate multiple subdivision has been provided in 
the subject authority file under the required topic, assign the topic using the name of the 
pertinent religion or denomination as a free-floating subdivision. 
 
 
Note:  One multiple is normally provided for religions and sects, and a second for 
individual Christian denominations.  Example: 
 
Baptism–Hinduism, [Judaism, etc.] 
Baptism–Anglican Communion, [Catholic Church, etc.] 
 
 
The multiple for religions and sects does not authorize the use of Christian denomina-
tions.  If a denomination is to be used under the topic, a multiple specifically for 
denominations is required. 
 
 
Multiple subdivisions for Christian denominations authorize the use of either corporate 
name headings for denominations, for example, Church of England, or generic subject 
headings for larger groupings, for example, Anglican Communion or Protestant 
churches.

2. Multiple subdivisions.  (Continued)  
 
 
b.  Multiple not yet established.  In order to subdivide a religious topic for the first time by 
religions or Christian denominations, establish the full heading string as required for the work 
being cataloged. Examples: 
 
150 ## $a [topic] $x Baptists. 
150 ## $a [topic] $x Catholic Church. 
150 ## $a [topic] $x Buddhism. 
150 ## $a [topic] $x Christianity. 
 
If a heading has been subdivided in the subject authority file by one or more names without 
provision for a multiple or an authority record for the specific heading, for example, Pastoral 
medicine–Methodist Church, establish the specific heading as required for the work being 
cataloged. 
 
 
3. Assignment of headings.  When any heading is assigned in accordance with the guidelines 
above, assign as an additional heading the name of the religion or Christian denomination with 
appropriate topical subdivision, if any.  Example: 
 
650 #0 $a Ordination $x Catholic Church. 
610 20 $a Catholic Church $x Clergy. 
 
 
Do not interpose geographic subdivisions between the topic and the subdivision for religion or 
denomination.  Assign two headings instead.  Example: 
 
 
650 #0 $a Church and state $x Catholic Church. 
650 #0 $a Church and state $z Italy. 
 
 [not 650 #0 $a Church and state $z Italy $x Catholic Church.]
