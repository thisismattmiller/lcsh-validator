A. Background 
LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead 
assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. 
*Textbooks*. To replace a form subdivision that includes a demographic group, also assign 
Library of Congress Demographic Groups Terms (LCDGT) to bring out the audience of the 
resource being cataloged, e.g. *German speakers* or *Arabic, Students of*. 
B. Recent revisions 
2026-01: Added instructions related to LCGFT implementation. 
C. Types of headings covered 
The subdivisions listed in sec. E and sec. F, below, may be used on a free-floating basis, as 
appropriate, under subject headings for classes of persons, including age and sex groups; social, 
economic, and political categories of persons; types of afflicted persons; members of religions; 
employees and occupational groups; etc. 
Examples:  
Youth 
Women 
Fathers 
Poor

The category includes headings for classes of persons that are formulated with subdivisions 
used under names of places or individual corporate bodies. 
Examples: 
Great Britain--Officials and employees 
Stanford University--Faculty 
United States. Navy--Officers 
Headings for classes of persons qualified by ethnic or national adjectival qualifiers are also 
included in this category. 
Examples: 
African American dentists 
French students 
Foreign workers, Turkish 
Subdivisions from this list may be used under headings for groups of literary authors where 
there is no conflict with a subdivision specifically designated for use under literary authors in H 
1155.2.  
D. Types of headings not covered 
This instruction sheet does not apply to headings for social classes or collective social 
groupings. 
Examples of excluded headings: 
Nobility 
Working class 
Families 
Clans 
The instruction sheet also does not apply to headings for ethnic groups and nationalities. 
Examples of excluded headings: 
Hispanic Americans

For subdivisions used under ethnic groups and nationalities, see H 1103.  
This instruction sheet also does not apply to headings for members of religious and monastic 
orders. For subdivisions used under names of individual religious and monastic orders, see H 
1186. 
E. Conflicts 
Determine if the use of a free-floating subdivision from this list conflicts with a previously 
established subject heading. In the event of a conflict between an established subject heading 
and a subdivision on this list, use the established heading unless a decision has been made to 
update or change all headings of that type. For example, use the established subject heading 
*Child abuse* rather than formulate a heading of the type *Children--Abuse of*. However, 
previously established headings of the type *Teachers, Training of* were changed to the form 
*Teachers--Training of*.
