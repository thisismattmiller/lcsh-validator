A. Background 
An electronic serial is a work in a machine-readable format, issued in successive designated 
parts for an indefinite period of time (that is, it meets the definition of a serial). It may be a 
remote access serial, such as those available on the Internet, or it may be a direct access serial, 
such as those issued on CD-ROM, flash drive, etc. This instruction sheet provides guidance on 
the assignment of subject headings to electronic serials. 
LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. For 
resources that would use form subdivisions, assign the LCGFT term appropriate to the form 
and/or genre of the resource in hand. For base instructions on assigning subject headings to 
serials, see H 178. For more detailed instructions on databases, see H 1520. For more detailed 
instructions on periodicals, see H 1927. For guidelines on assigning LCGFT, see J 243. 
There is no need to express the electronic format via subject headings or genre/form terms. 
The format is carrier information expressed via descriptive cataloging values.

C. Definitions 
Electronic serials: Serials in a digital format, such as PDF, epub, etc. May be digitized or born 
digital. 
D. General rule 
To an electronic serial, assign the appropriate heading(s) subdivided by the free-floating 
subdivision *--Periodicals*. Do not further subdivide by *--Databases* to indicate that the 
serial is in electronic form. 
LC practice: Assign the appropriate subject headings for the serial publication as a whole. Also 
assign the LCGFT term appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, i.e. LCGFT 
*Periodicals*. 
Examples: 
Title: Electronic journal of differential equations. 
650 #0 $a Differential equations $v Periodicals. 
LC practice: 
650 #0 $a Differential equations. 
655 #7 $a Periodicals. $2 lcgft 
 
Title: TESL-EJ : teaching English as a second or foreign language : an 
electronic journal. 
650 #0 $a English language $x Study and teaching $x Foreign speakers $v 
Periodicals. 
LC practice: 
650 #0 $a English language $x Study and teaching $x Foreign speakers. 
655 #7 $a Periodicals. $2 lcgft 
E. Databases that are updated 
For databases that are regularly updated, follow the instructions in H 1520 Section D. Do not 
further subdivide by *--Periodicals* to indicate that the database is issued serially. 
LC practice: For databases that are regularly updated, assign the appropriate subject headings 
for the topical focus of the data collected in the database. Also assign the LCGFT term 
appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, i.e. LCGFT *Databases*. Do not 
consider the form to be a periodical. 
Examples: 
Title: Vietnam investment database.

F. Serials about databases 
For electronic serials whose subject matter is databases, follow the instructions in H 1520 
Section H and add the free-floating form subdivision *--Periodicals*. 
LC practice: Assign the appropriate subject headings for the serial publication as a whole. Also 
assign the LCGFT term appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, i.e. LCGFT 
*Periodicals*. 
G. Reference type works 
Do not assign the subdivision *--Databases* to electronic serials that have the structure of and 
present themselves as reference-type works such as bibliographies, catalogs, collections of 
abstracts, directories, dictionaries, indexes, or other similar types. Instead, assign the 
appropriate form headings or the appropriate subjects subdivided by the pertinent form 
subdivision. Further subdivide by *--Periodicals* unless the subdivision is one not further 
subdivided by *--Periodicals* according to the provisions of H 1927, sec. E. If one of these 
electronic serials is also a database, do not further subdivide by *--Databases* and do not 
assign an additional subject for the database. For guidelines to similar approaches for reference 
type works that are not serials, see H 1520 Section F.1. 
LC practice: Assign the appropriate subject headings for the topical focus of the resource. Also 
assign the LCGFT term appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT 
*Telephone directories* and *Periodicals*. If the resource contains many different types of 
genre and/or forms, including one or more databases, consider the needs of specificity, 
according to J 110. 
Example: 
Title: The Sociofile database. 
[a quarterly cumulation of abstracts from different sources in the 
field of sociology.] 
650 #0 $a Sociology $v Abstracts $v Periodicals. 
LC practice: 
650 #0 $a Sociology. 
655 #7 $a Periodicals $2 lcgft 
655 #7 $a Abstracts $2 lcgft

H. Periodicals about databases on a topic 
Assign headings of the type *[topic]--Databases--Periodicals* only to periodicals whose subject 
matter is databases on a particular subject. 
LC practice: Assign the appropriate subject headings for the serial publication as a whole. Also 
assign the LCGFT term appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, i.e. LCGFT 
*Periodicals*. 
Example: 
Title: CAD/CAM database strategies. 
[a quarterly paper journal that deals with the design of CAD/CAM 
databases.] 
650 #0 $a CAD/CAM systems $x Databases $v Periodicals. 
LC practice: 
650 #0 $a CAD/CAM systems $x Databases. 
655 #7 $a Periodicals $2 lcgft
