A. Background 
Genealogy is the study of family history and the accounts and records of the ancestry and 
descent of persons, families, groups, etc. This instruction sheet provides guidelines for 
cataloging genealogy materials, including works on individual families. For instructions on 
cataloging local history and genealogical source materials, see H 1845. For instructions on how 
to establish family names for genealogical resources, see H 1631.5. For instructions on 
establishing names of noble families, royal houses, dynasties, etc., see H 1574. For classification 
of genealogical materials, see F 600.

C. Definitions 
Genealogy: the study of ancestry, not limited to a particular place, including works on methods 
of locating and using various types of documents in order to create a family tree or write a 
family history. 
D. The heading Genealogy 
Assign the heading *Genealogy* to general works on the study of ancestry not limited to a 
particular place, including works on methods of locating and using various types of documents 
in order to create a family tree or write a family history. 
Do not subdivide this heading by *--Handbooks, manuals, etc.*, *--History*, *--Methodology*, 
*--Research*, or *--Sources*. 
E. The subdivision --Genealogy 
E.1. General 
Assign the free-floating form subdivision *--Genealogy* under names of places, individual 
corporate bodies, classes of persons, and ethnic groups to works that contain collections of 
family records and/or histories of families from the place or belonging to the organization, class 
of persons, or ethnic group. 
Do not subdivide headings of this type by *--History* or by *--Sources*. 
LC practice: Assign names of places, individual corporate bodies, classes of persons, and ethnic 
groups to works that contain collections of family records and/or histories of families from the 
place or belonging to the organization, class of persons, or ethnic group. Assign LCGFT 
appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Family histories* or 
*Genealogical tables*. 
Examples: 
650 #0 $a African Americans $v Genealogy.

E.2. Additional headings 
Assign an array of headings to bring out place, ethnic group, class of persons, types of 
documents, etc. If the work includes names with birth and/or death dates, assign also 
*Registers of births, etc.--[place]*. If the work includes more specific types of documents, 
assign specific headings, for example, *Marriage records*; *Death notices*; *Baptismal 
records*. 
LC practice: Assign an array of headings to bring out place, ethnic group, class of persons, etc. 
that is the focus of the resource. For guidelines on recording the year of the census, if that is 
the source of the data, see H 1366. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the 
resource in hand, including the types of documents, e.g. LCGFT *Vital statistics* or *Death 
registers*. 
Example: 
Title: 1850 census of Tazewell County, Virginia, transcribed and 
indexed for genealogists. 
651 #0 $a Tazewell County (Va.) $v Genealogy. 
650 #0 $a Registers of births, etc. $z Virginia $z Tazewell County. 
651 #0 $a Tazewell County (Va.) $v Census, 1850. 
651 #0 $a United States $v Census, 1850. 
650 #0 $a African Americans $z Virginia $z Tazewell County $v Genealogy. 
LC practice: 
651 #0 $a Tazewell County (Va.) $x Census, 1850. 
651 #0 $a United States $x Census, 1850. 
650 #0 $a African Americans $z Virginia $z Tazewell County.

E.3. Methodology 
Assign the free-floating subdivision *--Genealogy--Methodology* under names of places, 
individual corporate bodies, classes of persons, and ethnic groups for comprehensive texts on 
methods of locating genealogical records, tracing family histories, etc., in these places, or 
pertaining to these groups or classes of persons. 
Assign the free-floating subdivision *--Genealogy--Handbooks, manuals, etc.*, under names of 
places, individual corporate bodies, classes of persons, and ethnic groups to works that present 
brief information about genealogy on these topics, such as lists of agencies and institutions to 
consult, types of records to study, etc. 
For works containing both comprehensive instructions on methodology and brief information 
for ready reference, assign only *--Genealogy--Handbooks, manuals, etc.*, under the 
appropriate heading. 
LC practice: Assign the free-floating topical subdivision *--Genealogy--Methodology* under 
names of places, individual corporate bodies, classes of persons, and ethnic groups for 
comprehensive texts on methods of locating genealogical records, tracing family histories, etc., 
in these places, or pertaining to these groups or classes of persons. Assign LCGFT appropriate to 
the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Self-instructional works*. 
Assign the free-floating topical subdivision *--Genealogy*, under names of places, individual 
corporate bodies, classes of persons, and ethnic groups to works that present information 
about genealogy on these topics, such as lists of agencies and institutions to consult, types of 
records to study, etc. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in 
hand, e.g. LCGFT *Handbooks and manuals*. 
For works containing both comprehensive instructions on methodology and brief information 
for ready reference, assign only the topical subdivision *--Genealogy*, under the appropriate 
heading and the LCGFT term *Handbooks and manuals*. 
F. Headings for family names 
F.1. General rule 
Assign a heading for the name of the family.

Subdivide family names as needed by the free-floating form and topical subdivisions listed in H 
1120. 
Do not subdivide family names by *--Biography*, *--History*, or *--Sources*. 
Optionally, also assign a heading for the authorized access point of the family from the name 
authority record in the LC/Name Authority File (NAF). 
LC practice: Do not subdivide a family name by a form subdivision. Assign LCGFT appropriate to 
the form and/or genre of the resource in hand. 
F.2. Works that discuss more than one family 
If more than one family is discussed, assign headings for each family, up to a maximum of four. 
F.3. Location of the family 
In accordance with H 1845, bring out the location of the family in an additional heading, if 
possible. 
Example: 
Title: The Cotton family of Northern Virginia. 
600 30 $a Cotton family. 
600 10 $a Cotton, John, $d 1721-1808 $x Family. 
651 #0 $a Virginia, Northern $v Genealogy. 
LC practice: 
600 30 $a Cotton family. 
600 10 $a Cotton, John, $d 1721-1808 $x Family. 
651 #0 $a Virginia, Northern. 
655 #7 $a Family histories. $2 lcgft 
655 #7 $a Genealogical tables. $2 lcgft 
For countries other than the United States, assign an additional heading of the type *[country]-
-Genealogy*. 
If the work is a collective biography of the members of a family, do not assign a heading of the 
type *[place]--Genealogy*, unless the work also contains genealogical material. 
LC practice: For countries other than the United States, assign an additional heading for the 
place. 
Example: 
Title: Pierssené.

F.4. Progenitors and individual family members 
Assign an additional heading for the name of the progenitor, or any individual family member, 
subdivided by the free-floating subdivision *--Family* if he or she is mentioned on the title 
page or given prominent treatment in the text. 
Example: 
Title: The Giles Driver family : a compilation of the descendants of 
Giles Driver, Isle of Wight County, Virginia. 
600 30 $a Driver family. 
600 10 $a Driver, Giles, $c Sr., $d 1775-1824 $x Family. 
651 #0 $a Isle of Wight County (Va.) $v Genealogy. 
651 #0 $a Southern States $v Genealogy. 
LC practice: 
600 30 $a Driver family. 
600 10 $a Driver, Giles, $c Sr., $d 1775-1824 $x Family. 
651 #0 $a Isle of Wight County (Va.) 
651 #0 $a Southern States. 
655 #7 $a Family histories. $2 lcgft 
655 #7 $a Genealogical tables. $2 lcgft 
G. Geographic Area codes 
For genealogical or biographical works about individual families, assign codes based on the 
headings selected for the work being cataloged. Do not assign a code for the country in which 
the family resides unless another heading that includes the name of the country is assigned.
