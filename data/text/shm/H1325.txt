A. Background 
Prior to 1998, lists of bibliographies on specific topics were entered under the heading 
*Bibliography--Bibliography*, further subdivided by the appropriate topic, for example, 
*Bibliography--Bibliography--Botany*. In 1998, the heading *Bibliography--Bibliography* was 
changed to *Bibliography of bibliographies* and the free-floating form subdivision *--
Bibliography of bibliographies* was established for use under subjects. This instruction sheet 
provides guidelines for assigning headings to works that are bibliographies of bibliographies. 
LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead 
assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. 
*Bibliographies*. 
For guidelines on abstracts, see H 1205. For guidelines on bibliographies of individual people, 
see H 1322. For guidelines on bio-bibliographies, see H 1328. For catalogs of library materials, 
see H 1361. For guidelines on digests of legal topics, see H 1550. 
B. Recent revisions 
2026-02: Updated template. Added instructions related to LCGFT implementation. 
C. Definitions 
Bibliography of bibliographies: list of bibliographies 
F. General rule 
Assign the heading *Bibliography of bibliographies* to a work that consists of a list of 
bibliographies not limited to any specific topic.

G. Bibliography of bibliographies on specific topics 
To a work that consists of a list of bibliographies on a specific topic, assign the appropriate 
heading for the topic, subdivided by the free-floating form subdivision *--Bibliography of 
bibliographies*. 
LC practice: Assign a subject heading to bring out the topic. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the 
form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Bibliographies*. 
Examples: 
600 10 $a Shakespeare, William $d 1564-1616 $v Bibliography of 
bibliographies. 
650 #0 $a Outdoor recreation $v Bibliography of bibliographies. 
651 #0 $a Latin America $v Bibliography of bibliographies. 
LC practice: 
6XX #0 $a [topic]. 
655 #7 $a Bibliographies. $2 lcgft
