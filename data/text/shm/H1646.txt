A. Background 
LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead 
assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. 
*Textbooks*. To replace a form subdivision that includes a demographic group, also assign 
Library of Congress Demographic Groups Terms (LCDGT) to bring out the audience of the 
resource being cataloged, e.g. *German speakers* or *Arabic, Students of*. 
B. Recent revisions   
2026-01: Updated template. Added instructions related to LCGFT implementation. 
C. General rule 
Use *--Handbooks, manuals, etc.* as a free-floating form subdivision under subject headings of 
all types for concise reference works in which facts and information pertaining to the topic are 
arranged for ready reference and consultation rather than for continuous reading and study. 
LC practice: Assign a subject heading for the topic of the work. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the 
form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g., *Handbooks and manuals*. 
Example: 
650 ## $a Technology $v Handbooks, manuals, etc. 
LC practice: 
650 ## $a Technology.

D. Classes of persons 
Use the subdivision under classes of persons only if a heading for the corresponding field or 
activity does not exist and cannot be established, for example, *Nurses' aides--Handbooks, 
manuals, etc.* 
LC practice: Assign a subject heading for the topic of the work. Use a class of persons heading if 
the field or activity does not exist and cannot be established. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the 
form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g., *Handbooks and manuals*.
