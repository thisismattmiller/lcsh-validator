BACKGROUND:  The subdivision BPopular works is used under scientific, technical, and 
legal subjects for works on these topics intended for the layperson.  The practice of using 
BPopular works as a free-floating subdivision under any theological topic was discontinued in 
1987.  This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning this subdivision. 
 
 
 
1.  General.  Assign the free-floating form subdivision BPopular works under scientific, 
technical, and legal subjects for works in these fields addressed to the layperson.  Examples: 
 
650 #0 $a Automobiles $v Popular works. 
650 #0 $a Chemistry $v Popular works. 
650 #0 $a Divorce $x Law and legislation $z California $v Popular 
          works. 
650 #0 $a Labor laws and legislation $v Popular works. 
650 #0 $a Oceanography $v Popular works. 
 
Assign this subdivision only under those scientific, technical, and legal disciplines and topics in 
which works for the nonspecialist are distinctly different from those written for specialists in the 
field and are identified as being specifically for the layperson. 
 
Do not use this subdivision for textbooks on a topic. 
 
 
 
2.  Medicine.  Assign the heading Medicine, Popular to general works on medicine written for 
the layperson.  Example: 
 
Title:  A layman's guide to modern medicine. 
650 #0 $a Medicine, Popular. 
 
Assign the free-floating subdivision BPopular works under medical disciplines, individual 
diseases, and under headings of the type [part of the body]BDiseases for materials written for the 
layperson on the subject.  Examples: 
 
Title:  Your heart ; questions you have, answers you need. 
650 #0 $a Heart $x Diseases $v Popular works. 
 
Title:  How to get your money's worth out of psychiatry. 
650 #0 $a Psychiatry $v Popular works.

3.  Special topics.  Under other topics, where BPopular works is not authorized for use as a 
free-floating subdivision, establish the subdivision editorially if appropriate for the subject 
matter, for example, Theology, DoctrinalBPopular works. 
 
 
4.  Manuals.  Under technical subjects, assign the free-floating form subdivision BAmateurs' 
manuals for "do-it-yourself" or "how-to" manuals for the layperson on these subjects.  Do not 
interpose the subdivision BPopular works between the topic and the subdivision BAmateurs' 
manuals.  Example: 
 
Title:  Household repairs : a manual for homeowners. 
650 #0 $a Dwellings $x Maintenance and repair $v Amateurs' 
            manuals.
