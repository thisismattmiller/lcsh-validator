BACKGROUND:  The subject heading Political science, the free-floating subdivision BPolitics 
and government, and headings and subdivisions for special political topics are assigned to 
works that discuss the theory, practice, and history of politics and government and citizen 
participation in the political process.  This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning 
subject headings to works on politics and government and other political topics. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
1.  Political science.  Assign the heading Political science (May Subd Geog) to works on the 
discipline or theory of politics.  Subdivide the heading by place for works on the discipline as 
practiced in a particular place. 
 
Do not use this heading for works on the history of political events or institutions. 
 
 
 
2.  Political history.  
 
a.  General.  Assign the heading World politics to works on political history 
worldwide. 
 
Do not assign this heading to theoretical works.  For these works, use the heading 
International relations. 
 
Assign the heading World politics with established period subdivisions to works on 
world political history limited to a particular time period.  If an appropriate period 
subdivision does not exist, establish it for use under this heading.  Because of the size of 
the files, subdivisions covering ten year periods have been established routinely, 
beginning with l945-l955.  Example: 
 
Title:  Prospects for East-West relations. 
650 #0 $a World politics $y l975-1985. 
651 #0 $a United States $x Foreign relations  
            $z Soviet Union. 
651 #0 $a Soviet Union $x Foreign relations  
            $z United States.

2.  Political history.  (Continued) 
 
b.  By place.  Assign the free-floating subdivision BPolitics and government under 
names of countries, cities, etc., to works on the history of political events and/or the 
history, description, or critical analysis of governmental and political institutions, parties, 
organizations, etc., treated collectively. 
 
Further subdivide headings of the type [place]BPolitics and government by established 
chronological subdivisions or by the free-floating century subdivisions listed under 
BPolitics and government in H 1140:  B16th, [17th, 18th, 19th, 20th, or 21st] century. 
Assign century subdivisions only when they do not conflict with established subdivisions 
for specific time periods.  Establish new chronological subdivisions under headings of 
the type [place]BPolitics and government to correspond to important events in the 
history of the place, such as the reign or term of office of a ruler or president of a country. 
For places smaller than countries, establish subdivisions for significant time periods 
where there is sufficient material to warrant it.

c.  By ethnic group. 
 
(1) BPolitics and government.  Assign the free-floating subdivision BPolitics 
and government under individual ethnic groups for works on the group's 
self-government and/or the political activity of the group or its individual 
members.  Further subdivide headings of the type [ethnic group]BPolitics and 
government by the free-floating century subdivisions listed under BPolitics and 
government in H 1103: B16th, [17th, 18th, 19th, 20th, or 21st] century. 
 
(2) BGovernment relations.  Assign the free-floating subdivision BGovernment 
relations under individual ethnic groups for works on the relations between the 
group as a whole and the government of the place in which they reside.

3.  Practical politics.  Assign the heading Politics, Practical (May Subd Geog) to works on 
practical methods used in political campaigns, political party work, etc.  
 
 
To works discussing the use of practical political methods in a particular place, assign a heading 
to bring out the place.  Usually this will be a heading of the type [place]BPolitics and 
government.  
 
 
 
 
4.  Political participation. 
 
a.  General.  Assign the heading Political participation (May Subd Geog) to general 
works on the participation of citizens in the political process. 
 
 
 
b.  Classes of persons, organizations, etc.  Assign the free-floating subdivision 
BPolitical activity (May Subd Geog) under classes of persons, types of corporate bodies, 
names of individual corporate bodies, persons, families, individual military services, and 
Christian denominations for works on the political participation of these persons or 
organizations.  Example: 
 
Title:  Banners unfurled : higher education in Pakistan. 
650 #0 $a Education, Higher $z Pakistan. 
650 #0 $a College students $x Political activity 
            $z Pakistan. 
 
 
 
c.  By subject.  Assign the free-floating subdivision BCitizen participation under 
topical headings for works on the participation of citizens in carrying out an activity. 
 
 
Do not assign this subdivision under headings for broad disciplines.  Use the 
subdivision under headings for the specific activity in which citizens participate and 
share in policy decisions.

5.  Political aspects of nonpolitical topics.  Assign the free-floating subdivision BPolitical 
aspects (May Subd Geog) under topical headings for works on the political dimensions or 
implications of nonpolitical topics.  Example: 
 
 
Title:  Political dynamics of direct foreign investment. 
650 #0 $a Investments, Foreign $x Political aspects. 
 
 
 
6.  Special topics.  Assign appropriate specific headings to works on special political topics.  
Examples: 
 
Campaign funds 
Lobbying 
Local government 
Political parties 
Pressure groups 
Right and left (Political science) 
Voter registration 
 
 
 
7.  Assigning additional headings.  Works on political topics are usually multifaceted, 
requiring an array of headings to bring out the important aspects of the material.  Assign several 
headings, if appropriate, to cover major topics discussed in a work.  In most instances, one of 
these should be a heading of the type [place]BPolitics and government.  Examples: 
 
Title:  Who's running America? 
650 #0 $a Elite (Social sciences) $z United States. 
650 #0 $a Power (Social sciences) 
650 #0 $a Leadership. 
651 #0 $a United States $x Politics and government  
            $y l993-2001. 
 
 
Title:  Global collective security in the 1980s. 
650 #0 $a National security. 
650 #0 $a World politics $y 1975-1985.
