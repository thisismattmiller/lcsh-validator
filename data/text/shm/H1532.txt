BACKGROUND:  The free-floating subdivisions BDesign and construction; BDesign; 
BDesigns and plans are used under certain types of topical headings.  This instruction sheet 
provides guidelines for assigning these subdivisions. 
 
 
 
 
1.  Structures, machines, equipment.  Use the free-floating subdivision BDesign and 
construction under types of structures, machines, equipment, etc., for works on their 
engineering and/or construction, for example, AutomobilesBDesign and construction; CattleB 
HousingBDesign and construction. 
 
 
 
2.  Examinations.  Use the free-floating subdivision BDesign and construction under 
Examinations and under headings for types of tests and individual examinations for works on 
methods 
of 
designing 
and 
constructing 
tests, 
for 
example, 
Multiple 
choice 
examinationsBDesign and construction; Graduate Record ExaminationBDesign and 
construction. 
 
 
 
3.  Other topics.  Use the free-floating subdivision BDesign under other topics where the use of 
BDesign and construction would not be appropriate, for example, GardensBDesign; 
QuiltsBDesign. 
 
Do not use the free-floating subdivision if a phrase heading has been established for the concept, 
for example, Costume design; Magazine design.  Assign the phrase heading instead. 
 
 
 
4.  Designs and plans.  Use the free-floating form subdivision BDesigns and plans under 
architectural and landscape headings for works containing architectural drawings, for example, 
Church architectureBDesigns and plans; Landscape architectureBDesigns and plans.
