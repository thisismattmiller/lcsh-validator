BACKGROUND:  The subdivision BStudy and teaching is used under certain types of headings 
for works on study and teaching, including methods, institutions, facilities, programs, courses, 
funding, etc.  This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning the subdivision BStudy 
and teaching.  For classification of works on the teaching of particular subjects, see F 730. 
 
 
1.  General.  Assign the free-floating subdivision BStudy and teaching (May Subd Geog) under 
names of individual persons, corporate bodies, and places, and under classes of persons, ethnic 
groups, and topical headings for works on study and teaching about these subjects. 
 
Do not use this subdivision under music headings, or under names of individual Christian 
denominations. 
 
For some broad disciplines, phrase headings have been established to cover all aspects of study 
and teaching pertaining to these subjects, for example, Agricultural education; Technical 
education.  In these fields headings of the type [topic]BStudy and teaching are restricted in 
meaning to methods of study and teaching. 
 
 
 
2.  Level of study.  Assign the free-floating subdivision BStudy and teaching, qualified by level 
of study, to works on the study and teaching of a topic limited to a particular educational level, 
for example, HistoryBStudy and teaching (Elementary)BFrance; ScienceBStudy and 
teaching (Higher)BGreat Britain. 
 
The following free-floating subdivisions may be used, if appropriate: 
 
BStudy and teaching (Continuing education) 
BStudy and teaching (Early childhood) 
BStudy and teaching (Elementary) 
BStudy and teaching (Graduate) 
BStudy and teaching (Higher) 
BStudy and teaching (Internship) 
BStudy and teaching (Middle school) 
BStudy and teaching (Preschool) 
BStudy and teaching (Primary) 
BStudy and teaching (Residency) 
BStudy and teaching (Secondary)

3.  Supervision.  Assign the free-floating subdivision BStudy and teachingBSupervision (May 
Subd Geog) under topical headings to works on the supervision of study and teaching in these 
fields.  Example: 
 
Title:  The Art of clinical supervision : a pastoral counseling perspective. 
650 #0 $a Pastoral counseling $x Study and teaching  
            $x Supervision. 
 
 
4.  BStudy and teaching vs. BTraining of.  To works that discuss the study and teaching of a 
topic as part of training for an occupation or profession, assign headings of the type [topic]B 
Study and teaching or [...] education and [class of persons]BTraining of, if appropriate.  For 
instructions on assigning the subdivision BTraining of, see H 2217. 
 
 
5.  Places.  Assign the free-floating subdivision BStudy and teaching (May Subd Geog) under 
names of places for general works on study and teaching about these places. 
 
Phrase headings of the type [...] studies for area studies, for example, African studies, were 
discontinued in 1977 and should be changed when encountered on bibliographic records. 
 
For works on study and teaching about a place published in that place, omit the geographical 
subdivision under BStudy and teaching.  Example: 
 
Title:  Understanding the United States : a guide for American teachers. 
651 #0 $a United States $x Study and teaching (Secondary) 
 
For works on study and teaching about a country for use in a locality of the same country or for 
use in another country, subdivide BStudy and teaching by the place in which the study and 
teaching is to be carried out.  Examples: 
 
Title:  Helping Chinese students to understand the United States. 
651 #0 $a United States $x Study and teaching (Secondary)  
            $z China. 
  
Title:  Studying the United States : a guide for social studies teachers in 
Boston. 
651 #0 $a United States $x Study and teaching (Secondary) 
            $z Massachusetts $z Boston.

5.  Places.  (Continued) 
 
For works on a special aspect of study and teaching about a place, interpose a topical subdivision 
between the place and the subdivision BStudy and teaching, for example, SwedenBSocial life 
and customsBStudy and teaching. 
 
Do not interpose place between a topical heading and the subdivision BStudy and teaching. 
 
 
 
 
6.  Languages.  Assign the free-floating subdivision BStudy and teaching (May Subd Geog) 
under individual languages and groups of languages to general works on the study and teaching 
of these languages. 
 
Add a parenthetical qualifier to indicate level of study, if appropriate, for example, English 
languageBStudy and teaching (Continuing education). 
 
In accordance with H 1154, subdivide headings of the type [language or language 
group]BStudy and teaching by special topics, if appropriate, to bring out particular aspects of 
the study and teaching of these languages, for example, Spanish languageBStudy and 
teachingBForeign speakers. 
 
 
 
 
7.  Sacred works.  Assign the free-floating subdivision BStudy and teaching (May Subd Geog) 
under uniform titles for sacred works to works on how to study and teach these sacred works, 
including their individual parts, for example, Book of MormonBStudy and teaching. 
 
Note: Prior to 1991, the subdivision BStudy was used under individual sacred works.  
That subdivision is now obsolete. 
 
 
 
 
8.  Music.  The free-floating subdivision BInstruction and study (May Subd Geog) is used 
instead of BStudy and teaching in the field of music.  For guidelines on assigning this 
subdivision, see H 1160 and H 1161.

9.  Audiovisual 
materials.  Assign 
the 
free-floating 
subdivision 
BStudy 
and 
teachingBAudio-visual aids under topics to works on aids to the learning and teaching of a 
subject. 
 
To works on audiovisual materials about a topic that are not specifically designed for educational 
purposes, assign the subdivision BAudio-visual aids directly under the topic. 
 
For guidelines on assigning headings to catalogs of audiovisual materials, see H 1361, sec. 3. 
 
 
 
10.  Simulation 
methods.  Assign 
the 
free-floating 
subdivision 
BStudy 
and 
teach-
ingBSimulation methods under topics to works on the use of simulation methods for instruction 
in a specific field. 
 
To works on the use of a particular simulation method, assign an additional heading for the 
method, for example, Simulation games in education. 
 
 
 
11.  Activity programs.  Assign the free-floating subdivision BStudy and teachingBActivity 
programs (May Subd Geog) under topics to works on activities to be engaged in for the purpose 
of acquiring certain skills, concepts, or knowledge. 
 
 
 
12.  Textbooks.  Do not assign headings of the type [topic]BStudy and teaching to textbooks for 
students.  For guidelines on assigning headings to textbooks, see H 2187.
