A. Background  
This instruction sheet provides Library of Congress catalogers with policy guidelines for 
assigning subject headings to serials.  
LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead 
assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. 
*Textbooks*. To replace a form subdivision that includes a demographic group, also assign 
Library of Congress Demographic Groups Terms (LCDGT) to bring out the audience of the 
resource being cataloged, e.g. *German speakers* or *Arabic, Students of*. 
For guidelines on electronic serials, see H 1580.5. For guidelines on indexes to periodicals, see H 
1670. For guidelines on newspapers on special topics and newspapers of ethnic groups in the 
United States, see H 1920. For classification of periodicals, see F 210 and F 220. 
B. Recent revisions  
2026-01: Updated template. Added instructions related to LCGFT implementation. 
C. General rule 
Assign a full set of subject headings in accordance with normal guidelines. Choose headings that 
characterize the scope and intent of the serial as a whole, not headings that would be 
appropriate only for the content of the specific issue(s) at hand. 
D. Serials linked to previously cataloged serials 
Assign appropriate and valid subject headings without regard to the headings assigned to 
previously cataloged serials that are linked to the new serial, even if the numbering continues

E. The subdivision --Periodicals 
Use the form subdivision *--Periodicals* according to the guidelines provided in H 1927. 
LC practice: Assign LCGFT to bring out the genre and/or form of the resource being cataloged, 
e.g., *Periodicals*.
