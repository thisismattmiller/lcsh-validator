B. Types of headings covered 
Names of individual bodies of water, including oceans, seas, bays, rivers, lakes, man-made 
lakes, reservoirs, estuaries, and canals. 
Examples: 
North Atlantic Ocean 
California, Gulf of (Mexico) 
Colorado River (Colo.-Mexico) 
Glacier Bay (Alaska) 
Suez Canal (Egypt) 
Superior, Lake 
Tug Fork 
Wolfgangsee (Austria) 
Potomac River Estuary

C. Types of headings not covered 
The category does not include headings for types of bodies of water, such as *Lakes*; *Saline 
waters*; *Sounds (Geomorphology)*. The category also does not include headings for national 
rivers and wild and scenic rivers, such as *New River Gorge National River (W. Va.)*; *Obed 
Wild and Scenic River (Wash.)*. 
D. Relation to H 1140 
Appropriate subdivisions on the list of subdivisions used under names of places, H 1140, may 
also be used under names of bodies of water. This list contains the additional subdivisions 
authorized for use only under individual bodies of water. 
E. Conflicts 
Any subdivision listed here can be used as a free-floating subdivision under any heading 
belonging to the category if it is appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. 
Subject authority records may exist for headings employing variant phrases or subdivisions 
equivalent to a subdivision on this list. 
F. LC practice 
If an exceptional variant form is to be retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-
floating subdivision form following the procedures in H 195 if the reference does not yet exist. 
Otherwise, submit a proposal to change the variant form along with all bibliographic records 
requiring correction following the procedures in H 193.

H. Regions based on bodies of water 
The term *Region* may be added to existing headings for names of bodies of water to form 
free-floating phrase headings (cf. H 800, sec. 10). If the existing heading has a parenthetical 
qualifier, add the term before the qualifier. 
Examples: 
Tahoe, Lake, Region (Calif. and Nev.) 
Danube River Region 
Penobscot Bay Region (Me.) 
For complete instructions on the construction and use of headings for regions based on 
geographic features, see H 760. 
I. Former free-floating terms 
I.1. --Delta 
Use of the free-floating subdivision --Delta under names of rivers was discontinued in 1991. For 
instructions on establishing deltas, see H 800, sec. 6. 
I.2. Estuary 
The use of “Estuary” as a free-floating term added to names of rivers was discontinued in 1991. 
For instructions on establishing estuaries, see H 800, sec. 7.  
I.3. Watershed 
The use of “Watershed” as a free-floating term added to names of rivers and lakes was 
discontinued in March 1993. For instructions on establishing watersheds, see H 800, sec. 9. 
I.4. Valley 
The use of “Valley” as a free-floating term added to names of river systems was discontinued in 
July 1995. For instructions on establishing valleys, see H 800, sec. 8.
