A. Background 
The form subdivision *--Guidebooks* is used as a free-floating form subdivision for handbooks 
for travelers, sightseers, etc., containing information about routes, facilities and 
accommodations, items of interest, etc., that is, "how to get there and what to see and do" 
information. The related form subdivision *--Tours* is used as a free-floating subdivision for 
works providing planned itineraries. For guidelines on the topical subdivision *--Description 
and travel*, see H 1530. 
LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead 
assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. 
*Textbooks*.  To replace a form subdivision that includes a demographic group, also assign 
Library of Congress Demographic Groups Terms (LCDGT) to bring out the audience of the 
resource being cataloged, e.g. *German speakers* or *Arabic, Students of*.

C. Definitions 
Guidebooks: resources for travelers, sightseers, etc., containing information about routes, 
facilities and accommodations, items of interest, etc., that is, "how to get there and what to see 
and do" information 
D. General rule 
Use *--Guidebooks* under appropriate subject headings for works containing information of 
interest to persons en route or in the field. 
Use the subdivision only under subjects that have a connotation of place within them, thus 
allowing for the possibility of travel and viewing. For example, the subdivision may not be used 
under a discipline such as *Economics* since it has no connotation of place. It may, however, 
be used under a heading such as *Geology* for field guides to particular places. 
Some works called "guides" may actually be representative of other forms of publications. If the 
"guide" is simply a textbook on a topic, no form subdivision is needed (see also H 2187). If it is a 
reference work, the form subdivision *--Handbooks, manuals, etc.* may be applicable (cf. H 
1646). If it is a work describing methods for gaining practical experience through first-hand 
observation and collection of data, the topical subdivision *--Fieldwork* may be appropriate. 
LC practice: For resources that contain information of interest to travelers to a place, assign 
subject headings for the topic, place, etc., that is the focus of the travel. Assign the LCGFT 
appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Guidebooks*. This 
instruction applies to sections E-I below. 
E. Types of authorized headings 
Use *--Guidebooks* under the following types of headings: 
• Place names, and named geographic entities such as parks, archaeological sites, etc. 
• Types of lands, land uses, geographic features, etc., such as gardens, resorts, refuges, 
trails, rivers, etc. 
• Structures, buildings, institutions, including both named entities and types of the same 
• Types of architecture 
• Activities involving travel in the field, for example, agriculture, backpacking, bird 
watching, geology, hiking, mountaineering, viticulture, fishing, etc. 
• Art and antiquities, including specific art genres 
• Classes of persons and ethnic groups followed by the subdivision *--Travel*

H. Topical headings 
When using *--Guidebooks* under locally subdivided topical headings, make an additional 
entry, also subdivided by *--Guidebooks*, either under the corresponding place name, or 
under the entity, such as a park, used as the local subdivision. 
LC practice: For resources that contain information of interest to travelers related to a specific 
topic, assign subject headings for both the *[topic]--Place* and the *[place]*. If there is a 
specific focus on the type of travel or tourism, also assign a subject heading for that type of 
travel or tourism. Assign the LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in 
hand, e.g. LCGFT *Guidebooks*. 
Examples: 
650 #0 $a Cemeteries $z New England $v Guidebooks. 
651 #0 $a New England $v Guidebooks. 
LC practice: 
650 #0 $a Cemeteries $z New England. 
651 #0 $a New England.

I. Tours 
Use the free-floating form subdivision *--Tours* after names of places and corporate bodies for 
works providing planned itineraries for travelers in those places or visitors to those 
organizations. 
LC practice: Assign subject headings for names of places and corporate bodies for works 
providing planned itineraries for travelers in those places or visitors to those organizations. Also 
assign the LCGFT term appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, i.e. LCGFT 
*Guidebooks*.
