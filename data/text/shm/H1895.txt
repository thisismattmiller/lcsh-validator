Miniature Books  H 1895 
 
 
 
Subject Headings Manual 
H 1895 Page 1 
 
June 2013 
BACKGROUND:  In 1985 the topical heading Miniature books replaced the obsolete heading 
BibliographyBMicroscopic and miniature editions; the form heading Miniature booksB 
Specimens, assigned to actual specimens of such books, replaced BibliographyBMiniature and 
microscopic editionsBSpecimens.  This instruction sheet presents guidelines on the use of the 
topical subject heading Miniature books and the form heading Miniature booksBSpecimens.   
 
 
1.  Scope of the headings.  The topical heading Miniature books (May Subd Geog) is used 
for works that discuss books 10 centimeters (i.e. 100 millimeters) or less in both height and 
width. 
 
The form heading Miniature booksBSpecimens is used for actual specimens of such books.  
This form heading is assigned without geographic subdivision. 
 
 
2.  Assignment of the form heading.  In addition to the appropriate topical or form headings, 
assign to a miniature book the form heading Miniature booksBSpecimens.
