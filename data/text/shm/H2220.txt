A. Background 
The heading *Translations* and the subdivisions *--Translations* and *--Translations into 
[name of language]* are assigned to collections of translations or to works about translations. 
Use of the subdivisions *Translations from [name of language]*, *Translations from foreign 
languages*, *Translations into foreign languages*, and *Translations, [name of language]* 
was discontinued in 1991. For specific instructions on translations of the Bible and other sacred 
works, see H 1300. For works on the technique of translating, see H 2219. 
LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead 
assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. 
*Textbooks*. To replace a form subdivision that includes a demographic group, also assign 
Library of Congress Demographic Groups Terms (LCDGT) to bring out the audience of the 
resource being cataloged, e.g. *German speakers* or *Arabic, Students of*.

C. Definitions 
Translation: an act, process, or instance of translating, such as a rendering from one language 
into another0F1; in general, translation applies to written texts and interpretation applies to oral 
communication 
D. Literary translations 
D.1. Literary form headings 
Assign the free-floating subdivisions *Translations* and *Translations into [name of 
language]* under literary form headings for collections of translated works and translations of 
individual works in a specific form that have been assigned a literary form heading. For works 
translated into one language assign the subdivision *--Translations into [name of language]*, 
for example, *--Translations into French*. For works translated into several languages assign 
the subdivision *--Translations*. For works about translations assign the further subdivision *--
History and criticism* or other subdivision as appropriate. 
LC practice: Assign subject headings to the topical focus of the work, if applicable. Also assign 
one to bring out ethnicity and/or nationality, if appropriate. Bring out the language of a 
translation through other elements of descriptive cataloging. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the 
form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Translations* and *Poetry*. 
Example: 
Title: Contemporary Portuguese poetry : an anthology in English. 
650 #0 $a Portuguese poetry $y 20th century $v Translations into English.

650 #0 $a Portuguese poetry $y 20th century.

D.2. Individual literary authors and works 
Assign the free-floating subdivisions *--Translations* and *--Translations into [name of 
language]* under names of individual literary authors or literary works entered under title for 
collections of translations. Do not assign these subdivisions under author-title subject headings. 
Do not assign these subdivisions under authors or uniform titles for single translations of one 
work. For works about translations assign the further subdivision *--History and criticism* or 
other subdivision as appropriate. 
LC practice: Assign subject headings to the topical focus of the work, if applicable. Also assign 
one to bring out ethnicity and/or nationality, if appropriate. Bring out the language of a 
translation through other elements of descriptive cataloging. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the 
form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Translations* and *Poetry*. 
Example: 
Title: Catullus : the complete poems for American readers. 
600 10 $a Catullus, Gaius Valerius $v Translations into English.

E. Non-literary translations 
LC practice: Assign subject headings to the topical focus of the work, if applicable. Also assign 
one to bring out ethnicity and/or nationality, if appropriate. Bring out the language of a 
translation through other elements of descriptive cataloging. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the 
form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Translations* and *Poetry*. 
E.1. General works 
Assign the heading *Translations* to works on translations from several languages not limited 
to a subject or form. For works on translations from one language assign the heading *[name of 
language] imprints* with free-floating subdivision *--Translations* or *--Translations into 
[name of language]* and further subdivisions as appropriate. 
Example:

E.2. Specific subjects or forms 
Assign the free-floating subdivisions *--Translations* and *--Translations into [name of 
language]* under topical headings and under headings for types or forms of published 
materials for collections of translated works. For works translated into one language assign the 
subdivision *--Translations into [name of language]*, for example, *--Translations into 
French*. For works translated into several languages assign the subdivision *--Translations*. 
For works about translations, assign the further subdivision *--History and criticism* or other 
subdivision as appropriate. 
Note: Assign these subdivisions under topical headings only if the primary purpose of the work 
is to provide translated materials and this purpose is stated or implied by the title. Do not 
assign these subdivisions to works by one author or to translations of individual works 
(including individual periodicals). For special instructions on folklore genre headings and music 
headings see H 1627, sec. 3.a. and H 1917. 
Example: 
Title: English language equivalent editions of foreign language 
serials. 
650 #0 $a Periodicals $x Translations into English $v Bibliography. 
LC practice: 
650 #0 $a Periodicals $x Translations into English. 
655 #7 $a Bibliographies. $2 lcgft
