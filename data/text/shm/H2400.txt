Yearbooks  H 2400 
 
 
 
Subject Headings Manual 
H 2400 Page 1 
 
June 2013 
BACKGROUND:  Until 1986, the subdivision BYearbooks was used as a form subdivision 
under headings assigned to publications appearing annually, each volume of which summarizes 
the accomplishments or events of a particular year within a specific discipline or area of 
endeavor.  This subdivision was discontinued in 1986 in favor of the subdivision BPeriodicals. 
 
For classification of yearbooks, see F 230. 
 
 
 
1.  General rule.  Do not use the subdivision BYearbooks under headings assigned to any new 
work being cataloged, except as described in sec. 4, below.  To all serials issued indefinitely at 
regular or stated intervals, whether weekly, monthly, yearly, biennially, etc., assign headings 
with the free-floating form subdivision BPeriodicals.  For instructions on using the subdivision 
BPeriodicals, see H 1927. 
 
 
LC practice: 
2.  Correcting existing bibliographic records.  Do not make any systematic attempt to correct 
the subdivision on existing bibliographic records.  However, if the subject headings on an 
individual record are being revised or updated for other reasons, change BYearbooks to 
BPeriodicals whenever it is encountered. 
 
 
LC practice: 
3.  Serial continuations.  If a continuation of a serial is being cataloged, and earlier titles have 
been assigned headings with the subdivision BYearbooks, update MARC records of the earlier 
titles so that the headings on all titles match. 
 
 
4.  Student yearbooks.  Use the free-floating subdivision BStudentsBYearbooks under names of 
individual educational institutions for student yearbooks from those institutions, for example, 
Bowdoin CollegeBStudentsBYearbooks.
