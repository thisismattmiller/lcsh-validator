A. Background 
The form subdivision *--Controversial literature* is used under headings for individual 
religions, sects or denominations, under religious and monastic orders, and under sacred works, 
for works that argue against or express opposition toward those groups or works. The practice 
of using the subdivision under headings for general religious and philosophical topics was 
discontinued in 1998. The practice of using the subdivision for works that argue either for or 
against the topic and of further subdividing it by *--Pro* or *--Con* was discontinued in 1972. 
The form subdivision *--Apologetic works* is used under names of religions or denominations 
for works explaining the religion or denomination and defending it against criticism from the 
outside. This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning the subdivisions *--
Controversial literature* and *--Apologetic works*. 
LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead 
assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. 
*Apologetics writings*.

C. Definitions 
Controversial literature: works that argue against or express opposition to sacred works or 
religious organizations and their beliefs, especially those written by authors from outside the 
group being discussed.

Apologetic works: works explaining a religion, denomination, sect, etc., and defending it against 
criticism from the outside. 
D. Religions, denominations, etc. 
Use the free-floating form subdivision *--Controversial literature* under religions and their 
sects, Christian denominations, and monastic and religious orders for works that express 
opposition to these organizations and their beliefs, especially those written by authors from 
outside the group being discussed. Do not interpose another form or topical subdivision 
between the name of the religion and the subdivision *--Controversial literature*. 
Examples: 
650 #0 $a Buddhism $v Controversial literature. 
610 20 $a Catholic Church $v Controversial literature. 
610 20 $a Jesuits $v Controversial literature. 
For works explaining a religion or denomination and defending it against criticism from the 
outside, use the free-floating form subdivision *--Apologetic works* under the name of the 
religion or denomination. 
LC practice: Assign a subject heading with the appropriate topical subdivisions. Do not assign 
the form subdivisions *--Controversial literature* and *--Apologetic works*. Also assign LCGFT 
appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Apologetics writings 
*. 
Examples: 
Title: Searching for sense : the logic of Catholic belief. 
610 20 $a Catholic Church $v Apologetic works. 
LC practice: 
610 20 $a Catholic Church $x Doctrines. 
655 #7 $a Apologetic writings. $2 lcgft 
 
Title: A defense of Judaism versus proselytizing Christianity. 
650 #0 $a Christianity $v Controversial literature. 
650 #0 $a Judaism $v Apologetic works. 
LC practice: 
650 #0 $a Christianity. 
650 #0 $a Judaism. 
655 #7 $a Apologetic writings. $2 lcgft

E. Sacred works 
Use the free-floating form subdivision *--Controversial literature* under sacred works or their 
individual parts for works that express opposition to these writings, question their 
authoritativeness, dispute their divine inspiration, etc. Do not interpose another form or topical 
subdivision between the uniform title and the subdivision *--Controversial literature*. 
LC practice: Assign a subject heading with the appropriate topical subdivisions. Do not assign 
the form subdivisions *--Controversial literature* and *--Apologetic works*. Also assign LCGFT 
appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Religious 
materials*. 
Examples: 
630 00 $a Bible. $p Genesis $v Controversial literature. 
[not 
630 00 $a Bible. $p Genesis $x Inspiration $v Controversial 
literature.] 
LC practice: 
630 00 $a Bible. $p Genesis. 
655 #7 $a Religious materials. $2 lcgft 
 
630 00 $a Book of Mormon $v Controversial literature. 
LC practice: 
630 00 $a Book of Mormon. 
655 #7 $a Religious materials. $2 lcgft
