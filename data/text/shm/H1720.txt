A. Background 
Use of the subdivision *--Legends and stories* under types of animals was discontinued in 
1981. Several subdivisions are now used in place of the discontinued subdivision. 
LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead 
assign an appropriate term or terms from the Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), 
e.g. *Fiction*, *Folk literature*, *Legends*, etc. 
B. Recent revisions 
2026-02: Updated template. Added instructions related to LCGFT implementation. 
C. Fiction 
To one or more fictitious stories about an animal or a group of animals, assign the subdivision 
*--Fiction* under the type of animal (cf. H 1790) 
To collections of stories involving various types of animals, assign *Animals--Fiction*. 
LC practice: Do not use a form subdivision of the type *--Fiction*. Instead assign an appropriate 
term or terms from LCGFT, e.g. *Fiction*, *Animal fiction*, etc.

D. Folklore 
To one or more legends about an animal or group of animals, assign the subdivision *--
Folklore* under the type of animal (cf. H 1627). 
To collections of legends involving various types of animals, assign *Animals--Folklore*. 
LC practice: Do not use a form subdivision of the type *--Folklore*. Instead assign an 
appropriate term or terms from LCGFT, e.g. *Folk literature*, *Folk literature*, etc.  
Example: 
650 #0 $a Dogs $v Folklore. 
LC practice: 
650 #0 $a Dogs. 
655 #7 $a Folk tales. $2 lcgft 
E. True stories about individual animals and pets 
E.1. Collections 
To collections of true-life stories about animals, assign the subdivision *--Biography* under the 
type of animal. Interpose geographic subdivision if appropriate and assign additional headings 
as needed. 
LC practice: Do not use a form subdivision of the type *--Biography*. Instead, assign an 
appropriate term or terms from LCGFT, e.g. *Biographies*, etc. To replace a form subdivision 
that includes a demographic group, also assign LCDGT to bring out the audience of the resource 
being cataloged, e.g. *Children*, *Teenagers*, etc.  
Examples: 
Title: Great canine heroes of all time. 
650 #0 $a Dogs $v Biography. 
LC practice: 
650 #0 $a Dogs. 
655 #7 $a Biographies. $2 lcgft

E.2. Individual biographies 
For individual biographies, use the subdivision *--Biography* under types of animals, with 
geographic subdivision if appropriate. If the animal has a name that is established as a heading, 
or if the animal is well known enough for its name to be established, assign it as an additional 
heading. See H 1332, section 9, for guidelines on establishing headings for names of individual 
animals. Assign the owner’s name(s) if appropriate. 
LC practice: Do not use a form subdivision of the type *--Biography*. Instead, assign an 
appropriate term or terms from LCGFT, e.g. *Biographies*, etc. To replace a form subdivision 
that includes a demographic group, also assign LCDGT to bring out the audience of the resource 
being cataloged, e.g. *Children*, *Teenagers*, etc.  
Example: 
Title: Barry, the bravest Saint Bernard. 
600 00 $a Barry $c (Dog), $d 1800-1814 $v Juvenile literature. 
650 #0 $a Saint Bernard dog $z Switzerland $v Biography $v Juvenile 
literature. 
650 #0 $a Rescue dogs $z Switzerland $v Biography $v Juvenile literature.

600 00 $a Barry $c (Dog), $d 1800-1814. 
650 #0 $a Saint Bernard dog $z Switzerland. 
650 #0 $a Rescue dogs $z Switzerland.

E.3. Anecdotes about pets 
To anecdotal accounts of pets, assign the subdivision *--Anecdotes* under the type of animal, 
with local subdivision if appropriate. Assign the owner’s name(s) if appropriate. 
LC practice: Do not use a form subdivision of the type *--Anecdotes*. Instead assign an 
appropriate term or terms from LCGFT, e.g. *Anecdotes*, etc. 
Example: 
600 10 $a [name of owner] 
650 #0 $a Dogs $z California $v Anecdotes. 
LC practice: 
600 10 $a [name of owner] 
650 #0 $a Dogs $z California. 
655 #7 $a Anecdotes. $2 lcgft 
If the pet has a name that is established as a heading, or if the animal is well known enough for 
its name to be established, assign the name as an additional heading. 
LC practice: Do not use a form subdivision of the type *--Anecdotes*. Instead assign an 
appropriate term or terms from LCGFT, e.g. *Anecdotes*, etc.  
Example: 
600 10 $a Obama, Bo.

LC practice:
