Acronyms  H 1206.5

June 2013 
BACKGROUND:  An acronym is a word formed from the first, or first few, letters of a series of 
words.  This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning the heading Acronyms and the 
free-floating subdivision BAcronyms. 
 
 
 
 
 
1.  General.  Assign the heading Acronyms to general works about acronyms.  Assign the 
heading AcronymsBDictionaries to works that consist of alphabetical lists of acronyms in 
multiple languages, together with their meanings. 
 
 
 
 
2.  By language.   
 
 
 
a.  English.  Assign the heading Acronyms to works about English acronyms.  Assign 
the heading AcronymsBDictionaries to works that consist of alphabetical lists of 
acronyms in the English language. 
 
 
 
b.  Other languages.  Assign the heading [name of language or language group]B 
Acronyms to works about acronyms in that language or language group.  Assign the 
heading [name of language or language group]BAcronymsBDictionaries to works that 
consist of alphabetical lists of acronyms in that language or language group, together 
with their meanings. 
 
 
 
 
3.  By topic.  Assign the heading for the appropriate topic subdivided by the free-floating 
subdivision BAcronyms to works about acronyms pertaining to the topic.  For works that 
consist of alphabetical lists of acronyms pertaining to a topic, further subdivide 
[topic]BAcronyms by the free-floating subdivision BDictionaries.

3.  By topic.  (Continued) 
 
For works about acronyms in a language other than English, assign an additional heading for 
[language]BAcronyms.  For dictionaries of acronyms in a language other than English, further 
divide the heading [topic]BAcronymsBDictionaries by the name of the language and assign an 
additional heading [language]BAcronymsBDictionaries. 
 
Examples: 
 
Title:  Guide to federal government acronyms. 
650 #0 $a Administrative agencies $z United States  
          $x Acronyms. 
650 #0 $a Executive departments $z United States  
          $x Acronyms. 
 
Title:  Dictionary of medical and health  acronyms. 
650 #0 $a Medicine $x Acronyms $v Dictionaries. 
650 #0 $a Health $x Acronyms $v Dictionaries.
