BACKGROUND:  Subject headings designating individual industries based on products have, 
in the past, been formulated in several different ways: 
 
 
[product heading] industries  
[product heading] industry 
Group I 
[product heading] industries and trade  
[product heading] industry and trade  
[product heading] trade 
 
 
 
 
[product heading] manufacture and trade 
Group II 
[product heading]BIndustry and trade  
[product heading]BTrade and manufacture 
 
 
 
In order to simplify and standardize headings for industries based on products, new industry 
headings are now being established using only the formulation [product heading] industry. 
 
Existing industry headings in Group I continue to be valid.  Those in Group II are obsolete, and 
have, for the most part, been cancelled from the subject authority file. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
1.  General rule.  Use the industry heading for works that discuss the economic and 
manufacturing aspects connected with the production and trade of types of commodities or 
products.  Do not assign the industry heading to cover a discussion of the product itself. 
 
For works that discuss the process of moving goods from the producer through the trade 
channels to the consumer, use the subdivision BMarketing under types of commodities or 
products.  Do not use the subdivision BMarketing under industry headings.  For instructions 
on the use of the subdivision BMarketing, see H 1870.

2.  Establishing new headings.  Establish headings for industries based on products by adding 
the word industry to the heading for the specific product whenever possible.  Examples: 
 
 
 
    Product Heading     
 
 
    Industry Heading      
 
 
Macadamia nut 
 
 
 
Macadamia nut industry 
Pollution control equipment  
 
Pollution control equipment industry 
Annuals (Plants) 
 
 
 
Annuals (Plants) industry 
 
 
 
 
 
If the resulting heading is awkward and does not conform to common usage, make necessary 
adjustments, such as adding industry to the singular form of a product that is established in the 
plural form, or adding industry to the uninverted form of a product that is established in inverted 
form.  Examples: 
 
 
 
    Product Heading     
 
 
    Industry Heading      
  
 
Cigarettes 
 
 
 
 
Cigarette industry 
Cigars  
 
 
 
 
Cigar industry 
Household appliances, Electric 
 
Electric household appliances 
industry 
 
 
 
 
 
If the heading for the product is not in the subject authority file, establish the product heading at 
the same time.

4.  Reference patterns. 
 
a.  UF references.  Whenever possible, add the necessary 450 fields at the product 
heading level rather than at the industry level.  Example: 
 
 
150 ## $a Macadamia nut  
 
450 ## $a Queensland nut 
 
  [not 150 ## $a Macadamia nut industry 
450 ## $a Queensland nut industry] 
 
 
If the 450 field cannot be added to the product heading or if the term refers only to the 
industy, add it to the industry heading.  Example: 
 
 
150 ## $a Agricultural industries 
450 ## $a Agribusiness

4.  Reference patterns.  (Continued) 
 
 
b.  Broader terms.  If the industry heading at the next higher level of the hierarchy 
comes readily to mind and has been established, add it as a 550 (broader term) field for 
the new heading being established.  If the appropriate broader term has not been 
established, it may be established or the 550 field may be omitted.  Example: 
 
 
150 ## $a Macadamia nut industry 
550 ## $w g $a Nut industry 
 
 
 
c.  Related terms.   Link the heading for the product and the heading for the industry as 
related terms by means of 550 fields if the two headings do not begin with the same word 
or word stem.  Example: 
 
 
150 ## $a Electric household appliances industry 
550 ## $a Household appliances, Electric 
 
150 ## $a Household appliances, Electric 
550 ## $a Electric household appliances industry
