A. Background 
LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead 
assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. 
*Textbooks*. To replace a form subdivision that includes a demographic group, also assign 
Library of Congress Demographic Groups Terms (LCDGT) to bring out the audience of the 
resource being cataloged, e.g. *German speakers* or *Arabic, Students of*. 
For guidelines on works about art, see H 1250. For guidelines on works about illustrations, see 
H 1659. For guidelines on works about pictorial works, see H 1935. For guidelines on works on 
exhibitions, see H 1593. 
B. Recent revisions 
2026-07: Updated template. Added alternative instructions for LCGFT implementation.

C. Definitions 
Artistic photography: photography used for creative expression and treated as a fine art. 
D. General works on artistic photography 
Assign the heading *Photography, Artistic* to works that consist chiefly of photographs whose 
main purpose is photography as a fine art. Assign the heading also to general discussions of 
artistic photography. 
LC practice: Assign the subject heading *Photography, Artistic* for works that are chiefly 
collections of or reproductions of photographs whose main purpose is photography as a fine 
art. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT 
*Illustrated works*. 
Example: 
Title: Great unknowns : an exquisite collection of black and white 
photography. 
650 #0 $a Photography, Artistic. 
LC practice: 
650 #0 $a Photography, Artistic. 
655 #7 $a Illustrated works. $2 lcgft 
E. Artistic photography from a particular place 
Do not divide the heading *Photography, Artistic* by place. To express the geographic origin of 
the photographs, assign an additional subject heading *Photography--[place]*. 
Example: 
Title: Visual instincts : contemporary Australian photography. 
650 #0 $a Photography, Artistic. 
650 #0 $a Photography $z Australia. 
LC practice: 
650 #0 $a Photography, Artistic. 
650 #0 $a Photography $z Australia. 
655 #7 $a Illustrated works. $2 lcgft 
F. Artistic photography from a particular time period 
Assign the heading *Photography, Artistic*, subdivided by period subdivisions from the free-
floating list in H 1148. Do not interpose the subdivision *--History*.

G. Special types of photography 
For works on or photographic collections of special types of photography, assign the specific 
heading that best represents that type of photography, for example, *Landscape 
photography*; *Fashion photography*; *Photography, Humorous*. For portrait photography, 
see Sec. I, below. 
LC practice: Assign the subject heading for the type of artistic photography for works that are 
chiefly collections of or reproductions of photographs with a specific focus. Assign LCGFT 
appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT * Illustrated works *.

H. Photography of specific items, places, etc. 
Determine whether the focus of the piece is on artistic photography or on the actual items, 
places, etc. If the primary focus is artistic photography, assign a heading for photography of the 
topic, for example, *Photography of mountains; Dance photography*. Assign additional 
headings for the topic with the form subdivision *--Pictorial works*. For works that merely

I. Portrait photography 
For works on or general collections of portrait photographs, assign the heading *Portrait 
photography*. If a specific class of persons is represented, assign a heading for that class of 
persons subdivided by *--Portraits*, with *Portrait photography* as an additional heading. If 
there is an existing heading of the type *Photography of [class of persons]* assign that heading 
instead of *Portrait photography* and, if the visual identity of the individuals is emphasized, 
assign an additional heading for class of persons with subdivision *--Portraits*. For works that 
contain portraits primarily as visual representations of persons without emphasis on 
photography, see H 1935.

K. Exhibition catalogs 
For an exhibition catalog of artistic photographs, not limited to a particular theme or type of 
photography, assign the subject heading *Photography, Artistic--Exhibitions*. For an exhibition 
catalog of photographs on a particular theme or photography of a particular type, assign a 
heading for the theme or type, for example, *Photography of clouds--Exhibitions*; *Glamour 
photography--Exhibitions*. 
LC practice: For an exhibition catalog of artistic photographs, not limited to a particular theme 
or type of photography, assign the subject heading *Photography, Artistic*. For an exhibition 
catalog of photographs on a particular theme or photography of a particular type, assign a 
heading for the theme or type, for example, *Photography of clouds*. Also assign LCGFT 
appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Exhibition catalogs*. 
K.1. Works of one photographer 
If the exhibition consists of works by one photographer, assign an additional heading for the 
name of the photographer with subdivision *--Exhibitions*. 
LC practice: If the exhibition consists of works by one photographer, assign an additional 
heading for the name of the photographer. 
K.2. Works belongs to an institution or private collection 
If the photographs are a part of an institution's permanent collection, assign an additional 
heading for the institution with subdivision *--Exhibitions* in accordance with H 1593. If the 
photographs are identified as coming from an individual private collection, including a formerly 
private collection now belonging to a public institution, assign additional headings as specified 
in H 1427. 
LC practice: If the photographs are a part of an institution's permanent collection, assign an 
additional heading for the institution, in accordance with H 1593. If the photographs are 
identified as coming from an individual private collection, including a formerly private collection 
now belonging to a public institution, assign additional headings as specified in H 1427. 
Example: 
Title: To be and not to be : Centre d'Art Santa Mònica, Barcelona, 23 
d'abril del 1990.
