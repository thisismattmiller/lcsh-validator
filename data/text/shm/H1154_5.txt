PATTERN:  Labor laws and legislation 
 
 
TYPES OF HEADINGS COVERED BY THE PATTERN:  Headings for legal topics, for 
example, Torts; Civil procedure; Domestic relations; Airspace (International law); legal 
phrase headings, for example, Agricultural laws and legislation; Criminal law; Trade 
regulation; headings for legal systems, for example, Islamic law; International law; topical 
headings subdivided by the legal subdivisions –Law and legislation; –Legal status, laws, etc.; 
and –Safety regulations; place names subdivided by legal subdivisions, for example, –Foreign 
relations–Law and legislation. 
 
 
Note:  Establish editorially new uses of the subdivision –Law and legislation.  For 
guidelines on the usage of the subdivisions –Law and legislation; –Legal status, laws, 
etc.; and –Safety regulations under topical headings, see H 1705.   
 
 
CONFLICTS:  Any subdivision listed here can be used as a free-floating subdivision under any 
heading belonging to this category if it is appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject 
authority file.  Subject authority records may exist for headings employing variant phrases or 
subdivisions equivalent to subdivisions on this list. 
 
LC practice: 
If an exceptional variant form is to be retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent 
free-floating subdivision form following the procedures in H 195 if the reference does not yet 
exist.  Otherwise, submit a proposal to change the variant form along with all bibliographic 
records requiring correction following the procedures in H 193. 
 
Note:  Most form subdivisions coded $v in this list may also be used as topical 
subdivisions coded $x when assigned to works about the form (see H 1075, sec. 1.d.).
