Information Services  H 1675.5 
 
 
 
Subject Headings Manual 
H 1675.5 Page 1 
 
June 2013 
BACKGROUND:  Information centers are organizations that collect, organize, store, retrieve, 
and disseminate information and provide special services for their clients, such as literature 
searches, abstracting and indexing, compiling of bibliographies, selective dissemination of 
information, etc.  This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning headings to works on 
these centers and their services. 
 
 
 
 
1.  General.  Assign the heading Information services (May Subd Geog) to general works on 
information centers and the services they provide to their clients.  Example: 
 
 
 
Title:  Information services in Boston. 
650 #0 $a Information services $z Massachusetts $z Boston. 
 
 
 
 
2.  Special topics.   
 
 
a.  General.  Use the free-floating subdivision BInformation services under names of 
countries, cities, etc., names of individual corporate bodies, including individual 
legislative bodies and military services, names of individual persons, and topical 
headings, for works on information centers devoted to collecting and disseminating 
information about these subjects.  Examples: 
 
 
 
650 #0 $a Medicine $x Information services. 
651 #0 $a Middle East $x Information services. 
610 10 $a United States. $b Army $x Information services. 
610 10 $a United States. $b Congress $x Information  
            services. 
600 10 $a Jung, C. G. $q (Carl Gustav), $d 1875-1961  
            $x Information services.

b.  By place.  BInformation services was formerly further subdivided by place.  That 
practice was discontinued in 1990.  If the location of the information services is judged 
to be significant, bring it out by assigning Information servicesB[place] as an additional 
heading.  Example: 
 
Title:  Directory of Northern Canadian and Indian information centres in 
Hull. 
650 #0 $a Indians of North America $z Canada  
            $x Information services $v Directories. 
650 #0 $a Parks $z Canada $x Information services  
            $v Directories. 
651 #0 $a Canada, Northern $x Information services  
            $v Directories. 
650 #0 $a Information services $z Québec (Province)  
            $z Hull $v Directories.
