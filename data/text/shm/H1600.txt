A. Background 
A festschrift is a complimentary or memorial publication usually in the form of a collection of 
essays, addresses, or biographical, bibliographical, scientific, or other contributions, often 
embodying the results of research, issued in honor of a person, an institution, or a society, 
usually on the occasion of an anniversary celebration. This instruction sheet provides guidelines 
for assigning subject headings to festschriften. 
LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead 
assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. 
*Textbooks*. To replace a form subdivision that includes a demographic group, also assign 
Library of Congress Demographic Groups Terms (LCDGT) to bring out the audience of the 
resource being cataloged, e.g. *German speakers* or *Arabic, Students of*.

D. Topic 
Assign a heading or headings to cover the subject matter of the festschrift as a whole, normally 
without form subdivision. (The subdivision *--Addresses, essays, lectures*, formerly used for 
this type of material, was discontinued in 1986.) 
If the work is the result of a congress held in honor of the person, society, etc., use the form 
subdivision *--Congresses*. 
LC practice: Assign heading(s) for the topic(s) covered in the festschrift, following standard 
practices. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. 
LCGFT *Festschriften*. 
Examples: 
Title: Africa in the long run : festschrift in the honour of Professor 
Arvi Hurskainen. 
600 10 $a Hurskainen, Arvi.  
650 #0 $a Ethnology $z Africa  
650 #0 $a Anthropological linguistics $z Africa.  
650 #0 $a African languages $x Research  
651 #0 $a Africa $x Social life and customs. 
LC practice: 
No change of subject headings. 
655 #7 $a Festschriften. $2 lcgft 
E. Honoree(s) 
Assign a heading for the name of the person or corporate body in whose honor the publication 
is issued only if more than 20% of the work is about the person or corporate body. 
If the work contains a bibliography relating to the honoree which is more than 20% of the work, 
assign the name of the honoree with the subdivision *--Bibliography*. 
LC practice: If the work contains a bibliography relating to the honoree which is more than 20% 
of the work, assign the name of the honoree as a subject heading. Assign LCGFT appropriate to 
the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Bibliographies*.
