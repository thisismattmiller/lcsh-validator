Subject Headings Manual 
H 2055 Page 1

1.  General rule.  Use the free-floating subdivision BSocial conditions under names of regions, 
countries, cities, etc.; under names of ethnic groups; and under classes of persons, including 
occupational groups.  Use the subdivision for works discussing the social history or sociology 
of a place, ethnic group, or class of persons, including such subtopics of sociology as social 
problems, stability, change, interaction, adjustment, structure, social institutions, etc.  For the 
sociological conditions within institutions, use the subdivision BSociological aspects under 
headings for types of institutions.  Examples: 
 
651 #0 $a Europe $x Social conditions. 
651 #0 $a China $x Social conditions. 
651 #0 $a Buffalo (N.Y.) $x Social conditions. 
650 #0 $a African Americans $z Rhode Island $x Social conditions. 
650 #0 $a Indians of North America $x Social conditions. 
650 #0 $a Older people $z Iowa $x Social conditions. 
650 #0 $a Carpenters $z Ohio $z Canton $x Social conditions. 
 
 
 
2.  Sociological topics in relation to specific places.  When further subdividing a sociological 
topic or problem by place, also assign, if appropriate, an additional heading for [place]BSocial 
conditions.  Example: 
 
Title:  Social mobility in Montgomery County, Maryland. 
650 #0 $a Social mobility $z Maryland $z Montgomery County. 
651 #0 $a Montgomery County (Md.) $x Social conditions. 
 
 
 
3.  BSocioeconomic status.  The subdivision BSocioeconomic status under headings for 
special occupational groups is no longer used.  Instead, use the free-floating subdivision(s) 
BSocial conditions and/or BEconomic conditions, as appropriate. 
 
 
 
4.  Further form or topical subdivisions.  BSocial conditions may be further subdivided by 
free-floating form or topical subdivisions appropriate for the work cataloged.  Examples: 
 
651 #0 $a Colombia $x Social conditions $x Mathematical models. 
650 #0 $a Women $x Social conditions $v Statistics. 
 
Do not further subdivide BSocial conditions by BHistory.

5.  Period subdivisions.  Further subdivide headings of the type [place]BSocial conditions by 
established chronological subdivisions or by the free-floating century subdivisions listed under 
BSocial conditions in H 1140:  B16th, [17th, 18th, 19th, 20th, or 21st] century.  Assign 
century subdivisions only when they do not conflict with established subdivisions for specific 
time periods.  Establish new subdivisions for other significant periods under headings of the 
type [place]BSocial conditions where there is sufficient material to warrant it.  Examples: 
 
651 #0 $a Europe $x Social conditions $y 1789-1900. 
651 #0 $a Great Britain $x Social conditions $y 20th century. 
651 #0 $a United States $x Social conditions $y 1960-1980. 
651 #0 $a Chicago (Ill.) $x Social conditions $y 19th century. 
 
When appropriate, further subdivide [place]BSocial conditionsB[period subdivision] by 
free-floating form or topical subdivisions that are used after BSocial conditions.  Examples: 
 
651 #0 $a Europe $x Social conditions $y 20th century  
            $v Periodicals. 
651 #0 $a China $x Social conditions $y 1949-1976 $v Statistics. 
651 #0 $a India $x Social conditions $y 1947- $x Mathematical 
            models. 
 
When appropriate, further subdivide headings of the type [class of persons]BSocial conditions 
and [ethnic group]BSocial conditions by the free-floating century subdivisions listed under 
BSocial conditions in H 1100 and H 1103: B16th, [17th, 18th, 19th, 20th, or 21st] century. 
 
6.  Examples of assignment of headings. 
 
Title:  The social history of the United States. 
651 #0 $a United States $x Social conditions. 
 
Title:  Carpenters in California : general social conditions. 
650 #0 $a Carpenters $z California $x Social conditions. 
 
Title:  The socioeconomic status of librarians. 
650 #0 $a Librarians $x Economic conditions.   
650 #0 $a Librarians $x Social conditions. 
 
Title:  Factors contributing to the high divorce rate among District of Columbia 
police. 
650 #0 $a Divorce $z Washington (D.C.)  
650 #0 $a Police $z Washington (D.C.) $x Social conditions. 
651 #0 $a Washington (D.C.) $x Social conditions.
