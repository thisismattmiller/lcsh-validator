BACKGROUND:  Until 1973 subject headings for research in various fields were established 
in the form [topic]BResearch (May Subd Geog) or as phrase headings such as Historical 
research or Medical research.  In 1973 the subdivision BResearch (May Subd Geog) was 
established as a free-floating subdivision and phrase headings were later converted to 
[topic]BResearch wherever possible. 
 
For instructions on the use of the free-floating subdivision BLegal research, see H 1710. 
 
 
 
1.  The subject heading Research.  Assign the heading to general works that discuss 
comprehensively all aspects of research, such as proposals, finance, goals, management, 
personnel, special projects, etc., in all fields, and to works on general scientific research.  Do 
not assign the heading to works that discuss the results of research.  For works that are limited 
to a specific aspect of research assign the heading Research with the appropriate topical 
subdivision, such as BFinance, BManagement, or BMethodology. 
 
 
2.  The free-floating subdivision BResearch.  Assign BResearch (May Subd Geog) as a 
free-floating subdivision under names of places and corporate bodies, classes of persons, ethnic 
groups, and topical headings, for general works that discuss comprehensively all aspects of 
research, such as proposals, finance, goals, etc., as applied to the topic.  Do not assign the 
subdivision to works that discuss the results of research in a particular field.  For works that are 
limited to a specific aspect of research assign the subdivision BResearch further subdivided by 
the appropriate topical subdivision, such as BFinance, BManagement, or BMethodology. 
 
 
3.  Conflict.  Before assigning the subdivision, determine whether the use under consideration 
conflicts with a previously established heading in the subject authority file.  For example, the 
heading ScienceBResearch may not be assigned because it appears as a UF under the heading 
Research. 
 
 
4.  Phrase headings for research.  Establish a phrase heading for research only to designate a 
type of research or when it is not possible to construct a heading of the type [topic]B Research.  
Examples: 
 
150 ## $a Action research 
150 ## $a Single subject research

5.  Special aspects of research.  For works that are limited to a special aspect of research, 
assign the appropriate heading for the topic.  Examples: 
 
650 #0 $a Agriculture $x Research $z United States $x Finance. 
650 #0 $a Education $x Research $x Management. 
650 #0 $a History $x Methodology. 
650 #0 $a Medicine $x Research grants $z United States.
