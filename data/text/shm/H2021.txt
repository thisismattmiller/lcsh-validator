BACKGROUND:  The subdivisions BReviews and BBook reviews are used as form 
subdivisions for collections of explanatory or critical accounts of artistic productions or 
performances, such as books, plays, concerts, etc.  This instruction sheet provides guidelines 
for assigning these subdivisions.  
 
 
 
1.  Books. 
 
a.  BooksBReviews.  Use the free-floating subdivision BReviews under the heading 
Books for collections of descriptive and evaluative accounts of books on many subjects.  
Example: 
 
Title:  Book review digest. 
650 #0 $a Books $v Reviews $v Periodicals. 
 
Assign this heading only to collections too broad to be assigned a more specific heading. 
 
Note:  For works about the technique of writing book reviews, assign the topical 
heading Book reviewing (May Subd Geog). 
 
Also use the free-floating subdivision BReviews under headings for types of books, for 
example, Reference booksBReviews. 
 
 
 
b.  [topic]BBook reviews.  Assign the free-floating subdivision BBook reviews under 
topical headings and names of places for collections of appraisals of books on these 
topics.  Examples: 
 
 
650 #0 $a Art $v Book reviews. 
651 #0 $a California $v Book reviews. 
650 #0 $a English literature $y l9th century $x History and 
            criticism $v Book reviews. 
650 #0 $a French imprints $v Book reviews. 
650 #0 $a Medicine $v Book reviews. 
651 #0 $a United States $x History $y Civil War, 1861-1865 
            $v Book reviews.

2.  Newspapers.  Assign the heading NewspapersBSections, columns, etc.BReviews and 
headings of the type [...] newspapersBSections, columns, etc.BReviews for works on the 
writing and editing of reviews of books, music, theater, etc., and for collections of these writings. 
 Example: 
 
650 #0 $a Canadian newspapers $x Sections, columns, etc.  
          $x Reviews. 
 
 
3.  Non-book materials.  Assign the free-floating subdivision BReviews under headings for 
non-book materials for collections of descriptive and evaluative accounts of these materials.  
Examples: 
 
650 #0 $a Compact discs $v Reviews. 
650 #0 $a Audiotapes $v Reviews. 
 
 
4.  Mass media and performing arts.  Assign the free-floating subdivision BReviews under 
headings for mass media and the performing arts for collections of critical writings about 
programs, performances, etc.  Examples: 
 
650 #0 $a Performing arts $z England $z London $v Reviews. 
650 #0 $a Television programs $z United States $v Reviews. 
650 #0 $a Theater $v Reviews.
