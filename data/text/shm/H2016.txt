Religious Life and Customs  H 2016 
 
 
 
Subject Headings Manual 
H 2016 Page 1 
 
June 2013 
BACKGROUND:  The subdivision BReligious life and customs is used under names of 
countries, cities, etc., for works on the religious customs, practices, etc., of these places.  This 
instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning this subdivision. 
 
 
 
1.  General.  Assign the free-floating subdivision BReligious life and customs under names of 
countries, cities, etc.  Examples: 
 
651 #0 $a Asia $x Religious life and customs. 
651 #0 $a Brazil $x Religious life and customs. 
651 #0 $a California $x Religious life and customs. 
651 #0 $a New York (N.Y.) $x Religious life and customs. 
 
 
 
2.  By period.  Do not use the subdivision BHistory after BReligious life and customs (see 
H 1647).  If period subdivisions have been established and are appropriate for the work, assign 
headings of the type [place]BReligious life and customsB[period subdivision] to works on the 
history of the religious customs, etc., in a place during a particular period.  Example: 
 
651 #0 $a Germany $x Religious life and customs $y Middle Ages, 
            843-1517. 
 
Establish new period subdivisions under headings of the type [place]BReligious life and 
customs as needed, except if the place in question is a city or town. 
 
 
 
3.  By religion or denomination.  Assign headings of the type [religion or denomination]B 
Customs and practices and [place]BReligious life and customs to works on the customs and 
practices of an individual religion or denomination in a particular place.  Example: 
 
Title:  Popular expressions of Catholicism in Mexico today. 
610 20 $a Catholic Church $z Mexico $x Customs and  
            practices. 
651 #0 $a Mexico $x Religious life and customs.
