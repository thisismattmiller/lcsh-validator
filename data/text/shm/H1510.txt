A. Background 
Cross-cultural studies are studies in which comparable data on cultural traits or behavior from 
two or more cultures or societies are presented. These studies may assess similarities or 
diversities occurring in those cultures or test hypotheses concerning individual or group 
behavior. This instruction sheet provides guidelines on the usage of the free-floating 
subdivision *–Cross-cultural studies*. 
LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. For 
resources that would use form subdivisions, assign the LCGFT term appropriate to the form 
and/or genre of the resource in hand. 
B. Recent revisions 
2025-02: Updated template. Added alternative instructions for LCGFT implementation. 
C. Definitions 
Cross-cultural studies: works that report the results of cross-cultural studies or discuss the 
methods and techniques of conducting such studies.

D. General rule 
Use the free-floating subdivision *–Cross-cultural studies* as a form subdivision under topical 
headings for works that report the results of cross-cultural studies on those topics or as a 
topical subdivision for works that discuss the methods and techniques of conducting such 
studies. 
LC practice: Assign the appropriate subject headings for the topical focus of the resource. Do 
not use the free-floating subdivision *–Cross-cultural studies* as a form subdivision for works 
that report the results of cross-cultural studies. Instead, assign the LCGFT term appropriate to 
the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Cross-cultural studies*. 
Example: 
Title: Suicide in different cultures.

E. Restrictions on usage 
E.1. General 
Reserve use of the subdivision *–Cross-cultural studies* to designate works that compare 
cultural traits or culturally modifiable phenomena. 
Topics such as physiological processes may be compared, but the topic in question should be 
one that is modifiable by the social environment or through the socialization process. 
LC practice: Assign the appropriate subject headings for the topical focus of the resource. Also 
assign the LCGFT term appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT 
*Cross-cultural studies*. 
Examples:

E.2. Ethnic groups 
Do not use the subdivision *–Cross-cultural studies* under names of individual ethnic groups 
or for works that discuss a single ethnic group. The work must compare traits in at least two or 
more cultures or societies in order to designate it a cross-cultural study.
