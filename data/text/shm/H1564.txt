BACKGROUND:  The heading Discoveries in geography and the free-floating subdivision 
BDiscovery and exploration under places are used for works on geographical exploration of 
places that are unsettled or sparsely settled and are unknown to the rest of the world.  This 
instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning the heading Discoveries in geography and 
the free-floating subdivision BDiscovery and exploration.  It also covers use of the heading 
Outer spaceBExploration for space exploration in general and use of the subdivision 
BExploration under headings for celestial bodies. 
 
                                        
                              
 
 
1.  History of exploration.   
 
a.  General.  Assign the heading Discoveries in geography to general works on the 
history of discovery and exploration.  Treat this heading as inherently historical and do 
not subdivide it by BHistory. 
 
 
b.  By country.  For works on the history of discovery and exploration conducted by one 
country, subdivide the heading Discoveries in geography by the nationality carrying out 
the exploration.  Examples: 
 
650 #0 $a Discoveries in geography $x American. 
650 #0 $a Discoveries in geography $x Spanish. 
 
The subdivision BExploring expeditions, formerly used under names of places for works 
on expeditions sent from those places for purposes of exploration, was discontinued in 
1993. 
 
 
 
2.  Exploration of a particular place.   
 
a.  General.  Use the free-floating subdivision BDiscovery and exploration under 
names of places that were unsettled or sparsely settled and largely unknown to the rest of 
the world at the time of exploration for historical works on the exploration of those 
places or first person accounts of explorers.  In accordance with H 1647, do not 
subdivide further by BHistory.

2.  Exploration of a particular place.  (Continued) 
 
b.  The New World. 
 
(1) General.  Assign the heading AmericaBDiscovery and exploration to 
works on the discovery and exploration of the New World by Columbus and other 
early explorers.  
 
For first person accounts, assign as an additional heading AmericaBEarly 
accounts to 1600. 
 
(2) Special.  For works on later explorations in individual regions, countries, 
states, etc., of the Western Hemisphere, assign a heading of the type 
[place]BDiscovery and exploration. 
 
The former practice of prohibiting the use of the subdivision BDiscovery and 
exploration under the United States and Latin America and under places smaller 
than countries was discontinued in 1987. 
 
c.  By country.  For works on the exploration of a place by explorers from a particular 
foreign country, assign a heading of the type [place]BDiscovery and exploration 
subdivided further by the nationality of the explorers or country supporting the 
exploration.  Examples: 
 
651 #0 $a America $x Discovery and exploration $x Spanish. 
 
 
3.  Space exploration.  Use the heading Outer spaceBExploration for the topic of exploration 
of space in general, through either manned space vehicles or unmanned probes.  For the 
exploration of individual and types of objects in space, assign headings of the type 
MoonBExploration; 
Inner 
planetsBExploration; 
Jupiter 
(Planet)BExploration; 
etc.  
Establish new headings as needed.  Do not use the subdivision BDiscovery and exploration 
under names of individual celestial bodies.    
 
4.  Assigning additional headings.  Works on discovery and exploration are usually 
multifaceted, requiring an array of headings to bring out various aspects of the material.  Assign 
several headings, if appropriate, to cover all important topics discussed in the work, such as the 
place explored, the persons or country conducting the exploration, special aspects of the 
exploration, etc.

5.  Examples. 
 
Title:  Scientific expeditions in the Portuguese overseas territories. 
650 #0 $a Scientific expeditions $z Portugal $x History. 
651 #0 $a Africa $x Discovery and exploration $x Portuguese. 
 
 
 
Title:  World exploration by the Royal Geographic Society. 
610 20 $a Royal Geographical Society (Great Britain)  
            $x History. 
650 #0 $a Discoveries in geography $x British. 
 
 
 
Title:  America's founding years, 1492-1815. 
651 #0 $a United States $x History $y Colonial period, ca. 
            1600-1775. 
651 #0 $a United States $x History $y Revolution, 1775-1783. 
651 #0 $a United States $x History $y 1783-1815. 
651 #0 $a America $x Discovery and exploration. 
 
 
 
Title:  Westward vision : the story of the Oregon Trail. 
651 #0 $a Oregon Trail. 
650 #0 $a Overland journeys to the Pacific. 
651 #0 $a West (U.S.) $x Discovery and exploration. 
 
 
 
Title:  Spanish exploration of the Pacific Northwest. 
651 #0 $a Northwest, Pacific $x Discovery and exploration  
            $x Spanish. 
650 #0 $a Explorers $z Northwest, Pacific $x History. 
650 #0 $a Explorers $z Spain $x History. 
 
 
 
Title:  The Portuguese explorers. 
650 #0 $a Discoveries in geography $x Portuguese. 
650 #0 $a Explorers $z Portugal. 
650 #0 $a Voyages and travels. 
651 #0 $a Portugal $x History $y Period of discoveries, 
            1385-1580.
