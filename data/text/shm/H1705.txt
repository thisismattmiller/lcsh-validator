BACKGROUND:  The subdivisions BLaw and legislation; BLegal status, laws, etc.; and 
BSafety regulations are the authorized subdivisions used to designate the legal aspect of topics.  
This instruction sheet provides guidelines for the usage of these legal subdivisions.  Any 
subdivision provided under the pattern heading Labor laws and legislation may be used as a 
further subdivision under topical headings which use these legal subdivisions, in accordance 
with H 1154.5. 
 
 
 
 
1.  BLaw and legislation (May Subd Geog).  Use this subdivision under topical headings for 
works about the legal aspects of the topic, and/or works that contain the text of laws on the topic. 
 Code the subdivision $x.  Establish new uses of this subdivision editorially, and provide the 
appropriate reference structure. 
 
Note:  Where BLaw and legislation has been established under a pattern heading, for 
example, CancerBLaw and legislation, the subdivision is free-floating under headings 
governed by that pattern. 
 
Do not use BLaw and legislation if: 
 
$ the topical heading itself is inherently legal, for example, Torts; Civil 
procedure; Domestic relations; Spouse’s share; Adjoining landowners. 
 
Note:  Until 2014, BLaw and legislation was not used under headings for 
crimes and groups of crimes (e.g., Murder; Computer crimes).  Since 
works about crimes often discuss their economic, psychological, etc., 
aspects, and it is useful to explicitly identify works that are about their 
legal aspects, BLaw and legislation may now be used under headings for 
crimes and groups of crimes. Each use of the subdivision must be 
editorially established. 
 
$ a phrase heading exists for the topic, for example, Agricultural laws and 
legislation; Criminal law; Trade regulation. 
 
$ if the topical heading represents a group of people.  In such cases, use the 
subdivision BLegal status, laws, etc.

2.  BLegal status, laws, etc. (May Subd Geog).  Use this free-floating subdivision under classes 
of persons and ethnic groups, for example, Children; Auditors; Japanese Americans. 
 
 
3.  BSafety regulations (May Subd Geog).  Use this free-floating subdivision under types of 
objects, chemicals, materials, machines, installations, industries, or activities, or under names of 
disciplines, for safety rules or orders which have the force of law, for example, Agricultural 
machinery; Coal mines and mining; Radiation.
