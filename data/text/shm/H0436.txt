Provisional Name Headings  H 436 
 
 
 
Subject Headings Manual 
H 436 Page 1 
 
June 2013 
BACKGROUND:  When a descriptive cataloger determines that a name heading cannot be 
satisfactorily established according to descriptive rules because of inadequate information about 
the person, corporate body, or uniform title the heading represents, a "provisional" name 
heading is established, coded c in field 008/33 (the Level of Establishment box on the 008 input 
screen in authority records in the LC ILS).  Name headings coded Aprovisional@ may be assigned 
as subject headings without further action. 
 
 
 
If a name heading coded Aprovisional@ is needed for assignment as a subject heading, assign the 
heading as established, or, if the work being cataloged contains additional information that will 
enable the heading to be fully rather than provisionally established, consider revising the heading 
or requesting a descriptive cataloger to do so.
