Description and Travel  H 1530 
 
 
 
Subject Headings Manual 
H 1530 Page 1 
 
June 2013 
BACKGROUND:  Formerly the subdivision BDescription and travel was used under names of 
places larger than cities, and under headings for metropolitan areas.  The subdivision 
BDescription was used under names of cities (except ancient cities) and names of individual 
universities and colleges.  In 1991, the subdivision BDescription was cancelled in favor of 
using the subdivision BDescription and travel under names of both cities and larger places for 
descriptive works and accounts of travel, including the history of travel, in those places.  This 
subdivision is not used for broad geographical works about a specific place.  The subdivision 
BGeography is used under names of places for works of that type.  This instruction sheet 
provides guidelines for assigning the subdivision BDescription and travel. 
 
For the use of the subdivisions BGuidebooks and BTours, see H 1645; for BPictorial works, see 
H 1935; for BAerial photographs and BAerial views, see H 1210.5.   
 
For works on discovery and exploration, see H 1564. 
 
 
 
 
1.  General rule.  Use the freeBfloating subdivision BDescription and travel under names of 
places  for descriptive works or accounts of travel, including the history of travel, in those 
places.  Examples: 
 
 
 
Title:  Across China. 
651 #0 $a China $x Description and travel. 
600 10 $a Jenkins, Peter, $d 1951- $x Travel $z China. 
 
 
Title:  Boston ways : high, by, and folk. 
651 #0 $a Boston (Mass.) $x History. 
651 #0 $a Boston (Mass.) $x Description and travel. 
 
 
 
Do not use the subdivision BDescription and travel under names of individual parks, Indian 
reservations, universities and colleges, or extinct cities and towns (pre-1500).  For descriptive 
works or accounts of travel in these places, assign the heading for the entity without further 
subdivision.

2.  Period subdivisions.  Do not establish period subdivisions after BDescription and travel.  
For works written or issued before 1800, use the free-floating form subdivision BEarly works to 
1800 after BDescription and travel.  Example: 
 
 
Title:  Travels in the colonies in 1773-1775 : described in the letters of 
William Mylne. 
651 #0 $a United States $x Description and travel  
            $v Early works to 1800. 
600 10 $a Mylne, William $v Correspondence. 
 
 
In situations where the time period covered in the work is judged to be significant, it may be 
brought out by assigning an additional heading, such as [place]BHistoryB[period subdivision].  
This is optional and should not be regarded as a requirement.  Example: 
 
 
Title:  Once upon a city : New York from 1870 to 1890. 
651 #0 $a New York (N.Y.) $x Description and travel. 
651 #0 $a New York (N.Y.) $x History $y 1865-1898. 
651 #0 $a New York (N.Y.) $x Social life and customs $y 19th 
            century.
