Dissertations and Theses  H 1570 
 
 
 
Subject Headings Manual 
H 1570 Page 1 
 
June 2013 
BACKGROUND:  Bibliographies of dissertations and theses may be general in nature, or 
limited to a particular subject.  They may also list those items accepted by a particular 
institution or the institutions collectively of a particular geographic area.  For classification of 
bibliographies and catalogs of dissertations and theses, see F 585. 
 
 
 
 
 
1.  General rule.  Assign to a bibliography of dissertations and/or theses the appropriate 
combination of the following subject headings: 
 
 
6XX X0 $a [subject or name heading] $v Bibliography. 
[Omit this heading if the work is not limited to a specific topic.] 
 
610 X0 $a [institution] $x Dissertations $v Bibliography. 
[Omit this heading if the work is not limited to a specific institution.] 
 
650 #0 $a Dissertations, Academic $z [country] $v Bibliography. 
 
 
 
 
2.  Geographic subdivision.  When subdividing the heading Dissertations, Academic 
geographically, do not subdivide below the country level even when dealing with individual 
institutions.  Subdivide only by names of countries or larger geographic units.  For example, 
assign to a list of theses of Harvard University the heading Dissertations, AcademicBUnited 
StatesB Bibliography.
