A. Background  
LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead 
assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. 
*Catalogs*. To replace a form subdivision that includes a demographic group, also assign 
Library of Congress Demographic Groups Terms (LCDGT) to bring out the audience of the 
resource being cataloged, e.g. *German speakers* or *Arabic, Students of*. 
For guidelines on assigning LCGFT to periodicals, see the LCGFT Manual J243. 
For guidelines on catalogs of periodicals, see H 1361. For guidelines on electronic serials, see H 
1580.5. For guidelines on indexes to periodicals, see H 1670. For guidelines on newspapers on 
special topics and newspapers of ethnic groups in the United States, see H 1920. For guidelines 
on classification of periodicals, see F 210 and F 220.

C. Definitions 
Periodicals: a publication other than a newspaper that is actually or purportedly issued 
according to a regular schedule (monthly, quarterly, biennially, etc.) in successive parts, each of 
which bears a numerical or chronological designation, and that is intended to be continued 
indefinitely. 
Note: This definition is broader than the traditional definition, which states that a periodical is 
generally published more frequently than annually and normally contains separate articles. 
Serials: used in a broad sense to refer to any title cataloged as a serial, including periodicals, 
newspapers, monographic series, etc. 
D. General rule 
Use *--Periodicals* as a form subdivision under all headings assigned to works that conform to 
the definition of periodicals given above, including annual reports. Use this subdivision under 
topical headings, personal name headings, corporate body headings, geographic headings, etc., 
without restriction. 
LC practice: Assign subject headings to bring out the topic of the periodical. Also assign LCGFT 
appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Periodicals*. 
D.1. Regularity of publication 
When a resource’s frequency cannot be determined, assign the form subdivision *--
Periodicals* using cataloger’s judgement. Base this judgment on whether or not the periodical 
appears to be issued regularly. In case of doubt, assume that it is issued regularly and assign the 
form subdivision *--Periodicals*. 
If a publication is known to be issued irregularly but contains articles, etc., and in all other ways 
appears to be a periodical as defined above, assign the form subdivision *--Periodicals* 
LC practice: Use judgment to determine the frequency, as described above. As appropriate, 
assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT 
*Periodicals*. 
D.2. Juvenile periodicals 
When using this form subdivision *--Periodicals* in conjunction with the form subdivision *--
Juvenile literature*, construct the heading as follows:  
Model: 
[topic]--Periodicals--Juvenile literature.

LC practice: Do not use either form subdivision. Assign LCDGT to identify the intended 
audience, e.g. LCDGT *Children*. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the 
resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Periodicals*. 
D.3 Serials that are not periodicals 
Do not use the form subdivision under headings assigned to publications that are revised and 
reissued in full on a regular basis, and are therefore cataloged as serials for convenience, but 
are not periodicals as defined above. For example, Library of Congress Subject Headings was 
published annually and is cataloged as a serial, but the subdivision *--Periodicals* is 
inappropriate for this work. 
D.4 Monographic series 
Do not use the form subdivision under headings assigned to monographic series. 
E. --Periodicals under form headings and other form subdivisions 
Use the form subdivision *--Periodicals* as a further subdivision under most form headings and 
form subdivisions, for example, *American poetry--Periodicals; *Tuberculosis--Statistics--
Periodicals*. 
LC practice: Assign the subject heading for the form when it is needed to bring out language, 
nationality, or ethnicity. Also assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the 
resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Poetry* and/or *Periodicals*. 
E.1. Exception 
Do not use the form subdivision *--Periodicals* as a further subdivision under the following 
form subdivisions: 
• Amateurs’ manual 
• Atlases 
• Calendars 
• Cases 
• Congress 
• Databases 
• Dictionaries 
• Digests 
• Directories 
• Encyclopedias 
• Gazetteers

F. Works about periodicals 
Use *--Periodicals* also as a topical subdivision for general works that discuss periodicals on a 
specific subject. Do not further subdivide by *--History and criticism*. For works on the history 
of periodicals on a specific subject, the subdivision *--History* may be used. For bibliographies, 
catalogs, indexes, union lists, etc., of periodicals further subdivide *--Periodicals* by the 
appropriate form subdivision. However, for a bibliography of periodical articles on a topic, 
assign *[topic]--Bibliography* without interposing the subdivision *--Periodicals*. 
LC practice: For works about periodicals, assign *--Periodicals* as a topical subdivision. Assign 
LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT 
*Bibliographies*.
