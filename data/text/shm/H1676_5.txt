Inspection  H 1676.5 
 
 
 
Subject Headings Manual 
H 1676.5 Page 1 
 
June 2013 
BACKGROUND:  This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning the free-floating 
subdivision BInspection and for using phrase headings for particular types of inspection. 
 
 
 
1.  Merchandise and products.  Use the free-floating subdivision BInspection (May Subd 
Geog) under types of merchandise and products for works on methods of examining them to 
determine their fitness for marketing.  Example: 
 
650 #0 $a Lumber $x Inspection. 
 
2.  Machines, engineering structures, and types of buildings.  Use the free-floating 
subdivision BInspection (May Subd Geog) under types of machines, engineering structures, and 
buildings for works on methods and procedures for determining compliance with safety 
regulations.  Example: 
 
650 #0 $a Automobiles $x Inspection $z Virginia. 
 
3.  Plants and animals.  Use the free-floating subdivision BInspection (May Subd Geog) under 
individual and groups of plants and domestic animals for works on procedures for examining 
them to determine the presence of diseases or pests.  Examples:  
 
650 #0 $a Cattle $x Inspection. 
650 #0 $a Corn $x Inspection. 
650 #0 $a Poultry $x Inspection. 
 
4.  Military services.  Assign the free-floating subdivision BInspection under names of 
individual military services for works on official examinations of the service to determine and 
report on its state of readiness for duty.  Example: 
 
610 10 $a United States. $b Army $x Inspection. 
 
5.  Phrase headings.  Do not use the free-floating subdivision BInspection under topics if a 
phrase heading for the concept has already been established.  Examples: 
 
150 ## $a Factory inspection 
150 ## $a Mine inspection 
 
When assigning phrase headings of this type, verify that a 450 field of the type [topic]B 
Inspection appears in the authority record.  Make such a reference, if it does not already exist.
