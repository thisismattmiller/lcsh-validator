A. Background 
LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead 
assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. 
*Law digests*. 
For guidelines on abstracts, see H 1205. For guidelines on bibliographies of individual persons, 
see H 1322. For guidelines on bio-bibliographies, see H 1328. For guidelines on bibliographies of 
bibliographies, see H 1325. For catalogs of library materials, see H 1361. 
B. Recent revisions 
2026-02: Updated template. Added instructions related to LCGFT implementation. 
C. Definitions 
Digests: systematically arranged compilations of brief summaries of individual statutes, 
regulations, court decisions, or regulatory agency decisions on particular topics 
D. General rule 
Assign the subdivision *--Digests* as a free-floating form subdivision under legal headings for 
monographic or serial works consisting of systematically arranged compilations of brief 
summaries of individual statutes, regulations, court decisions, or regulatory agency decisions on 
particular topics. Do not further subdivide *--Digests* by *--Periodicals*. 
LC practice: Assign subject headings for the topic of the digest. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the 
form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Law digests*.
