A. Background 
This instruction sheet provided special guidelines for the assignment of genre/form terms to 
radio programs, or to portions of radio programs, that are cataloged by the Library of Congress. 
This information is now available in the Library of Congress Genre/Form Manual (LCGFT) 
instruction sheet J 270 Radio Programs and Podcasts.  
Guidelines for establishing new genre/form terms were included in this instruction sheet. This 
information can be found in the LCGFT Manual in instruction sheets J 120 When to Establish a 
New Genre/Form Term, J 140 Preparation of Genre/Form Term Proposals, and J 160 Authority 
Research and Citations for Genre/Form Term Proposals.  
For guidelines on assigning subject headings to bring out the content of individual radio 
programs, consult the appropriate instruction sheet(s).
