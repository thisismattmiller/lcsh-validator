Miscellanea  H 1910 
 
 
 
Subject Headings Manual 
H 1910 Page 1 
 
June 2013 
BACKGROUND:  The free-floating subdivision BMiscellanea is used for three types of 
materials described below.  This instruction sheet provides guidelines for its use in these three 
situations. 
 
 
 
1.  General.  Assign the free-floating form subdivision BMiscellanea under headings of any 
type, including topical subjects, names of persons, classes of persons, ethnic groups, individual 
corporate bodies, places, etc., for compilations of unusual or miscellaneous facts about the 
subject without continuous text.  Examples: 
 
630 00 $a Bible $v Miscellanea. 
650 #0 $a Medicine $v Miscellanea. 
610 20 $a United Nations $v Miscellanea. 
 
 
2.  Special.  Assign the free-floating topical subdivision BMiscellanea under headings of any 
type for works that deal with special aspects of a subject for which no other subdivision exists or 
can be established and yet, because of their unusual nature, cannot be treated as general works on 
the subject.  Examples: 
 
Title:  5000 B.C. and other philosophical fantasies. 
650 #0 $a Philosophy $x Miscellanea. 
 
Title:  Mindpower and the spiritual dimension. 
650 #0 $a Occultism $x Miscellanea.  
650 #0 $a Metaphysics $x Miscellanea. 
 
Title:  Napoleon's glands : and other ventures in biohistory. 
650 #0 $a Diseases and history $x Miscellanea. 
650 #0 $a Medicine $x Miscellanea. 
 
 
3.  Questions and answers.  Assign the free-floating form subdivision BMiscellanea under 
headings of any type for works on a topic written in a question and answer format. 
 
Assign the heading Questions and answers only to general collections of questions and answers 
not limited to a specific subject.
