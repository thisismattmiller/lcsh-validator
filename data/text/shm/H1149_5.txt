PATTERN:  Great BritainBColonies 
 
 
1.  The subdivision BColonies.  Use the subdivision BColonies under names of countries for 
works discussing collectively the colonies ruled by a country, for example, FranceBColonies; 
JapanB Colonies; RomeBColonies. 
 
Do not use the subdivision BColonies for works that discuss an individual colony.  For such 
works, assign the name of the colony with an appropriate subdivision, or the topical subject 
divided by the name of the colony, for example, IndiaBPolitics and governmentB1919-1947; 
Postage stampsBIndia. 
 
 
2.  [Topic]B[country].  Topical subjects subdivided by place may also be further subdivided by 
BColonies, for example, Postage stampsBGreat BritainBColonies. 
 
 
3.  [Country]BColonies.  Headings of the type [country]BColonies may be further subdivided 
by the topical and broad regional geographic subdivisions listed below, for example,  ItalyB 
ColoniesBCommerce; 
GermanyBColoniesBAfrica; 
FranceBColoniesBOceaniaBEconomic 
conditions. 
 
 
4.  The subdivision BPolitics and government.  Do not use the subdivision BPolitics and 
government under headings of the type [country]BColonies.  For general colonial politics and 
government, use the subdivision BAdministration, for example, PortugalBColoniesBAdminis- 
tration; Great BritainBColoniesBAmericaBAdministration. 
 
 
 
REGIONAL SUBDIVISIONS:
