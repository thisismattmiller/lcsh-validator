A. Background 
LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead 
assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. 
*Bibliographies*. 
For guidelines on abstracts, see H 1205. For guidelines on bio-bibliographies, see H 1328. For 
guidelines on bibliographies of bibliographies, see H 1325. For catalogs of library materials, see 
H 1361. For guidelines on digests of legal materials, see H 1550. 
B. Recent revisions 
2026-01: Updated template. Added instructions related to LCGFT implementation. 
C. General rule 
Use the free-floating subdivision *--Bibliography* under names of individuals for lists of works 
by or about those persons. 
For lists of musical works by a person that include musical notation for the opening measures of 
individual works or sections of works, use the free-floating form subdivision *--Thematic 
catalogs*. 
LC practice: Assign the names of individuals for lists of works by or about those persons. Assign 
LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT 
*Bibliographies* and/or a more specific term, e.g. *Thematic catalogs (Music)*. 
D. Special topics 
If the bibliography has a topical orientation, assign an additional heading of the type *[topic]--
Bibliography* to bring out the topic.
