A. Background 
The free-floating subdivision *--Sources* is used under historical topics, author-title and 
uniform title headings, certain types of literature headings, and headings for systems of law, for 
collections of writings such as documents, letters, etc., compiled for use by students, scholars, 
etc., in their research on the history of those topics, and for works discussing source materials 
of all types. It is also used under names of persons for works discussing the individual's sources

materials. This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning this subdivision. 
LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. For 
resources that would use form subdivisions, assign the LCGFT term appropriate to the genre 
and/or form of the resource in hand.

C. Definitions 
Sources: Collections of writings such as documents, letters, etc., compiled for use by students, 
scholars, etc.  
D. History 
Assign the free-floating subdivision *--Sources* under historical headings for collections or 
discussions of historical source materials. The subdivision *--Sources* is used directly after 
headings and subdivisions that denote history or a historical event or have an obvious historical 
connotation. The subdivision *--History--Sources*, or *--History--[period subdivision]--
Sources* is used after other headings to denote historical source materials. See H 1647 for 
instructions on use of the subdivision *--History* and a discussion of headings and subdivisions 
that are treated as inherently historical. 
LC practice: For discussions of historical sources materials, use the general subdivision *--
Sources* in conjunction with the appropriate topical subject headings. For collections of 
primary source materials, assign the appropriate subject headings for the topical focus of the 
resources. Also assign the LCGFT term(s) appropriate to the genre and/or form of the resource, 
e.g. LCGFT *Primary sources* or *Notebooks*. If it is a compilation of different types of 
primary source materials, add *Primary sources* and, if appropriate, also assign more specific 
terms. If the resource contains many different types of genre and/or forms, including one or 
more databases, consider the needs of specificity according to LCGFT Manual J 110. 
D.1. Collections 
Assign the free-floating form subdivision *--Sources* as a form subdivision under historical 
headings for collections of contemporary writings or records, such as legal documents, letters, 
diaries, family papers, etc., assembled at a later time to serve as source materials for use by 
students, scholars, etc., in their study or research on the subject.

F. Literature 
F.1. General 
Assign the free-floating topical subdivision *--Sources* under literatures, including individual 
genres, for discussions of sources of ideas or inspiration for these literary works, for example, 
*English poetry--Early modern, 1500-1700--Sources*. 
F.2. Individual literary authors 
In accordance with H 1110, assign the free-floating subdivision *--Sources* under names of 
individual literary authors for works discussing the individual's sources of ideas or inspiration 
for their literary works and for collections of those source materials, for example, 
*Shakespeare, William, 1564-1616--Sources*. 
LC practice: For discussions of source materials, assign the subject heading *[name of person]--
Sources*, using the topical subdivision. For collections of source materials, assign the subject 
heading *[name of person]* and other appropriate subject headings. Also assign the LCGFT 
term appropriate to the genre and/or form of the resource, e.g. LCGFT *Primary sources* or 
*Personal correspondence*. If the resource contains many different types of genre and/or 
forms, including one or more databases, consider the needs of specificity according to LCGFT 
Manual J 110. 
G. Names of persons 
In accordance with H 1110, assign the free-floating subdivision *--Sources* under names of 
persons for works discussing the individual's sources of ideas or inspiration for their endeavors 
or creative works, and for collections of those source materials. 
LC practice: For discussions of source materials, assign the subject heading *[name of person]--
Sources*, using the topical subdivision. For collections of source materials, assign the subject 
heading *[name of person]* and other appropriate subject headings. Also assign the LCGFT 
term appropriate to the genre and/or form of the resource, e.g. LCGFT *Primary sources* or 
*Personal correspondence*. If the resource contains many different types of genre and/or

H. Law 
LC practice: For discussions of source materials, assign the appropriate subject headings for the 
topical focus of the discussion, using the free-floating topical subdivision *--Sources*. For 
collections of source materials, assign appropriate subject headings for the topic of the 
collection. Also assign the LCGFT term appropriate to the genre and/or form of the resource, 
e.g. LCGFT *Law materials* or *Legal instruments*. If the resource contains many different

H.1. Systems of law 
Assign the free-floating subdivision *--Sources* under systems of law for works containing 
historical documents such as acts, customs, court decisions and records, jurists' writings, etc., 
that led to the formation of a particular legal system, and for works discussing these 
documents. 
LC practice: See LC practice stated above. 
Examples: 
650 #0 $a Canon law $v Sources. 
650 #0 $a Islamic law $v Sources. 
650 #0 $a Law $z Burma $v Sources. 
650 #0 $a Law, Cherokee $v Sources.

H.2. Legal topics 
Assign the free-floating subdivision *--Sources* under headings of the type *[legal topic]--
History* for collections and discussions of documents serving as source materials for the 
history of the topic. For collections, code *--Sources* $v; for discussions of sources, code *--
Sources* $x.

I.1. Bibliographies 
Assign the free-floating subdivision *--Bibliography* under headings of the type *[topic]--
Sources* or *[topic]--History--Sources* for bibliographies of source materials on the topic. 
LC practice: Assign the appropriate subject headings for the topical focus of the bibliography. 
Also assign the LCGFT term appropriate to the genre and/or form of the resource, e.g. LCGFT 
*Bibliographies*.
