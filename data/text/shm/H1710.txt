June 2013 
BACKGROUND:  This instruction sheet describes the use of the subdivisions BLegal research 
and BResearch under legal topics. 
 
 
 
 
1.  BLegal research.  Use this subdivision under inherently legal headings, including topical 
headings with the subdivision BLaw and legislation or BLegal status, laws, etc., for works 
which discuss the use of legal research tools such as court reports, codes, digests, citators, etc., in 
determining the status of statutory, regulatory, or case law on a topic.  Example: 
 
Title:  How to find current tax law. 
650 #0 $a Taxation $x Law and legislation $z United States  
            $x Legal research. 
 
BLegal research is controlled by the pattern heading for legal topics, Labor laws and 
legislation, and is therefore free-floating when used according to these guidelines.  As 
illustrated in the above example, the subdivision is not further subdivided by place. 
   
 
 
2.  BResearch.  Use this subdivision, further subdivided by place when appropriate, under legal 
topics in accordance with the normal practice for its use under other topical headings, i.e. for 
descriptions of proposed research, including such details as management, finance, personnel, 
special projects, methodology, goals, etc..  Example: 
 
Title:  Threats to validity in quasi-experimental evaluations of consumer 
protection reforms : a critical review of extant research. 
650 #0 $a Consumer protection $x Law and legislation  
            $x Research $z United States. 
650 #0 $a Consumer credit $x Law and legislation $x Research 
            $z United States.
