Articles in the Initial Position  H 290 
 
 
 
Subject Headings Manual 
H 290 Page 1 
 
June 2013 
1.  General rule.  Do not establish subject headings with an article (definite or indefinite) in the 
initial position.  This instruction applies both to English articles and to all foreign language 
equivalents in the nominative case.  Examples: 
 
 
150 ## $a Arts 
   [not 
150 ## $a The arts] 
 
150 ## $a Finite, The 
   [not 
150 ## $a The finite] 
 
150 ## $a Questione della lingua 
   [not 
150 ## $a La questione della lingua] 
 
 
 
2.  Geographic names.  For geographic names beginning with an article, see H 690, sec. 6. 
 
 
 
3.  Titles of publications.  Omit all initial articles that precede words in the nominative case 
when assigning titles as subject headings.  For further details about the assignment of uniform 
titles as subject headings, see H 1435.
