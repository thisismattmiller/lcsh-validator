## Citation check

The cited rule text in `rules/` was compared with each claim. In 31 of 35 the cited entry says what the verdict says it says. Four misread or over-cite:

- Administrative agencies--China--Reorganization: cites "H 1095 sec. F (usage statement)" but --Reorganization has no H 1095 entry; it is on H 1105 and H 1159 only. The claim itself is right.
- Colon (Anatomy)--Cancer--Patients--Japan--Biography: the H 1095 --Biography usage does name classes of persons, and --Patients is one. The doubt ignores that clause.
- Flowers in art--Catalogs: quotes "artists, sellers, corporate bodies and archaeological sites" as the usage; those are secondary clauses, the primary one is "types of objects". Same doubt as Fantasy in art, stated less accurately.
- Mentally ill--Care--Directories: the H 1095 --Directories usage ends "and topical headings for individual directories containing names, addresses...", which covers a directory after a topical subdivision. The doubt reads only the class-of-persons clause.

Tally: 21 usage-statement category calls, 6 two-pass splits, 3 "established directly, not after a place", 2 "siblings established, this one not", 3 other sheet readings (H 1154 multiple, H 1642 phrase heading, H 1075 order).
