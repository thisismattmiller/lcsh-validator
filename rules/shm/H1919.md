# H 1919 Name and Names

## Validation rules

1. `--Name` (singular) is free-floating under names of individual persons, deities, places, corporate bodies, Christian denominations, wars, events, and ethnic groups, for works on the origin, history, spelling, etc., of that name, e.g. `Catalonia (Spain)--Name`; `Jesus Christ--Name`; `United States--History--Civil War, 1861-1865--Name`. (H 1919 sec. 1)
2. `--Names` (plural) is free-floating under types of objects, animals, events, organizations, educational institutions, etc., for works on the origin and history of their names or naming rules and customs, e.g. `Dogs--Names`; `Stars--Names`; `Constellations--Names`. (H 1919 sec. 2.a)
3. Use singular `--Name` under an individual named entity and plural `--Names` under a type/class heading; do not interchange them. (H 1919 sec. 1, 2.a)
4. Do not use `[type]--Names` if an established phrase heading exists instead, e.g. `Street names` [not `Streets--Names`]. (H 1919 sec. 2.a)
5. For names in a particular language or language group, use `--Etymology--Names` under the language (per H 1154), e.g. `English language--Etymology--Names`; for a special category of names also assign a heading for the category (e.g. `Field names--Scotland`). (H 1919 sec. 2.b)

## Full text

BACKGROUND: The subdivisions --Name and --Names are free-floating under certain types of headings. This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning these subdivisions.

### 1. --Name.

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Name under names of individual persons, deities, places, corporate bodies, Christian denominations, wars, events, and ethnic groups for works on the origin, history, spelling, etc., of the name of the person, place, etc. Examples:

```
Title: The formation of Catalonia.
651 #0 $a Catalonia (Spain) $x Name.

Title: The name above every name.
600 00 $a Jesus Christ $x Name.

Title: Name of the American war of 1861-1865.
650 #0 $a United States $x History $y Civil War, 1861-1865 $x Name.
```

### 2. --Names.

**a. General.** Assign the free-floating subdivision --Names under types of objects, animals, events, organizations, educational institutions, etc., for works on the origin and history of these names and/or the rules, procedures, customs, etc., applied in selecting names for these types of entities. Examples:

```
Title: Anything but Rover : the art and science of naming your dog.
650 #0 $a Dogs $x Names.
650 #0 $a Dog breeds.

Title: What's in the names of stars and constellations?
650 #0 $a Stars $x Names.
650 #0 $a Constellations $x Names.
```

Do not assign a heading of this type if a phrase heading has been established instead. Example:

```
Street names  [not Streets--Names]
```

**b. Languages.** In accordance with H 1154, assign the free-floating subdivision --Etymology--Names under individual languages and groups of languages for works on the origin and spelling of names in a particular language. If the work discusses a special category of names, assign headings to cover both the language or language group and the special topic. Example:

```
Title: Scottish field names.
650 #0 $a Field names $z Scotland.
650 #0 $a English language $x Etymology $x Names.
651 #0 $a Scotland $x History, Local.
```
