# H 2225 Transportation

## Validation rules

1. `Transportation` (May Subd Geog) is the heading for general works on movement of goods or passengers, e.g. `Transportation--Great Britain`, `Transportation--Energy conservation--United States`. (H 2225 sec. 1)
2. Do not subdivide `Transportation` or `--Transportation` by `--Economic aspects`. (H 2225 sec. 1, 3)
3. `Urban transportation` (May Subd Geog) is for general works on urban transportation (e.g. `Urban transportation--France`); for individual cities, city regions, and metropolitan areas use `Transportation--[place]`, e.g. `Transportation--Pennsylvania--Philadelphia`, `Transportation--New Jersey--Trenton Metropolitan Area`, not `Urban transportation--[city]`. (H 2225 sec. 2)
4. `--Transportation` (May Subd Geog) is free-floating under types of objects, merchandise, classes of persons, ethnic groups, animals, military services, and individual wars, e.g. `Fruit--Transportation--Hawaii`, `Older people--Transportation--Great Britain`, `World War, 1939-1945--Transportation`. (H 2225 sec. 3)
5. Do not subdivide `--Transportation` by place when it is used under military services. (H 2225 sec. 3)
6. Do not establish special subdivisions under topics for particular types of transportation; assign additional headings (e.g. `Roads--Australia--History`, `Inland water transportation--United States`). (H 2225 sec. 3)
7. Special types or aspects of transportation take specific headings, e.g. `Coastwise shipping`, `High speed ground transportation`, `Inland water transportation`, `Rural transit`, `Urban transportation policy`. (H 2225 sec. 4)

## Full text

BACKGROUND: The subject heading Transportation (May Subd Geog), the free-floating subdivision --Transportation, and headings for particular types of transportation or special topics in transportation are assigned to works that discuss means or systems of conveying, moving, or shipping goods or passengers. This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning subject headings to works about transportation.

### 1. The subject heading Transportation

Assign the heading Transportation (May Subd Geog), with further subdivision, if appropriate, to general works on the movement of goods or passengers. Do not subdivide this heading by --Economic aspects. Examples:

```
Title: The economics of British public transport.
650 #0 $a Transportation $z Great Britain.

Title: Transportation energy data book.
650 #0 $a Transportation $x Energy conservation $z United States.
650 #0 $a Transportation $x Energy consumption $z United States.
```

### 2. The subject heading Urban transportation

Assign the heading Urban transportation (May Subd Geog) to general works on transportation in urban areas, including local transit, private transportation, streets, roads, etc. Assign the heading Transportation (May Subd Geog) to works on urban transportation in individual cities, city regions, and metropolitan areas. Examples:

```
Title: Urban transportation in France.
650 #0 $a Urban transportation $z France.

Title: Philadelphia on the move.
650 #0 $a Transportation $z Pennsylvania $z Philadelphia.

Title: The Trenton area : moving or standing still?
650 #0 $a Transportation $z New Jersey $z Trenton Metropolitan Area.
```

### 3. The free-floating subdivision --Transportation

Use the free-floating subdivision --Transportation (May Subd Geog) under types of objects, merchandise, classes of persons, ethnic groups, animals, military services, and individual wars for works on transportation as applied to these topics. Do not further subdivide --Transportation by place when used under military services. Do not subdivide --Transportation further by --Economic aspects. Examples:

```
Title: Transportation services for elderly and disabled people.
650 #0 $a Older people $x Transportation $z Great Britain.
650 #0 $a People with disabilities $x Transportation $z Great Britain.

Title: Hawaii unloads fresh fruit and vegetables.
650 #0 $a Fruit $x Transportation $z Hawaii.
650 #0 $a Vegetables $x Transportation $z Hawaii.
```

Assign additional headings, if necessary, to bring out special aspects of transportation in relation to the topic. Do not establish special subdivisions under topics to bring out particular types of transportation. Examples:

```
Title: Beef roads in Australia.
650 #0 $a Beef cattle $x Transportation $z Australia $x History.
650 #0 $a Roads $z Australia $x History.

Title: The Irish merchant marine in World War II.
650 #0 $a World War, 1939-1945 $x Transportation.
650 #0 $a Merchant marine $z Ireland $x History $y 20th century.
651 #0 $a Ireland $x History, Naval.

Title: Transportation of coal and grain by waterway.
650 #0 $a Coal $x Transportation $z United States.
650 #0 $a Grain $x Transportation $z United States.
650 #0 $a Inland water transportation $z United States.

Title: Going places in your own vehicle.
650 #0 $a People with disabilities $x Transportation.
650 #0 $a Automobile drivers with disabilities.
```

### 4. Special topics

Assign appropriate specific headings to works on special types or aspects of transportation. Examples:

```
650 #0 $a Coastwise shipping.
650 #0 $a High speed ground transportation.
650 #0 $a Inland water transportation.
650 #0 $a Rural transit.
650 #0 $a Urban transportation policy.
```
