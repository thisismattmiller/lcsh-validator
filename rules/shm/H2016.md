# H 2016 Religious Life and Customs

## Validation rules

1. `--Religious life and customs` is free-floating under names of countries, cities, etc., e.g. `Brazil--Religious life and customs`, `New York (N.Y.)--Religious life and customs`. (H 2016 sec. 1)
2. Do not use `--History` after `--Religious life and customs`. (H 2016 sec. 2)
3. Established period subdivisions may follow: `[place]--Religious life and customs--[period subdivision]`, e.g. `Germany--Religious life and customs--Middle Ages, 843-1517`. (H 2016 sec. 2)
4. New period subdivisions may be established under `[place]--Religious life and customs` except when the place is a city or town. (H 2016 sec. 2)
5. Customs of an individual religion or denomination in a place: `[religion or denomination]--Customs and practices` (with place, e.g. `Catholic Church--Mexico--Customs and practices`) plus `[place]--Religious life and customs`. (H 2016 sec. 3)

## Full text

BACKGROUND: The subdivision --Religious life and customs is used under names of countries, cities, etc., for works on the religious customs, practices, etc., of these places. This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning this subdivision.

### 1. General

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Religious life and customs under names of countries, cities, etc. Examples:

```
651 #0 $a Asia $x Religious life and customs.
651 #0 $a Brazil $x Religious life and customs.
651 #0 $a California $x Religious life and customs.
651 #0 $a New York (N.Y.) $x Religious life and customs.
```

### 2. By period

Do not use the subdivision --History after --Religious life and customs (see H 1647). If period subdivisions have been established and are appropriate for the work, assign headings of the type [place]--Religious life and customs--[period subdivision] to works on the history of the religious customs, etc., in a place during a particular period. Example:

```
651 #0 $a Germany $x Religious life and customs $y Middle Ages, 843-1517.
```

Establish new period subdivisions under headings of the type [place]--Religious life and customs as needed, except if the place in question is a city or town.

### 3. By religion or denomination

Assign headings of the type [religion or denomination]--Customs and practices and [place]--Religious life and customs to works on the customs and practices of an individual religion or denomination in a particular place. Example:

```
Title: Popular expressions of Catholicism in Mexico today.
610 20 $a Catholic Church $z Mexico $x Customs and practices.
651 #0 $a Mexico $x Religious life and customs.
```
