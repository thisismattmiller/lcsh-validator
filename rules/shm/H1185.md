# H 1185 Pattern Headings: Religions

## Validation rules

1. Subdivisions on this list are free-floating under any heading for a named religion, or a sect or denomination of a religion (e.g., Bahai Faith; Hinduism; International Society for Krishna Consciousness; Islam; Judaism; Shin (Sect); Shinto; Sufism; United Church of Religious Science; Vaishnavism), if appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. (H 1185, Types of headings; Conflicts)
2. Do not apply this list to Christianity or to Christian denominations and sects; use H 1187 for denominations and the subject authority file for Christianity. (H 1185, Types of headings)
3. Do not apply this list to name headings for individual or local temples, congregations, etc., or to subject headings for religious movements. (H 1185, Types of headings)
4. For individual religious or monastic orders of any religion use H 1186, not this list. (H 1185, Types of headings)
5. Where a variant phrase or subdivision equivalent to a list subdivision is established in the subject authority file, the established variant takes precedence over the free-floating form. (H 1185, Conflicts)
6. Most `$v` form subdivisions on the list may also be used as `$x` topical subdivisions for works about the form (e.g., `--Sermons--History and criticism`). (H 1185, Note; H 1075 sec. 1.d)
7. Only the subdivisions marked (May Subd Geog) may be followed by a geographic subdivision: --Liturgical objects, --Missions, --Political aspects, --Sacred books--Preservation, --Social aspects. (H 1185, list)
8. --Political aspects is used only under individual sects or denominations, not under a religion; for a religion use a heading of the type `[name of religion] and politics`, e.g., `Buddhism and politics` [not `Buddhism--Political aspects`]. (H 1185, note 1)
9. Do not use --Rituals or any of its further subdivisions (--Rituals--Texts, --Rituals--Texts--Concordances, --Rituals--Texts--History and criticism) under Judaism or sects of Judaism; use --Liturgy under Judaism, its sects, and its rites, which may be further subdivided by --Study and teaching, --Texts, and --Texts--History and criticism. (H 1185, note 2)
10. --Sermons (and --Sermons--History and criticism) is used only under individual sects or denominations; for an individual religion establish a heading of the type `[name of religion] sermons`, e.g., `Buddhist sermons` [not `Buddhism--Sermons`]. (H 1185, note 4)
11. --Relations--Christianity, [Islam, etc.] names another religion; make an additional entry with the two religions reversed (e.g., `Buddhism--Relations--Islam` and `Islam--Relations--Buddhism`); see H 1996 secs. 1 and 3. (H 1185, note 5)
12. Subdivisions for uniform titles of individual sacred works are on H 1188; --Sacred books is used under the religion for sacred books collectively. (H 1185, note 3)
13. In multi-level list entries the subfield coding is fixed: e.g., `$x Rituals $v Texts`, but `$x Rituals $x Texts $v Concordances`; `$x Sacred books $v Introductions`; `$x Sacred books $v Quotations`. (H 1185, list)
14. [LC practice] If an exceptional variant form is retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating form (H 195); otherwise propose changing the variant (H 193). (H 1185, LC practice)

## Full text

PATTERN: *Buddhism*

### Types of headings covered by the pattern

Headings for named religions, including their sects and denominations, established in either the name authority file or the subject authority file. Examples: Bahai Faith; Hinduism; International Society for Krishna Consciousness; Islam; Judaism; Shin (Sect); Shinto; Sufism; United Church of Religious Science; Vaishnavism. The category does not include Christianity or Christian denominations and sects. For variant Christian phrase headings and subdivisions used under Christianity, see the subject authority file. For subdivisions used under individual Christian denominations and sects, see H 1187. The category also does not cover name headings for individual or local temples, congregations, etc., nor subject headings for religious movements. For subdivisions used under the names of individual religious or monastic orders of all religions, see H 1186.

### Conflicts

Any subdivision listed here can be used as a free-floating subdivision under any heading belonging to the category if it is appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. Subject authority records may exist for headings employing variant phrases or subdivisions equivalent to subdivisions on this list.

LC practice: If an exceptional variant form is to be retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form following the procedures in H 195 if the reference does not yet exist. Otherwise, submit a proposal to change the variant form along with all bibliographic records requiring correction following the procedures in H 193.

Note: Most form subdivisions coded $v in this list may also be used as topical subdivisions coded $x when assigned to works about the form (see H 1075, sec. 1.d.).

### Notes

1. Not established under Buddhism. Use only under individual sects or denominations. For religions, assign a heading of the type: [name of religion] and politics, for example, *Buddhism and politics*.
2. Do not use the subdivision --Rituals and its further subdivisions under Judaism and sects of Judaism. Instead use the subdivision --Liturgy under Judaism, its sects, and its rites. The latter subdivision may be further subdivided by --Study and teaching; --Texts; and --Texts--History and criticism.
3. For subdivisions used under the uniform titles of individual sacred works, see H 1188.
4. Use only under individual sects or denominations. For individual religions, establish headings of the type [name of religion] sermons, for example, *Buddhist sermons*.
5. For instructions on the use of this subdivision see H 1996 sec. 1 and sec. 3. Make an additional subject entry with the two religions in reversed positions (e.g., Buddhism--Relations--Islam and Islam--Relations--Buddhism).
