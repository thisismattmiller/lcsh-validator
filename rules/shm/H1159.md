# H 1159 Pattern Headings: Military Services

## Validation rules

1. Subdivisions on this list are free-floating under names of individual military services established as corporate bodies in the NAF, including state or provincial organizations such as national guards (e.g. *Great Britain. Army*; *Italy. Marina*; *Germany. Kriegsmarine*; *Japan. Rikugun*; *Texas. National Guard*). (H 1159 sec. B)
2. The list also applies under *[region or country]--Armed Forces*, *[region or country]--National Guard*, *[place larger than a city]--Militia*, and *[international agency]--Armed Forces* (e.g. *Soviet Union--Armed Forces*; *United States--National Guard*; *Ohio--Militia*; *United Nations--Armed Forces*; *Warsaw Treaty Organization--Armed Forces*). (H 1159 sec. B)
3. Do not use this list under names of units lower than a branch of the armed forces (divisions, regiments, battalions, fleets, squadrons; see H 1995) or under government agencies operating military services (e.g. *United States. Navy Department*). (H 1159 sec. C)
4. Subdivisions are free-floating under all branches unless a conflicting subdivision is used under another branch; such conflicts are noted on the list. (H 1159 sec. D.1)
5. Class-of-persons subdivisions on this list (e.g. `--Apprentices`, `--Nurses`, `--Officers`, `--Women`) may be further subdivided by H 1100 subdivisions. (H 1159 sec. F.1)
6. `--History` and `--Recruiting, enlistment, etc.` may be further subdivided free-floating by the name of a war or armed conflict in which the service took part, formed from the war heading (e.g. *Great Britain. Royal Navy--History--Falkland Islands War, 1982*; *United States. Navy--History--Spanish-American War, 1898*; *France. Armée--History--Revolution, 1789-1799*; *Canada. Royal Canadian Navy--History--World War, 1939-1945*); assign an additional heading for the war. (H 1159 sec. F.2)
7. `--History--Revolution, 1775-1783` and `--Recruiting, enlistment, etc.--Revolution, 1775-1783` are not valid under *United States. Army*; use *United States. Continental Army--History* and *United States. Continental Army--Recruiting, enlistment, etc.* (H 1159 sec. H)
8. `--History` may be subdivided by the period subdivisions `--16th century` through `--21st century`. (H 1159 sec. H)
9. `--Air police` is used only under air forces; use `--Shore patrol` under navies and `--Military police` under other services. `--Military police` is not used under air forces or navies. `--Shore patrol` is used only under navies. (H 1159 sec. H)
10. `--Ground support` is used only under air forces; under other services use `--Aviation--Ground support`; `--Aviation--Ground support` is used under services other than air forces. (H 1159 sec. H)
11. `--Aviation--Job descriptions` is not used under air forces. (H 1159 sec. H-I)
12. `--Commissariat` is not used under navies; use `--Provisioning` under navies; `--Provisioning` is used only under navies. (H 1159 sec. H)
13. `--Watch duty` is used under individual navies. (H 1159 sec. H)
14. `--Colonial forces` is not established under any branch of the United States armed forces; use only under military services of countries having colonial forces (e.g. *Great Britain. Army--Colonial forces*; *Spain. Ejército--Colonial forces*). (H 1159 sec. H)
15. Do not use under *[place]--Armed Forces*: `--Chaplains` (use *Military chaplains--[place]*), `--Gay people` (use *Gay military personnel--[place]*), `--Medals, badges, decorations, etc.` (use *Military decorations--[place]*), `--Military capital` (use *Military capital--[place]*), `--Social services` (use *Military social work--[place]*). (H 1159 sec. H)
16. `--Officials and employees` is not used under armies, navies, and other branches; use only under *[place]--Armed Forces* and under names of government agencies responsible for the operation of individual military services (e.g. *United States. Department of the Army--Officials and employees*). (H 1159 sec. H)
17. `--Cruise, [date]` requires a date formed per H 1078. (H 1159 sec. H)
18. `--Foreign countries` ($z) is used as a geographic subdivision under military services. (H 1159 sec. H)
19. For `--Artillery`, `--Cavalry`, `--Infantry`, where the arm is an organized unit, use the subdivision (e.g. *Belgium. Armée--Artillery*) and add the corporate body heading (e.g. *Belgium. Armée. Artillerie*) for corporate/organizational aspects. (H 1159 sec. G, H)
20. Works on Canadian military services collectively take *Canada--Armed Forces* regardless of period; *Canada. Canadian Armed Forces* is used only after 1968 for corporate/organizational aspects. (H 1159 sec. G)
21. Legal subdivisions (`--Facilities--Law and legislation`, `--Finance--Law and legislation`, `--Medical care--Law and legislation`, etc.) may be further subdivided per H 1154.5. (H 1159 sec. H)
22. Only subdivisions marked May Subd Geog (e.g. `--Colonial forces`, `--Dental care`, `--Environmental aspects`, `--Foreign service`, `--Job descriptions`, `--Journalists`, `--Medical care`, `--Physical training`, `--Political activity`, `--Public relations`, `--Search and rescue operations`, `--Tactical aviation`, `--Vocational guidance`) may be subdivided geographically. (H 1159 sec. H)
23. `--Biography` ($v) under a military service is for collective biographies covering various categories; for a specific category use `--Biography` under the specific subdivision (e.g. `--African Americans--Biography`; `--Nurses--Biography`; `--Officers--Biography`). (H 1159 sec. I)
24. Form subdivisions on this list (valid LCSH) include combinations such as `--Archives--Microform catalogs`, `--Drill and tactics--Handbooks, manuals, etc.`, `--Examinations--Study guides`, `--History--Chronology`, `--Songs and music--Discography`, `--Songs and music--Texts`, `--Biography--Portraits`. (H 1159 sec. I)
25. [LC practice] LC catalogers no longer use form subdivisions (phased out in early 2025); they assign LCGFT terms instead. (H 1159 sec. I)
26. [LC practice] Establish new subdivisions under *United States--Armed Forces* where possible; otherwise under the pattern heading for the particular branch (e.g. *United States. Air Force*); if unique to non-US services, under the specific heading. (H 1159 sec. E)
27. [LC practice] If an exceptional variant form is retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form (H 195); otherwise propose a change (H 193). (H 1159 sec. D.2)

## Full text

Patterns: *United States--Armed Forces*; *United States. Air Force*; *United States. Army*; *United States. Marine Corps*; *United States. Navy*

### A. Recent revisions

2026-03: Updated template. Clarified text. Separated general and form subdivisions. Added instructions related to LCGFT implementation.

### B. Types of headings covered by the pattern

This instruction sheet contains the free-floating subdivisions that may be used under headings for names of individual military services established as corporate bodies in the name authority file, including military organizations at the state or province level, such as national guards. Also included are subject headings formed using the free-floating subdivisions *--Armed Forces* and *--National Guard* under names of regions and countries, and the free-floating subdivision *--Militia* under names of places larger than cities, and headings established using *--Armed Forces* under names of international agencies having armed forces.

Examples:

```
Great Britain. Army
Italy. Marina
Germany. Kriegsmarine
Japan. Rikugun
Texas. National Guard
Soviet Union--Armed Forces
United States--National Guard
Ohio--Militia
United Nations--Armed Forces
Warsaw Treaty Organization--Armed Forces
```

### C. Types of headings not covered

The category does not include names of subordinate organizational units lower than a branch of the armed forces, such as divisions, regiments, battalions, fleets, or squadrons. For instructions on assigning headings to histories of individual organizational units lower than a branch of the armed forces, see H 1995. Also excluded are names of government agencies responsible for the operation of individual military services, for example, *United States. Navy Department*.

### D. Conflicts

### D.1. General rule

Any subdivision listed here can be used as a free-floating subdivision under any heading belonging to the category if it is appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. Although many of the subdivisions listed here are logically applicable to only one branch of military service, they have been treated in this list as free-floating under all branches provided there is no conflicting subdivision used under another branch. Such conflicts are identified in the subdivision lists below. Subject authority records may exist for headings employing variant phrases or subdivisions equivalent to subdivisions on this list.

### D.2. LC practice

If an exceptional variant form is to be retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form following the procedures in H 195 if the reference does not yet exist. Otherwise, submit a proposal to change the variant form along with all bibliographic records requiring correction following the procedures in H 193.

### E. Establishing new subdivisions

Establish new subdivisions under *United States--Armed Forces* in all cases where it is possible to do so. If a subdivision is not logically appropriate under that heading, establish it under the pattern heading for the particular branch of military service needed for the work being cataloged. For example, if a new subdivision is required for a work on a particular air force and the subdivision cannot logically be established under *United States--Armed Forces*, establish it under *United States. Air Force*. If the topic of the proposed new subdivision is unique to the military services of countries other than the United States, and therefore cannot logically be established under any of the pattern headings, establish it under the specific heading required for the work being cataloged.

### F. Combinations of subdivisions

### F.1. Class of persons subdivisions

Subdivisions on this list representing classes of persons, for example, *--Apprentices*; *--Nurses*; *--Officers*, may be further subdivided, as appropriate, by subdivisions from the list of free-floating subdivisions used under classes of persons (H 1100).

### F.2. Period subdivisions

The subdivisions *--History* and *--Recruiting, enlistment, etc.* may be further subdivided on a free-floating basis by subdivisions for wars or armed conflicts in which the military service took part. The subdivisions may be formed based on headings for wars established either directly under their names or as subdivisions under place names.

Examples

```
Great Britain. Royal Navy--History--Falkland Islands War, 1982
United States. Navy--History--Spanish-American War, 1898
France. Armée--History--Revolution, 1789-1799
Canada. Royal Canadian Navy--History--World War, 1939-1945
```

Assign an additional heading designating the war with appropriate topical or geographic subdivisions. For subdivisions used under names of individual wars, see H 1200.

### G. Special provisions for corporate bodies

Several of the subdivisions listed below may be equivalent to a corporate body subheading. If the concept exists as a corporate body, assign a heading with the subdivision and assign an additional heading for the corporate body if the work discusses the corporate or organizational aspects of the body. For example, in Belgium the artillery is an organized unit represented by the corporate heading *Belgium. Armée. Artillerie*. For works on the Belgian artillery, assign the heading *Belgium. Armée--Artillery*. If appropriate, assign as an additional heading the name of the corporate body.

In some cases, the corporate heading may only be valid for certain time periods. For example, Canada united all military services into one organization in 1968. The corporate heading *Canada. Canadian Armed Forces* should be used only for the period after 1968 and only for discussions of the corporate or organizational aspects of the body. All works on the Canadian military services discussed collectively should be assigned the subject heading *Canada--Armed Forces*, regardless of the time period covered.

LC practice: In early 2025, the use of form subdivisions was phased out. Library of Congress catalogers assign Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT) to bring out the form(s) and/or genre(s) of the resource being cataloged. Therefore, the form subdivisions maintained on this list are not available for use by Library of Congress catalogers. Catalogers at institutions external to LC may use them as dictated by their own institutional policies.
