# H 2110 Study and Teaching

## Validation rules

1. `--Study and teaching` (May Subd Geog) is free-floating under names of individual persons, corporate bodies, and places, and under classes of persons, ethnic groups, and topical headings. (H 2110 sec. 1)
2. Do not use `--Study and teaching` under music headings (use `--Instruction and study` (May Subd Geog), per H 1160 and H 1161) or under names of individual Christian denominations. (H 2110 sec. 1, 8)
3. Where a phrase heading covers education in a broad discipline (e.g. `Agricultural education`, `Technical education`), `[topic]--Study and teaching` is restricted to methods of study and teaching. (H 2110 sec. 1)
4. Level of study is expressed by a parenthetical qualifier directly after the subdivision; only these are free-floating: `--Study and teaching (Continuing education)`, `--Study and teaching (Early childhood)`, `--Study and teaching (Elementary)`, `--Study and teaching (Graduate)`, `--Study and teaching (Higher)`, `--Study and teaching (Internship)`, `--Study and teaching (Middle school)`, `--Study and teaching (Preschool)`, `--Study and teaching (Primary)`, `--Study and teaching (Residency)`, `--Study and teaching (Secondary)`. (H 2110 sec. 2)
5. A geographic subdivision follows the (qualified) subdivision: `History--Study and teaching (Elementary)--France`, `Science--Study and teaching (Higher)--Great Britain`. (H 2110 sec. 2)
6. `--Study and teaching--Supervision` (May Subd Geog) is free-floating under topical headings. (H 2110 sec. 3)
7. Training for an occupation: `[topic]--Study and teaching` or `[...] education` and `[class of persons]--Training of` (see H 2217). (H 2110 sec. 4)
8. Phrase headings of the type `[...] studies` for area studies (e.g. `African studies`) were discontinued in 1977; use `[place]--Study and teaching`. (H 2110 sec. 5)
9. For works on study of a place published in that place, omit the geographic subdivision after `--Study and teaching`; for study of a place carried out elsewhere (another country or a locality of the same country), subdivide by the place where the teaching occurs, e.g. `United States--Study and teaching (Secondary)--China`, `United States--Study and teaching (Secondary)--Massachusetts--Boston`. (H 2110 sec. 5)
10. A topical subdivision may be interposed between a place and `--Study and teaching`, e.g. `Sweden--Social life and customs--Study and teaching`. (H 2110 sec. 5)
11. Do not interpose a place between a topical heading and `--Study and teaching` (invalid: `Science--United States--Study and teaching`). (H 2110 sec. 5)
12. `--Study and teaching` is free-floating under individual languages and language groups, with level qualifier if appropriate (e.g. `English language--Study and teaching (Continuing education)`), and may be further subdivided by special topics per H 1154 (e.g. `Spanish language--Study and teaching--Foreign speakers`). (H 2110 sec. 6)
13. `--Study and teaching` is free-floating under uniform titles of sacred works and their parts, e.g. `Book of Mormon--Study and teaching`, `Bible. Gospels--Study and teaching`. (H 2110 sec. 7)
14. The subdivision `--Study` under individual sacred works is obsolete (pre-1991). (H 2110 sec. 7 note)
15. `--Study and teaching--Audio-visual aids` is free-floating under topics for learning/teaching aids; audiovisual materials on a topic not designed for education take `[topic]--Audio-visual aids` directly. (H 2110 sec. 9)
16. `--Study and teaching--Simulation methods` is free-floating under topics; a particular simulation method gets an additional heading (e.g. `Simulation games in education`). (H 2110 sec. 10)
17. `--Study and teaching--Activity programs` (May Subd Geog) is free-floating under topics. (H 2110 sec. 11)
18. Do not assign `[topic]--Study and teaching` to textbooks for students (see H 2187). (H 2110 sec. 12)

## Full text

BACKGROUND: The subdivision --Study and teaching is used under certain types of headings for works on study and teaching, including methods, institutions, facilities, programs, courses, funding, etc. This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning the subdivision --Study and teaching. For classification of works on the teaching of particular subjects, see F 730.

### 1. General

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Study and teaching (May Subd Geog) under names of individual persons, corporate bodies, and places, and under classes of persons, ethnic groups, and topical headings for works on study and teaching about these subjects.

Do not use this subdivision under music headings, or under names of individual Christian denominations.

For some broad disciplines, phrase headings have been established to cover all aspects of study and teaching pertaining to these subjects, for example, Agricultural education; Technical education. In these fields headings of the type [topic]--Study and teaching are restricted in meaning to methods of study and teaching.

### 2. Level of study

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Study and teaching, qualified by level of study, to works on the study and teaching of a topic limited to a particular educational level, for example, History--Study and teaching (Elementary)--France; Science--Study and teaching (Higher)--Great Britain.

The following free-floating subdivisions may be used, if appropriate:

- --Study and teaching (Continuing education)
- --Study and teaching (Early childhood)
- --Study and teaching (Elementary)
- --Study and teaching (Graduate)
- --Study and teaching (Higher)
- --Study and teaching (Internship)
- --Study and teaching (Middle school)
- --Study and teaching (Preschool)
- --Study and teaching (Primary)
- --Study and teaching (Residency)
- --Study and teaching (Secondary)

### 3. Supervision

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Study and teaching--Supervision (May Subd Geog) under topical headings to works on the supervision of study and teaching in these fields. Example:

```
Title: The Art of clinical supervision : a pastoral counseling perspective.
650 #0 $a Pastoral counseling $x Study and teaching $x Supervision.
```

### 4. --Study and teaching vs. --Training of

To works that discuss the study and teaching of a topic as part of training for an occupation or profession, assign headings of the type [topic]--Study and teaching or [...] education and [class of persons]--Training of, if appropriate. For instructions on assigning the subdivision --Training of, see H 2217.

### 5. Places

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Study and teaching (May Subd Geog) under names of places for general works on study and teaching about these places.

Phrase headings of the type [...] studies for area studies, for example, African studies, were discontinued in 1977 and should be changed when encountered on bibliographic records.

For works on study and teaching about a place published in that place, omit the geographical subdivision under --Study and teaching. Example:

```
Title: Understanding the United States : a guide for American teachers.
651 #0 $a United States $x Study and teaching (Secondary)
```

For works on study and teaching about a country for use in a locality of the same country or for use in another country, subdivide --Study and teaching by the place in which the study and teaching is to be carried out. Examples:

```
Title: Helping Chinese students to understand the United States.
651 #0 $a United States $x Study and teaching (Secondary) $z China.

Title: Studying the United States : a guide for social studies teachers in Boston.
651 #0 $a United States $x Study and teaching (Secondary) $z Massachusetts $z Boston.
```

For works on a special aspect of study and teaching about a place, interpose a topical subdivision between the place and the subdivision --Study and teaching, for example, Sweden--Social life and customs--Study and teaching.

Do not interpose place between a topical heading and the subdivision --Study and teaching.

### 6. Languages

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Study and teaching (May Subd Geog) under individual languages and groups of languages to general works on the study and teaching of these languages.

Add a parenthetical qualifier to indicate level of study, if appropriate, for example, English language--Study and teaching (Continuing education).

In accordance with H 1154, subdivide headings of the type [language or language group]--Study and teaching by special topics, if appropriate, to bring out particular aspects of the study and teaching of these languages, for example, Spanish language--Study and teaching--Foreign speakers.

### 7. Sacred works

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Study and teaching (May Subd Geog) under uniform titles for sacred works to works on how to study and teach these sacred works, including their individual parts, for example, Book of Mormon--Study and teaching.

Note: Prior to 1991, the subdivision --Study was used under individual sacred works. That subdivision is now obsolete.

### 8. Music

The free-floating subdivision --Instruction and study (May Subd Geog) is used instead of --Study and teaching in the field of music. For guidelines on assigning this subdivision, see H 1160 and H 1161.

### 9. Audiovisual materials

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Study and teaching--Audio-visual aids under topics to works on aids to the learning and teaching of a subject.

To works on audiovisual materials about a topic that are not specifically designed for educational purposes, assign the subdivision --Audio-visual aids directly under the topic.

For guidelines on assigning headings to catalogs of audiovisual materials, see H 1361, sec. 3.

### 10. Simulation methods

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Study and teaching--Simulation methods under topics to works on the use of simulation methods for instruction in a specific field.

To works on the use of a particular simulation method, assign an additional heading for the method, for example, Simulation games in education.

### 11. Activity programs

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Study and teaching--Activity programs (May Subd Geog) under topics to works on activities to be engaged in for the purpose of acquiring certain skills, concepts, or knowledge.

### 12. Textbooks

Do not assign headings of the type [topic]--Study and teaching to textbooks for students. For guidelines on assigning headings to textbooks, see H 2187.
