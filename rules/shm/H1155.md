# H 1155 Pattern Headings: Legislative Bodies

## Validation rules

1. Subdivisions on this list are free-floating under names of individual legislative bodies, including individual chambers, established as corporate bodies in the name authority file (e.g. *Great Britain. Parliament*; *California. Legislature. Assembly*; *Germany (West). Bundestag*; *Germany. Reichstag*). (H 1155, Types of headings covered)
2. Do not use this list free-floating under the topical heading *Legislative bodies*. (H 1155, Types of headings covered)
3. Subdivisions from H 1105 (individual corporate bodies) are also free-floating under individual legislative bodies where there is no conflict. (H 1155, Relation to H 1105)
4. A listed subdivision may be used free-floating if appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. (H 1155, Conflicts)
5. No subdivision on this list is marked (May Subd Geog). (H 1155, list)
6. `--Alabama [Illinois, Texas, etc.] delegation` is a multiple subdivision: the bracketed names are examples of the names that may be substituted (e.g. Alabama, Illinois, Texas). (H 1155, list)
7. `--Elections, [date]` requires a date formed according to H 1078. (H 1155, note 3)
8. `--Cloture`, `--Majority leaders`, `--Majority whips`, `--Minority leaders`, `--Minority whips`, and `--Presiding officers` are established under *United States. Congress. Senate* and may be used under other legislative bodies where appropriate. (H 1155, note 1)
9. `--Speakers` is established under *United States. Congress. House* and may be used under other legislative bodies where appropriate. (H 1155, note 5)
10. `--Deputy speakers` and `--Dissolution` are not established under *United States. Congress* but may be used under other legislative bodies where appropriate. (H 1155, note 2)
11. `--Deputy speakers`, `--Majority leaders`, `--Majority whips`, `--Minority leaders`, `--Minority whips`, `--Officials and employees`, `--Presiding officers`, and `--Speakers` may be further subdivided by subdivisions for classes of persons (H 1100). (H 1155, note 4)
12. Form subdivisions on this list ($v `--Private bills`, `--Resolutions`, `--Committees--Indexes`, `--Committees--Rules and practice`) are valid LCSH; most may also be used as $x for works about the form. (H 1155, Note; H 1075 sec. 1.d)
13. [LC practice] If an exceptional variant form is retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form (H 195); otherwise propose a change (H 193). (H 1155, Conflicts)

## Full text

June 2013

PATTERN: *United States. Congress*

### Types of headings covered by the pattern

Names of individual legislative bodies, including their individual chambers, established as corporate bodies in the name authority file. Examples: *Great Britain. Parliament*; *California. Legislature. Assembly*; *Germany (West). Bundestag*; *Germany. Reichstag*. The category does not include the heading *Legislative bodies*.

### Relation to H 1105

Subdivisions on the list of free-floating subdivisions used under names of individual corporate bodies, H 1105, are also free-floating under individual legislative bodies where there is no conflict. This list contains additional subdivisions appropriate for legislative bodies.

### Conflicts

Any subdivision listed here can be used as a free-floating subdivision under any heading belonging to the category if it is appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. Subject authority records may exist for headings employing variant phrases or subdivisions equivalent to subdivisions on this list.

LC practice: If an exceptional variant form is to be retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form following the procedures in H 195 if the reference does not yet exist. Otherwise, submit a proposal to change the variant form along with all bibliographic records requiring correction following the procedures in H 193.

Note: Most form subdivisions coded $v in this list may also be used as topical subdivisions coded $x when assigned to works about the form (see H 1075, sec. 1.d.).
