# H 1148 Pattern Headings: Art

## Validation rules

1. Subdivisions on this list are free-floating under headings for individual art forms in the fine arts and for art and art forms qualified by nationality, region, ethnic group, or time period (e.g. *Drawing*; *Art, French*; *Painting, German*; *Iron sculpture, Japanese*; *Sculpture, Southeast Asian*; *Arts, Māori*; *Hispanic American art*; *Landscape painting, Medieval*; *Sculpture, Modern*). (H 1148 sec. B)
2. The category does not include the heading *Art*, headings qualified by religion (e.g. *Buddhist art*), or headings for the decorative arts (e.g. *Pottery*), performing arts (e.g. *Theater*), or architecture (e.g. *Architecture, Modern*); list subdivisions are usable under these only if allowed by other instruction sheets or established editorially. (H 1148 sec. C)
3. A listed subdivision may be used free-floating under any heading in the category if appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. (H 1148 sec. D)
4. Period subdivisions on this list are not free-floating under unqualified headings for art forms (e.g. *Drawing*) nor under headings for art forms qualified by time period (e.g. *Sculpture, Modern*); they require a national, regional, or ethnic qualifier. (H 1148 sec. F)
5. General period subdivisions `--10th century` through `--21st century` (`--10th century`, `--11th century`, ... `--20th century`, `--21st century`) may be used under art and art forms of all nations, regions, and ethnic groups except China, Japan, and Korea. (H 1148 sec. F.1)
6. Under art forms qualified by *Chinese*, use only: `--To 221 B.C.`; `--Qin-Han dynasties, 221 B.C.-220 A.D.`; `--Three kingdoms-Sui dynasty, 220-618`; `--Tang-Five dynasties, 618-960`; `--Song-Yuan dynasties, 960-1368`; `--Ming-Qing dynasties, 1368-1912`; `--20th century`; `--21st century`. (H 1148 sec. F.2)
7. Under art forms qualified by *Japanese*, use only: `--To 794`; `--To 1600`; `--To 1868`; `--Heian period, 794-1185`; `--Kamakura-Momoyama periods, 1185-1600`; `--Edo period, 1600-1868`; `--19th century`; `--1868-`; `--Meiji period, 1868-1912`; `--20th century`; `--Taishō period, 1912-1926`; `--21st century`. (H 1148 sec. F.3)
8. Under art forms qualified by *Korean*, use only: `--To 935`; `--To 1900`; `--Koryŏ period, 935-1392`; `--Chosŏn dynasty, 1392-1910`; `--19th century`; `--20th century`; `--21st century`. (H 1148 sec. F.4)
9. If a general period subdivision is inappropriate for a specific heading, the appropriate subdivision must be established under that specific heading (not free-floating). (H 1148 sec. F.1)
10. Topical and form subdivisions on this list may follow free-floating or established period subdivisions (e.g. *Painting, French--19th century--Themes, motives*), except as noted. (H 1148 sec. G)
11. `--Foreign influences` is not valid after a period subdivision. (H 1148 sec. G)
12. Additional subdivisions may be used from the general free-floating list in H 1095. (H 1148 sec. G)
13. Only `--Appreciation`, `--Conservation and restoration`, `--Expertising`, and `--Forgeries` on this list may be subdivided geographically. (H 1148 sec. G)
14. Form subdivisions on this list (`--Catalogs` $v, `--Chronology` $v) remain valid LCSH. (H 1148 sec. H)
15. [LC practice] LC catalogers no longer use form subdivisions; they assign LCGFT terms instead. (H 1148 sec. H)
16. [LC practice] Establish new subdivisions under *Art, Italian* whenever possible; establish subdivisions appropriate only to Chinese, Japanese, or Korean art under *Art, Chinese*; *Art, Japanese*; or *Art, Korean*. (H 1148 sec. E)
17. [LC practice] If an exceptional variant form is retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form (H 195); otherwise propose a change (H 193). (H 1148 sec. D)

## Full text

### A. Recent Revisions

2026-04: Updated template. Added instructions related to LCGFT implementation.

### B. Types of headings covered

Headings for individual art forms in the fine arts as well as art and art forms qualified by nationality, region, ethnic group, or time period.

Examples:

```
Drawing
Art, French
Painting, German
Iron sculpture, Japanese
Sculpture, Southeast Asian
Arts, Māori
Hispanic American art
Landscape painting, Medieval
Sculpture, Modern
```

### C. Types of headings not covered

The category does not include the heading *Art*, headings qualified by religion, nor headings for the decorative arts, performing arts, or architecture. The subdivisions in this list may be used for these categories if allowed by other instructions sheets or established editorially as appropriate to the topic.

Examples:

```
Buddhist art
Pottery
Theater
Architecture, Modern
```

### D. Conflicts

Any subdivision listed here can be used as a free-floating subdivision under any heading belonging to the category if it is appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file.

Subject authority records may exist for headings employing variant phrases or subdivisions equivalent to subdivisions on this list.

LC practice: If an exceptional variant form is to be retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form following the procedures in H 195 if the reference does not yet exist. Otherwise, submit a proposal to change the variant form along with all bibliographic records requiring correction following the procedures in H 193.

### E. New subdivisions

Establish new subdivisions under *Art, Italian* whenever possible. Establish subdivisions appropriate only to the art of China, Japan, or Korea under *Art, Chinese*; *Art, Japanese*; or *Art, Korean*.

### H. List of form subdivisions

LC practice: In 2026, the use of form subdivisions was phased out. Library of Congress catalogers assign Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT) to bring out the form(s) and/or genre(s) of the resource being cataloged. Therefore, the form subdivisions maintained on this list are not available for use by Library of Congress catalogers. Catalogers at institutions external to LC may use them as dictated by their own institutional policies.

| Subdivision | May Subd Geog | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `$v Catalogs` |  | Use as a form subdivision under types of objects, including types of merchandise, art objects, products, publications, collectors' items, technical equipment, etc., for listings of those objects that have been produced, that are available or located at particular places, or that occur on a particular market, often systematically arranged with descriptive details, prices, etc., accompanying each entry. |
| `$v Chronology` |  | Use as a form subdivision under headings for art for lists of dates of events pertinent to the subject in order of their occurrence. |
