# H 1155.6 Pattern Headings: Literary Works Entered Under Author

## Validation rules

1. Subdivisions on this list are free-floating under uniform titles of literary works, or parts of works, entered under author (author-title headings), e.g. *Dante Alighieri, 1265-1321. Divina commedia*; *Goethe, Johann Wolfgang von, 1749-1832. Faust. 1. Theil*. (H 1155.6 sec. B)
2. Literary works entered directly under title (anonymous, multi-authored, no author designation) take the H 1155.8 list instead. (H 1155.6 sec. C)
3. A listed subdivision may be used free-floating if appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. (H 1155.6 sec. D)
4. H 1095 subdivisions authorized under subjects in general (e.g. `--Bibliography`, `--Exhibitions`) or under individual works (e.g. `--Concordances`, `--Sources`) are also free-floating under literary works. (H 1155.6 sec. E)
5. For general criticism and interpretation of a work, use the unsubdivided author-title heading *[author]. [title]*; do not add a subdivision such as `--Criticism and interpretation`. (H 1155.6 sec. E)
6. To bring out other aspects of literary analysis of an individual work (e.g. characters), assign an additional heading for the author with the appropriate subdivision (H 1110), e.g. `600 10 $a Shakespeare, William, $d 1564-1616. $t Hamlet.` plus `600 10 $a Shakespeare, William, $d 1564-1616 $x Characters.` plus `650 #0 $a Women in literature.` (H 1155.6 sec. E)
7. Topical subdivisions on this list: `--Criticism, Textual`; `--Illustrations` ($x, for works about pictorial representation of the work). Neither may be subdivided geographically. (H 1155.6 sec. F)
8. Form subdivisions on this list (valid LCSH): `--Illustrations` ($v, collections of pictorial representations of the work); `--Pictorial works` ($v, pictures pertaining to the work as a physical object or, for dramatic works, to productions). (H 1155.6 sec. G)
9. [LC practice] LC catalogers no longer use form subdivisions; they assign LCGFT terms instead. (H 1155.6 sec. G)
10. [LC practice] If an exceptional variant form is retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form (H 195); otherwise propose a change (H 193). (H 1155.6 sec. D)

### A. Recent revisions

2025-11: Updated template. Clarified text. Separated general and form subdivisions. Added instructions related to LCGFT implementation.

### B. Types of headings covered by the pattern

Uniform titles of literary works, or parts of a work, that are entered under author.

Examples:

```
Dante Alighieri, 1265-1321. Divina commedia
Tolstoy, Leo, graf, 1828-1910. Voĭna i mir
Goethe, Johann Wolfgang von, 1749-1832. Faust. 1. Theil
Hemingway, Ernest, 1899-1961. Sun also rises.
```

### C. Types of headings not covered by the pattern

For subdivisions used under literary works entered directly under title, with no author designation, including anonymous and multi-authored works, see H 1155.8.

### D. Conflicts

Any subdivision listed here can be used as a free-floating subdivision under any heading belonging to the category if it is appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. Subject authority records may exist for headings employing variant phrases or subdivisions equivalent to subdivisions on this list.

LC practice: If an exceptional variant form is to be retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form following the procedures in H 195 if the reference does not yet exist. Otherwise, submit a proposal to change the variant form along with all bibliographic records requiring correction following the procedures in H 193.

### E. Special provisions

The subdivisions listed in H 1095 are authorized for use under subjects in general, for example, *--Bibliography* or *--Exhibitions*, or under individual works (author-title or title entries), for example, *--Concordances* or *--Sources*, are also free-floating under literary works. The subdivisions used under literary works entered under author are closely related to the subdivisions used under names of individual literary authors (H 1110). For general criticism and interpretation of a work, assign the unsubdivided heading: [author]. [title]. To bring out any other aspect of literary analysis pertaining to an individual work, assign an additional heading for the author with the appropriate subdivision, along with companion headings, if any, as indicated elsewhere in this manual.

Example:

```
600 10 $a Shakespeare, William, $d 1564-1616. $t Hamlet.
600 10 $a Shakespeare, William, $d 1564-1616 $x Characters.
650 #0 $a Women in literature.
```

### G. List of form subdivisions

LC practice: As stated in H 1075, Library of Congress catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT) to bring out the form(s) and/or genre(s) of the resource being cataloged. The form subdivisions maintained on this list are not available for use by LC catalogers. Catalogers at institutions external to LC may use them as dictated by their own institutional policies.

| Subdivision | May Subd Geog | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `$v Illustrations` |  | Use under individual literary works entered under author for collections of pictorial representations of those works. |
| `$v Pictorial works` |  | Use under individual literary works entered under author for works consisting of pictures or other visual images pertaining to the work as a physical object or, in the case of dramatic works, to productions of the work. |
