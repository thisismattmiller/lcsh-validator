# H 2060 Societies

## Validation rules

1. `--Societies, etc.` (exact form, with comma and "etc.") is a free-floating topical (`$x`) subdivision under subjects, including topics, places, wars, persons, and corporate bodies, e.g. `Chemistry--Societies, etc.`, `Bolívar, Simón, 1783-1830--Societies, etc.`, `Catholic Church--Italy--Societies, etc.`. (H 2060 sec. 1)
2. Do not use `--Societies, etc.` under a class of persons if the corresponding discipline heading can be used: `Engineering--Societies, etc.`, not `Engineers--Societies, etc.`. (H 2060 sec. 1)
3. Phrase headings for societies in a discipline have been replaced by `[discipline]--Societies, etc.`. (H 2060 background)
4. The subdivision `--Societies, periodicals, etc.` (formerly under names of persons) is discontinued; use `--Societies, etc.` and `--Periodicals`. (H 2060 background)
5. Do not use `--Societies, etc.` merely to show that a serial is issued by a society. (H 2060 background)
6. `--Societies and clubs` is a free-floating topical subdivision under headings for age and sex groups, including qualified ones, e.g. `Boys--Societies and clubs`, `Women--Societies and clubs`, `Rural women--Societies and clubs`, `Minority older people--Societies and clubs`. (H 2060 sec. 2)
7. `--Societies, etc.` and `--Societies and clubs` may be followed by any appropriate form subdivision, e.g. `--Bibliography`, `--Directories`. (H 2060 sec. 3)
8. Societies in a place: `Associations, institutions, etc.--[place]` (general), `Learned institutions and societies--[place]`, `Clubs--[place]`. (H 2060 sec. 4)
9. Serials issued by a society: business information only takes the society's name (with `--Periodicals` if appropriate); topical only takes the topic heading(s); both takes both. (H 2060 sec. 5)

## Full text

BACKGROUND: Past practice in subject cataloging provided several methods to show that a work discussed societies related to a particular subject or to show that a serial was published by a society. Phrase headings for societies in a particular discipline have been replaced by headings using the subdivision --Societies, etc. The subdivision --Societies, periodicals, etc., previously used under names of persons was replaced by the subdivisions --Societies, etc. and --Periodicals. Also discontinued is the practice of using the subdivision --Societies, etc. to show that a serial publication is issued by a society and contains information about the society.

For classification of society publications, see F 225.

### 1. --Societies, etc.

Use the subdivision --Societies, etc. as a free-floating topical subdivision under subjects for works discussing two or more societies or institutions related to those subjects. Examples:

```
650 #0 $a African Americans $z Massachusetts $x Societies, etc.
650 #0 $a Chemistry $x Societies, etc.
650 #0 $a Religious literature $x Publishing $x Societies, etc.
651 #0 $a United States $x History $y Civil War, 1861-1865 $x Societies, etc.
600 10 $a Bolívar, Simón, $d 1783-1830 $x Societies, etc.
610 20 $a Catholic Church $z Italy $x Societies, etc.
```

Do not use the subdivision under headings for classes of persons, if the corresponding heading for the discipline can be assigned. Example:

```
650 #0 $a Engineering $x Societies, etc.
  [not 650 #0 $a Engineers $x Societies, etc.]
```

### 2. --Societies and clubs

Use the subdivision --Societies and clubs as a free-floating topical subdivision under headings for age and sex groups for works on organizations devoted to these groups or whose membership is drawn from these groups. Such works may discuss methods of establishing and conducting these organizations or may discuss specific organizations collectively. Use the subdivision also under qualified headings for age and sex groups. Examples:

```
650 #0 $a Boys $x Societies and clubs.
650 #0 $a Women $x Societies and clubs.
650 #0 $a Rural women $x Societies and clubs.
650 #0 $a Jewish women $x Societies and clubs.
650 #0 $a Older people $x Societies and clubs.
650 #0 $a Minority older people $x Societies and clubs.
```

### 3. Further subdivision by form subdivision

The subdivisions --Societies, etc. and --Societies and clubs may be further subdivided by any appropriate form subdivision such as --Bibliography or --Directories.

### 4. Societies or clubs in a particular place

For general works on the societies and institutions in a particular place assign the heading Associations, institutions, etc.--[place]. For works discussing collectively the learned institutions or societies of a particular place assign Learned institutions and societies--[place]. For works discussing collectively the clubs of a particular place assign Clubs--[place].

### 5. Society publications

Individual serial publications issued by a society may contain information on business matters of the society and/or information on the topic in which the society specializes. Assign headings to these works according to the type of information contained.

#### a. Business information only

Assign a heading for the name of the society, with the subdivision --Periodicals if appropriate.

#### b. Topical information only

Assign the heading or headings for the topic, with the subdivision --Periodicals if appropriate.

#### c. Both business and topical information

Assign headings for both the society and the topic, with the subdivision --Periodicals if appropriate.
