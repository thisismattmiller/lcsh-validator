# H 2227 Treaties

## Validation rules

1. Individual treaties and concordats may be assigned as subjects only in the form established in AACR2 or RDA form in the name authority file; pre-AACR2 treaty headings in the subject authority file are invalid and are cancelled as encountered. (H 2227 background)
2. Name-authority treaty headings include the date, e.g. `Treaty for the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons in Latin America (1967)`, `International Convention for Safe Containers (1972)`, `Treaty on Principles Governing the Activities of States in the Exploration and Use of Outer Space, Including the Moon and Other Celestial Bodies (1967 January 27)`, or `Great Britain. Treaties, etc. United States, 1814 Dec. 24`. (H 2227 sec. 2.b examples)
3. General (non-topical) collections of treaty texts take general form headings such as `Treaties--Collections`, `Peace treaties`, `[place]--Foreign relations--Treaties`. (H 2227 sec. 1.a)
4. The subdivision `--Treaties` is used only under names of ethnic groups (e.g. `Potawatomi Indians--Treaties`, `Choctaw Indians--Treaties`), names of wars (e.g. `World War, 1939-1945--Treaties`), `Catholic Church--Foreign relations`, and headings of the type `[place]--Foreign relations`. (H 2227 sec. 1.b.1 note)
5. Do not use `--Treaties` after `[place]--Foreign relations--[place]` (invalid: `Soviet Union--Foreign relations--United States--Treaties`). (H 2227 sec. 1.b.1 note)
6. A period subdivision may intervene: `Japan--Foreign relations--1945-1989--Treaties`. (H 2227 sec. 2.a example)
7. Commercial treaties of a place take `[place]--Commercial treaties`, e.g. `European Economic Community countries--Commercial treaties`. (H 2227 sec. 1.b.2, 2.a)
8. Topical collections presenting a country's treaties: a general heading without local subdivision plus the same heading with the country as geographic subdivision (e.g. `Telecommunication--Law and legislation` and `Telecommunication--Law and legislation--United States`); for collections limited to two countries, or individual bilateral treaties, omit the general heading and assign one heading per party. (H 2227 sec. 1.b.2, 1.c.2)
9. Do not assign the heading for the name of the treaty to a work that consists solely of the text of the treaty; assign it to works that discuss the treaty, together with topical headings. (H 2227 sec. 1.c.1, 2.b)
10. Works discussing various treaties of a region or country take `[place]--Foreign relations--Treaties` or `[place]--Commercial treaties`. (H 2227 sec. 2.a)

## Full text

BACKGROUND: Before the adoption of AACR2 in 1981, many individual treaties and concordats were established in the subject authority file when they were needed as subject headings. Since 1981, only treaties or concordats established in AACR2 or RDA form in the name authority file may be validly assigned as subject headings. Most pre-AACR2 treaty headings have already been deleted from the subject authority file. Remaining headings of this type are cancelled as they are encountered, and are re-established in the name authority file.

### 1. Texts of treaties

#### a. General (non-topical) collections

Assign the appropriate general form heading, for example, Treaties--Collections; Peace treaties; [place]--Foreign relations--Treaties.

#### b. Topical collections

(1) Topical headings. Assign the appropriate topical heading(s), for example, Environmental law, International; Chemical warfare (International law); Postal conventions; Commercial treaties; Sex discrimination against women--Law and legislation; Choctaw Indians--Treaties; World War, 1939-1945--Treaties.

Note: The subdivision --Treaties is used only under names of ethnic groups, under names of wars, and under Catholic Church--Foreign relations and headings of the type [place]--Foreign relations. It is not used as a further subdivision after headings of the type [place]--Foreign relations--[place].

Examples:

```
Title: Labour law ratified conventions.
650 #0 $a Labor laws and legislation, International.

Title: Treaties between the Potawatomi tribe of Indians and the United States of America, 1789-1867.
650 #0 $a Potawatomi Indians $v Treaties.
```

(2) Geographic subdivision. If the purpose of the collection is to present the treaties to which a particular country is a party, assign a general heading, without local subdivision, to bring out the international aspect, and assign the same heading with local subdivision to bring out the country (except for collections of commercial treaties, for which the heading [country]--Commercial treaties is used as the second heading). Example:

```
Title: United States treaties and other international agreements pertaining to telecommunications.
650 #0 $a Telecommunication $x Law and legislation.
650 #0 $a Telecommunication $x Law and legislation $z United States.
```

Exception: If the collection is limited to only two countries, omit the general heading, and bring out both countries using local subdivision. Example:

```
Title: Trade in cotton textiles : agreements between the United States and Poland.
650 #0 $a Cotton textiles $x Law and legislation $z United States.
650 #0 $a Cotton textiles $x Law and legislation $z Poland.
```

#### c. Individual treaties

(1) General rule. Assign the appropriate topical heading(s) using the same types of headings as for topical collections.

Do not assign the heading for the name of the treaty itself to a work that consists solely of the text of the treaty.

Examples:

```
Title: Treaty to resolve pending boundary differences ... between the United States and Mexico, signed at Mexico Nov. 12, 1970.
651 #0 $a United States $x Boundaries $z Mexico.
651 #0 $a Mexico $x Boundaries $z United States.

Title: Treaty of peace with Turkey.
650 #0 $a World War, 1914-1918 $x Territorial questions $z Armenia.
650 #0 $a World War, 1914-1918 $x Territorial questions $z Turkey.

Title: The Egyptian-Israeli Peace Treaty, March 26, 1979.
650 #0 $a Israel-Arab War, 1973 $x Peace.

Title: International coffee agreement, 1976.
650 #0 $a Coffee $x Law and legislation.
```

(2) Bilateral treaties. In the case of individual bilateral agreements, assign duplicate headings to bring out each party, and omit the unsubdivided general heading. Example:

```
Title: Air transport services; agreement between the United States of America and Brazil.
650 #0 $a Aeronautics, Commercial $x Law and legislation $z United States.
650 #0 $a Aeronautics, Commercial $x Law and legislation $z Brazil.
```

### 2. Works about treaties

#### a. Collective

To works that discuss various treaties to which a region or country is a party, assign the heading [place]--Foreign relations--Treaties or [place]--Commercial treaties, as well as other topical headings appropriate to the work. Examples:

```
Title: Theory, law, and policy of contemporary Japanese treaties.
651 #0 $a Japan $x Foreign relations $y 1945-1989 $x Treaties.

Title: The long pretense: Soviet treaty diplomacy from Lenin to Gorbachev.
651 #0 $a Soviet Union $x Foreign relations $x Treaties.
651 #0 $a Soviet Union $x Foreign relations $z United States.
651 #0 $a United States $x Foreign relations $z Soviet Union.

Title: The free trade agreements among the EC countries: their implementation and interpretation.
651 #0 $a European Economic Community countries $x Commercial treaties.
650 #0 $a Free trade $z European Economic Community countries.
```

#### b. Individual treaties

Assign the heading for the name of a treaty or international convention to a work that discusses the treaty. Also assign topical headings appropriate to the work being cataloged. Examples:

```
Title: A fresh view on the Outer Space Treaty.
630 00 $a Treaty on Principles Governing the Activities of States in the Exploration and Use of Outer Space, Including the Moon and Other Celestial Bodies $d (1967 January 27)
650 #0 $a Space law $x International cooperation.
650 #0 $a Outer space $x Exploration $x International cooperation.

Title: The diplomatic history of the Treaty of Tlatelolco.
630 00 $a Treaty for the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons in Latin America $d (1967)
650 #0 $a Nuclear-weapon-free zones $z Latin America.

Title: Commentary on the international convention for safe containers.
630 00 $a International Convention for Safe Containers $d (1972)
650 #0 $a Containers $x Law and legislation.

Title: Northern boundary of the United States according to the Treaty of Ghent.
610 10 $a Great Britain. $t Treaties, etc. $g United States. $d 1814 Dec. 24.
651 #0 $a United States $x Boundaries $z Canada.
651 #0 $a Canada $x Boundaries $z United States.

Title: At the crossroads between peace and war : the London naval conference of 1930.
630 00 $a International Treaty for the Limitation and Reduction of Naval Armament $d (1930 April 22)
650 #0 $a Disarmament $x History $y 20th century.
650 #0 $a Sea-power $x History $y 20th century.

Title: Data almizrano-- : siete siglos y medio de historiografía Valenciana sobre el Tratado de Almizra, 1244-1994.
610 10 $a Castile (Spain) $t Treaties, etc. $g Aragon (Spain), $d 1244 Mar. 26 $x Historiography.
651 #0 $a Castile (Spain) $x History $y Ferdinand III, 1217-1252 $x Historiography.
651 #0 $a Aragon (Spain) $x History $y James I, 1213-1276 $x Historiography.
```
