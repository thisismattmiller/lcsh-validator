# H 1155.8 Pattern Headings: Literary Works Entered Under Title

## Validation rules

1. Subdivisions on this list are free-floating under uniform titles of literary works entered directly under title (e.g. *Arabian nights*; *Chanson de Roland*; *Charlemagne (Play)*; *Guillaume (Chanson de geste)*). (H 1155.8 sec. B)
2. Do not use this list under anonymous sacred classics (e.g. *Bible*; *Qur’an*; *Vedas*); use H 1188. Literary works entered under author use H 1155.6. (H 1155.8 sec. C)
3. A listed subdivision may be used free-floating if appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. (H 1155.8 sec. D)
4. H 1095 subdivisions authorized under subjects in general (e.g. `--Bibliography`, `--Exhibitions`) or under individual works (e.g. `--Concordances`, `--Sources`) are also free-floating under literary works. (H 1155.8 sec. E)
5. Do not use `--Criticism and interpretation` under literary works entered under title; for general criticism, or a discussion combining several subdivision approaches, use the uniform title without subdivision. (H 1155.8 sec. E)
6. Only `--Appreciation` on this list may be subdivided geographically. (H 1155.8 sec. F)
7. Do not use `--Film and video adaptations`; use `--Film adaptations` and/or `--Television adaptations`. (H 1155.8 sec. F-G)
8. `--Translations into French, [German, etc.]--History and criticism` takes a language name in place of the bracketed examples. (H 1155.8 sec. F)
9. `--Translations` ($v) is used for collections of translated works; do not use it for single translations. (H 1155.8 sec. G)
10. Form subdivisions on this list (valid LCSH): `--Adaptations`, `--Film adaptations`, `--Illustrations`, `--Language--Glossaries, etc.`, `--Manuscripts--Facsimiles`, `--Parodies, imitations, etc.`, `--Television adaptations`, `--Translations`. (H 1155.8 sec. G)
11. [LC practice] LC catalogers no longer use form subdivisions; they assign LCGFT terms instead. (H 1155.8 sec. G)
12. [LC practice] If an exceptional variant form is retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form (H 195); otherwise propose a change (H 193). (H 1155.8 sec. D)

## Full text

Pattern: *Beowulf*

### A. Recent revisions

2026-01: Updated template. Clarified text. Separated general and form subdivisions. Added instructions related to LCGFT implementation.

### B. Types of headings covered by the pattern

Headings for literary works entered directly under uniform title.

Examples:

```
Arabian nights
Chanson de Roland
Charlemagne (Play)
Guillaume (Chanson de geste)
```

### C. Types of headings not covered by the pattern

The subject cataloging of anonymous sacred classics, for example, *Bible*; *Qur’an*; *Vedas*, differs substantially and should not be confused with the treatment of the literary works discussed here. For free-floating subdivisions used under the uniform titles of sacred works, see H 1188. For subdivisions used under literary works entered under author, see H 1155.6.

### D. Conflicts

Any subdivision listed here can be used as a free-floating subdivision under any heading belonging to the category if it is appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. Subject authority records may exist for headings employing variant phrases or subdivisions equivalent to subdivisions on this list.

LC practice: If an exceptional variant form is to be retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form following the procedures in H 195 if the reference does not yet exist. Otherwise, submit a proposal to change the variant form along with all bibliographic records requiring correction following the procedures in H 193.

### E. Special provisions

The subdivisions listed in H 1095 as authorized for use under subjects in general, for example, *--Bibliography* or *--Exhibitions*, or under individual works (author-title or title entries), for example, *--Concordances* or *--Sources*, are also free-floating under literary works. The additional subdivisions listed below follow the usage given in the individual literary authors list (H 1110). Do not use the subdivision *--Criticism and interpretation* under entries for literary works. For a work of general criticism and interpretation or a discussion combining the approaches of several subdivisions, assign as a subject heading the uniform title of the literary work without subdivision.

### G. List of form subdivisions

LC practice: As stated in H 1075, Library of Congress catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT) to bring out the form(s) and/or genre(s) of the resource being cataloged. The form subdivisions maintained on this list are not available for use by LC catalogers. Catalogers at institutions external to LC may use them as dictated by their own institutional policies.
