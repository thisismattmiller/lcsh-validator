# H 1969.5 Radio Genre/Form Terms

## Validation rules

1. This sheet contains no subject heading construction rules; genre/form terms for radio programs are governed by the LCGFT Manual (J 270), not LCSH. (H 1969.5 sec. A)

## Full text

### A. Background

This instruction sheet provided special guidelines for the assignment of genre/form terms to radio programs, or to portions of radio programs, that are cataloged by the Library of Congress. This information is now available in the Library of Congress Genre/Form Manual (LCGFT) instruction sheet J 270 Radio Programs and Podcasts.

Guidelines for establishing new genre/form terms were included in this instruction sheet. This information can be found in the LCGFT Manual in instruction sheets J 120 When to Establish a New Genre/Form Term, J 140 Preparation of Genre/Form Term Proposals, and J 160 Authority Research and Citations for Genre/Form Term Proposals.

For guidelines on assigning subject headings to bring out the content of individual radio programs, consult the appropriate instruction sheet(s).

### B. Recent revisions

2026-02: Updated template. Removed content that is available in the LCGFT Manual.
