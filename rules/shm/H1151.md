# H 1151 Pattern Headings: Individual Educational Institutions

## Validation rules

1. Subdivisions on this list are free-floating under names of individual educational institutions at all levels and of all types, including systems, established as corporate bodies in the name authority file (e.g. *Anne Arundel Community College*; *Muleshoe Junior High School*; *Union Theological Seminary (New York, N.Y.)*; *University of California (System)*). (H 1151, Types of headings covered)
2. Do not use this list under headings for types of educational institutions (e.g. *Universities and colleges*); those follow H 1151.5. (H 1151, Types of headings covered)
3. Subdivisions from the H 1105 list for individual corporate bodies are also free-floating under individual educational institutions where there is no conflict. (H 1151, Relation to H 1105)
4. A listed subdivision may be used free-floating if appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. (H 1151, Conflicts)
5. Only `--Alumni and alumnae` on this list is marked (May Subd Geog). (H 1151, list)
6. `--Alumni and alumnae`, `--Benefactors`, `--Employees`, `--Faculty`, `--Foreign students`, `--Freshmen`, `--Graduate students`, `--Professional staff`, and `--Students` may be further subdivided by the subdivisions for classes of persons in H 1100. (H 1151, note 1)
7. `--Riot, [date]` and `--Student strike, [date]` require a date in place of [date], formed according to H 1078. (H 1151, note 2)
8. `--Entrance requirements--[subject]` and `--Examinations--[subject]` take the name of a subject in place of [subject]. (H 1151, list)
9. Form subdivisions on this list (`--Aerial views`, `--Regulations`, `--Curricula--Catalogs`, `--Dissertations--Style manuals`, `--Students--Yearbooks`) are coded $v; most may also be used as $x for works about the form. (H 1151, Note; H 1075 sec. 1.d)
10. [LC practice] If an exceptional variant form is retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form (H 195); otherwise propose a change (H 193). (H 1151, Conflicts)

## Full text

September 2013

PATTERN: *Harvard University*

### Types of headings covered by the pattern

Headings for names of individual educational institutions at all levels and of all types, including systems, which are established as corporate bodies in the name authority file. Examples: *Anne Arundel Community College*; *Muleshoe Junior High School*; *Medical University of South Carolina*; *Union Theological Seminary (New York, N.Y.)*; *University of California (System)*. The category does not include headings for types of educational institutions, which are covered by H 1151.5.

### Relation to H 1105

Subdivisions on the list of free-floating subdivisions used under names of individual corporate bodies, H 1105, are also free-floating under individual educational institutions where there is no conflict. This list contains additional subdivisions appropriate for educational institutions.

### Conflicts

Any subdivision listed here can be used as a free-floating subdivision under any heading belonging to this category if it is appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. Subject authority records may exist for headings employing variant phrases or subdivisions equivalent to subdivisions on this list.

LC practice: If an exceptional variant form is to be retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form following the procedures in H 195 if the reference does not yet exist. Otherwise, submit a proposal to change the variant form along with all bibliographic records requiring correction following the procedures in H 193.

Note: Most form subdivisions coded $v in this list may also be used as topical subdivisions coded $x when assigned to works about the form (see H 1075, sec. 1.d.).
