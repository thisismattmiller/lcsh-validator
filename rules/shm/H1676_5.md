# H 1676.5 Inspection

## Validation rules

1. `--Inspection` (May Subd Geog) is free-floating under types of merchandise and products (fitness for marketing), e.g. `Lumber--Inspection`. (H 1676.5 sec. 1)
2. `--Inspection` (May Subd Geog) is free-floating under types of machines, engineering structures, and buildings (compliance with safety regulations); geographic subdivision follows it, e.g. `Automobiles--Inspection--Virginia`. (H 1676.5 sec. 2)
3. `--Inspection` (May Subd Geog) is free-floating under individual plants and groups of plants and domestic animals (diseases or pests), e.g. `Cattle--Inspection`, `Corn--Inspection`, `Poultry--Inspection`. (H 1676.5 sec. 3)
4. `--Inspection` is free-floating under names of individual military services (state of readiness), e.g. `United States. Army--Inspection`. (H 1676.5 sec. 4)
5. Do not use `--Inspection` under a topic if a phrase heading for the concept is established, e.g. use the established phrase headings `Factory inspection` and `Mine inspection`; such headings carry a reference from the form `[topic]--Inspection`. (H 1676.5 sec. 5)

## Full text

BACKGROUND: This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning the free-floating subdivision --Inspection and for using phrase headings for particular types of inspection.

### 1. Merchandise and products.

Use the free-floating subdivision --Inspection (May Subd Geog) under types of merchandise and products for works on methods of examining them to determine their fitness for marketing. Example:

```
650 #0 $a Lumber $x Inspection.
```

### 2. Machines, engineering structures, and types of buildings.

Use the free-floating subdivision --Inspection (May Subd Geog) under types of machines, engineering structures, and buildings for works on methods and procedures for determining compliance with safety regulations. Example:

```
650 #0 $a Automobiles $x Inspection $z Virginia.
```

### 3. Plants and animals.

Use the free-floating subdivision --Inspection (May Subd Geog) under individual and groups of plants and domestic animals for works on procedures for examining them to determine the presence of diseases or pests. Examples:

```
650 #0 $a Cattle $x Inspection.
650 #0 $a Corn $x Inspection.
650 #0 $a Poultry $x Inspection.
```

### 4. Military services.

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Inspection under names of individual military services for works on official examinations of the service to determine and report on its state of readiness for duty. Example:

```
610 10 $a United States. $b Army $x Inspection.
```

### 5. Phrase headings.

Do not use the free-floating subdivision --Inspection under topics if a phrase heading for the concept has already been established. Examples:

```
150 ## $a Factory inspection
150 ## $a Mine inspection
```

When assigning phrase headings of this type, verify that a 450 field of the type [topic]--Inspection appears in the authority record. Make such a reference, if it does not already exist.
