# H 1996 Relations and Military Relations

## Validation rules

1. The subdivisions `--Relations (general) with [...]`, `--Relations (general) with foreign countries`, `--Relations (military) with [...]`, and `--Relations (military) with foreign countries` were discontinued in 1981 and are invalid. (H 1996 background)
2. `--Relations` (May Subd Geog) is free-floating under names of regions, countries, cities, etc., and under individual religions and Christian denominations. (H 1996 sec. 1)
3. `--Military relations` (May Subd Geog) is free-floating under names of regions, countries, cities, etc. only (not under religions), for non-hostile military relations. (H 1996 sec. 1)
4. For diplomatic relations use `--Foreign relations` under names of countries and regions larger than countries (see H 1629), not `--Relations`. (H 1996 sec. 1 note)
5. `--Relations--[place]` and `--Military relations--[place]` follow normal geographic subdivision rules (H 830), coded `$z`, e.g. `United States--Relations--China`, `Latin America--Relations--Spain--Murcia (Region)`. (H 1996 sec. 2.a)
6. When subdivided by place, a reciprocal heading with the two geographic names reversed is also assigned, e.g. `China--Relations--United States`. (H 1996 sec. 2.a)
7. As a general rule, do not use these subdivisions for relations between regions or jurisdictions within the same country, except for historical relationships (e.g. `Tibet (China)--Relations--China` / `China--Relations--China--Tibet`). (H 1996 sec. 2.b)
8. For relations with three or more regions or jurisdictions, use `--Relations` or `--Military relations` without further geographic subdivision, e.g. `France--Relations`. (H 1996 sec. 2.c)
9. Under religions or Christian denominations, `--Relations` is further subdivided by a religion or Christian denomination coded `$x`, e.g. `Catholic Church--Relations--Islam` with reciprocal `Islam--Relations--Catholic Church`. (H 1996 sec. 3.a)
10. Each `--Relations--[religion or denomination]` combination is editorially established. (H 1996 sec. 3.a)
11. Do not interpose geographic subdivisions between `--Relations` and the subdivision for the religion or denomination. (H 1996 sec. 3.a)
12. When `--Relations--Christianity` is used, also assign `Christianity and other religions--[religion]`, e.g. `Buddhism--Relations--Christianity` with `Christianity and other religions--Buddhism`; each such heading is editorially established. (H 1996 sec. 3.b)

## Full text

BACKGROUND: In 1981 the subdivisions --Relations (general) with [...], --Relations (general) with foreign countries, --Relations (military) with [...], and --Relations (military) with foreign countries were discontinued.

### 1. General rule

Use the following free-floating subdivisions further subdivided by a specific place when appropriate:

| Subdivision | Usage |
|---|---|
| --Relations (May Subd Geog) | Use under names of regions, countries, cities, etc. for general relations between one region or jurisdiction and another, and under individual religions and Christian denominations for relations between one religion or denomination and another. |
| --Military relations (May Subd Geog) | Use under names of regions, countries, cities, etc. for non-hostile military relations and/or cooperation between one region or jurisdiction and another. |

Note: For works on diplomatic relations among regions and countries, use the subdivision --Foreign relations under names of countries and regions larger than countries. See H 1629 for instructions on its use.

### 2. Relations between one region or jurisdiction and another

#### a. General rule

Further subdivide --Relations and --Military relations by region or country to designate relations with a specific region or country, following normal rules for geographic subdivision (cf. H 830). If they are so subdivided, make an additional subject entry with the two geographic names in reversed positions. Examples:

```
651 #0 $a United States $x Relations $z China.
651 #0 $a China $x Relations $z United States.

651 #0 $a Murcia (Spain : Region) $x Relations $z Latin America.
651 #0 $a Latin America $x Relations $z Spain $z Murcia (Region)
```

#### b. Relations within a single country

As a general rule, do not use these subdivisions to bring out relations between regions or jurisdictions within the same country, except when it is necessary to designate historical relationships, such as the relations between a country and a locality of the country at the time when it was an independent jurisdiction. Example:

```
Title: Relations between pre-1965 Tibet and China.
651 #0 $a Tibet (China) $x Relations $z China.
651 #0 $a China $x Relations $z China $z Tibet.
```

#### c. Relations with three or more regions or jurisdictions

Use the free-floating subdivisions --Relations and --Military relations for the general relations or military relations of a region or jurisdiction with three or more regions or jurisdictions. Example:

```
Title: Mission to civilize: the French way.
651 #0 $a France $x Relations.
650 #0 $a Civilization, Modern $x French influences
```

### 3. Relations between one religion or Christian denomination and another

#### a. General rule

Further subdivide --Relations by a religion or Christian denomination to designate relations with a specific denomination or religion. If --Relations is so subdivided, make an additional subject entry with the religions or denominations in reversed positions. Example:

```
Title: Sacred conversations and the evolution of dialogue
610 20 $a Catholic Church $x Relations $x Islam
650 #0 $a Islam $x Relations $x Catholic Church
```

Editorially establish each use of a further subdivision by religion or denomination.

Do not interpose geographic subdivisions between the subdivision --Relations and the subdivision for the religion or denomination.

#### b. Relations with Christianity

If Christianity is used as a subdivision, make an additional subject entry in the form Christianity and other religions--[religion] (e.g., Islam--Relations--Christianity; Christianity and other religions--Islam). Editorially establish each heading of this type. Example:

```
Title: Buddhism and Christianity
650 #0 $a Buddhism $x Relations $x Christianity.
650 #0 $a Christianity and other religions $x Buddhism.
```
