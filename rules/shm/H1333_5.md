# H 1333.5 Boundaries

## Validation rules

1. `--Boundaries` is a free-floating topical subdivision (`$x`) used under names of countries, cities, and other places, for works on a place's boundaries or boundary disputes, including treaty texts. (H 1333.5 sec. 1.b, 2.b)
2. Do not subdivide `--Boundaries` by `--Treaties` (`[place]--Boundaries--Treaties` is invalid). (H 1333.5 sec. 1.b, 2.b; H 2227)
3. For the boundary (or boundary dispute) between two places, the form is `[place]--Boundaries--[other place]`, with the other place as a `$z` subdivision after `--Boundaries`; assign reciprocal headings for each place, e.g. `United States--Boundaries--Canada` and `Canada--Boundaries--United States`. (H 1333.5 sec. 1.b, 2.b)
4. When the other place is within a larger jurisdiction, it is given as an indirect geographic chain after `--Boundaries`, e.g. `Rio Grande do Sul (Brazil)--Boundaries--Brazil--Santa Catarina (State)`; `Santa Catarina (Brazil : State)--Boundaries--Brazil--Rio Grande do Sul`. (H 1333.5 sec. 3)
5. General works on boundaries not limited to a place get the heading `Boundaries` (without a place). (H 1333.5 sec. 1.a)
6. General works on boundary controversies not limited to a place get the heading `Boundary disputes`. (H 1333.5 sec. 2.a)
7. For the region adjacent to a boundary, use `Borderlands--[place]` (`Borderlands` subdivided geographically) for each place, unless a special regional heading exists or can be established (e.g. `Mexican-American Border Region`, `Scottish Borders (England and Scotland)`). (H 1333.5 sec. 1.b)
8. Named boundaries are entered as geographic headings, e.g. `Mason-Dixon Line`, `Oder-Neisse Line (Germany and Poland)`. (H 1333.5 sec. 1.c)
9. Territorial realignments and boundary questions resulting from wars take the free-floating subdivision `--Territorial questions` under wars (per H 1200), followed by a geographic subdivision, e.g. `World War, 1939-1945--Territorial questions--Poland`; add `[place]--Boundaries` or `[place]--Boundaries--[other place]` as appropriate. (H 1333.5 sec. 2.c)

## Full text

BACKGROUND: A boundary, represented by a line on a map, is that which divides one political unit, such as a country, from another. This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning headings to works on boundaries and boundary disputes.

### 1. Boundaries

#### a. General

Assign the heading *Boundaries* to general works on boundaries not limited to a particular place.

#### b. By place

Assign the free-floating subdivision *--Boundaries* under names of countries, cities, etc., to works on the boundaries of a place, including texts of treaties to establish or alter these boundaries.

In accordance with H 2227, do not subdivide *--Boundaries* by *--Treaties*.

For works on the boundary between two places, assign for each place a heading of the type *[place]--Boundaries--[other place]*. Example:

```
Title: The U.S.-Canadian border.
651 #0 $a United States $x Boundaries $z Canada.
651 #0 $a Canada $x Boundaries $z United States.
```

For works on the region adjacent to a boundary, assign for each place a heading of the type *Borderlands--[place]* unless a special heading for the region exists or can be established, for example, *Mexican-American Border Region*; *Scottish Borders (England and Scotland)*.

```
Title: Economic conditions of the borderlands of Chad and Niger.
650 #0 $a Borderlands $z Chad.
650 #0 $a Borderlands $z Niger.
```

#### c. Named boundaries

Assign geographic heading for named boundaries where they exist or can be established, for example, *Mason-Dixon Line*; *Oder-Neisse Line (Germany and Poland)*.

### 2. Boundary disputes

#### a. General

Assign the heading *Boundary disputes* to general works on controversies, conflicts, etc., about the location of boundaries not limited to a particular place.

#### b. By place

Assign the free-floating subdivision *--Boundaries* under names of places to works on the boundary disputes of that place, including texts of treaties to resolve these disputes.

In accordance with H 2227, do not subdivide *--Boundaries* by *--Treaties*.

If the work is on a boundary dispute between two places, or the resolution of such a dispute, assign for each place a heading of the type *[place]--Boundaries--[other place]*. Example:

```
Title: Treaty to resolve boundary differences between the United States
and Mexico.
651 #0 $a United States $x Boundaries $z Mexico.
651 #0 $a Mexico $x Boundaries $z United States.
```

#### c. By war

In accordance with H 1200, assign the free-floating subdivision *--Territorial questions* under wars, etc., to works that discuss territorial realignments, boundary questions, etc., resulting from these conflicts and the settlement of them.

Assign an additional heading or headings, if appropriate, for *[place]--Boundaries* or *[place]--Boundaries--[other place]*. Example:

```
Title: Poland's boundaries after the war.
650 #0 $a World War, 1939-1945 $x Territorial
            questions $z Poland.
651 #0 $a Poland $x Boundaries.
```

### 3. Assignment of headings

To works on boundaries and boundary disputes assign several headings, if appropriate, to bring out the most important topics. Examples:

```
Title: Minorities under communism.
651 #0 $a Balkan Peninsula $x Politics and government
            $y 20th century.
650 #0 $a Nationalism $z Balkan Peninsula $x History $y 20th
            century.
650 #0 $a Minorities $z Balkan Peninsula $x History $y 20th
            century.
651 #0 $a Balkan Peninsula $x Boundaries.

Title: Germany under the treaty.
630 00 $a Treaty of Versailles $d (1919)
650 #0 $a World War, 1914-1918 $x Territorial questions
            $z Germany.
651 #0 $a Germany $x Boundaries.
651 #0 $a Germany $x History $y 1918-1933.

Title: The maritime boundary treaty with Canada.
651 #0 $a United States $x Boundaries $z Canada.
651 #0 $a Canada $x Boundaries $z United States.
650 #0 $a Territorial waters $z Canada.
650 #0 $a Territorial waters $z United States.

Title: The United States in Sino-Soviet crises.
651 #0 $a Soviet Union $x Foreign relations $z China.
651 #0 $a China $x Foreign relations $z Soviet Union.
651 #0 $a Soviet Union $x Boundaries $z China.
651 #0 $a China $x Boundaries $z Soviet Union.
651 #0 $a United States $x Foreign relations $y 1945-1989.

Title: The division between the states of Rio Grande do Sul and
Santa Catarina.
651 #0 $a Rio Grande do Sul (Brazil) $x Boundaries $z Brazil
            $z Santa Catarina (State)
651 #0 $a Santa Catarina (Brazil : State) $x Boundaries
            $z Brazil $z Rio Grande do Sul.
```
