# H 1195 Pattern Headings: Land Vehicles

## Validation rules

1. Subdivisions on this list are free-floating under headings for types of motorized vehicles used on land, including `Motor vehicles` and headings for individual makes or models (e.g., Buses; Dune buggies; Farm tractors; Sports cars; Tanks (Military science); Trucks; Harley-Davidson motorcycle; Leopard (Tank); Mustang automobile), if appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. (H 1195, Types of headings; Conflicts)
2. Where a variant phrase or subdivision equivalent to a list subdivision is established in the subject authority file, the established variant takes precedence. (H 1195, Conflicts)
3. All subdivisions on this list are topical (`$x`); none is a form subdivision. (H 1195, list)
4. A subdivision may be followed by a geographic subdivision only if the list marks it (May Subd Geog); in multi-level entries the marking applies to the whole string as listed (e.g., `--Bodies--Alignment` may subdivide geographically, `--Brakes` may not). (H 1195, list)
5. Engine-type qualifiers attach to --Motors exactly as printed: `--Motors (Compressed-gas)`, `--Motors (Diesel)`, `--Motors (Liquid nitrogen)`, `--Motors (Two-stroke cycle)`. (H 1195, list)
6. After any `--Law and legislation` entry (e.g., `--Defects--Law and legislation`, `--Motors--Exhaust gas--Law and legislation`), further subdivisions for legal topics from H 1154.5 may be used. (H 1195, note 1)
7. `--Transmission devices, Automatic` is an inverted subdivision form, distinct from `--Transmission devices`. (H 1195, list)
8. [LC practice] If an exceptional variant form is retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating form (H 195); otherwise propose changing the variant (H 193). (H 1195, LC practice)

## Full text

PATTERN: *Automobiles*

### Types of headings covered by the pattern

Headings for types of motorized vehicles used on land, including the heading *Motor vehicles* and headings for individual makes or models of land vehicles. Examples: Buses; Dune buggies; Farm tractors; Sports cars; Tanks (Military science); Trucks; Harley-Davidson motorcycle; Leopard (Tank); Mustang automobile.

### Conflicts

Any subdivision listed here can be used as a free-floating subdivision under any heading belonging to the category if it is appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. Subject authority records may exist for headings employing variant phrases or subdivisions equivalent to subdivisions on this list.

LC practice: If an exceptional variant form is to be retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form following the procedures in H 195 if the reference does not yet exist. Otherwise, submit a proposal to change the variant form along with all bibliographic records requiring correction following the procedures in H 193.

Note: Most form subdivisions coded $v in this list may also be used as topical subdivisions coded $x when assigned to works about the form (see H 1075, sec. 1.d.).
