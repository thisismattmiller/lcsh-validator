# H 1103 Free-Floating Subdivisions: Ethnic Groups

## Validation rules

1. The subdivisions on the sec. D (topical) and sec. E (form) lists may be used as free-floating subdivisions under headings for ethnic groups, including preliterate groups, historic peoples no longer in existence, races, and ethnic groups in the United States, e.g. `Berbers`, `French-Canadians`, `Aboriginal Australians`, `Hmong (Asian people)`, `Dinka (African people)`, `Turkic peoples`, `Minoans`, `Black people`, `Cuban Americans`, `Scandinavian Americans`. (H 1103 sec. A)
2. The list applies under `Jews` and headings for Jews of various nationalities (e.g. `Jews, Italian`), except for subdivisions noted in the list and indicated by UF references in the subject authority file. (H 1103 sec. A)
3. The list applies under `Indians`, the regional groupings `Indians of Central America`, `Indians of Mexico`, `Indians of North America`, `Indians of South America`, `Indians of the West Indies`, and groups of tribes and individual tribes (e.g. `Aleuts`, `Eskimos`, `Navajo Indians`, `Spokane Indians`, `Uto-Aztecan Indians`, `Zapotec Indians`). The separate pattern list H 1152 was discontinued in February 1994. (H 1103 sec. A)
4. The list does not apply under the general headings `Indigenous peoples` and `Tribes`, nor under jurisdictional name headings for U.S.-recognized tribal governments (e.g. `Spokane Tribe of the Spokane Reservation, Washington`). (H 1103 sec. A)
5. Classes of persons qualified by ethnic or national adjectives (e.g. `African American dentists`, `French students`, `Foreign workers, Turkish`) take subdivisions from H 1100, not this list. (H 1103 sec. A)
6. Do not subdivide a nationality heading by subdivisions on this list to express a topic about the nationality in its own country; use `[topic]--[place]` or `[place]--[topic]`: `Mental health--United States`, not `Americans--Mental health`; `Spain--Antiquities`, not `Spanish--Antiquities`. (H 1103 secs. A, C)
7. Subdivisions on this list may be used under nationalities only for topics concerning the nationality outside its native country, e.g. `Spanish--Florida--Antiquities`, `Turks--Employment--Germany`. (H 1103 secs. A, C)
8. A subdivision may be followed by a geographic subdivision only if marked "Yes" (May Subd Geog) on the list (`may_subd_geog` in rules/lists/H1103.json). (H 1103 secs. D-E)
9. Cross-reference entries (May Subd Geog column `--`, e.g. `Amusements`, `Athletics`, `Civilization`, `Demography`, `Rulers`, `Tools`, `Vital statistics`) are not valid subdivisions; use the referenced subdivision (`--Games`/`--Recreation`, `--Sports`, unsubdivided heading or `Civilization, [adjective]`, `--Population`, `--Kings and rulers`, `--Implements`, `--Statistics, Vital`). (H 1103 secs. D-E)
10. Under `Jews` do not use: `--Ethnic identity` or `--Race identity` (use `Jews--Identity`); `--Funeral customs and rites` (use `Jewish funeral rites and ceremonies`); `--Marriage customs and rites` (use `Jewish marriage customs and rites`); `--Museums` (use `Jewish museums`); `--Rites and ceremonies` (use `Judaism--Customs and practices`); `--Missions` (use `Missions to Jews`; for missionary activities of Judaism use `Proselytizing--Judaism`). (H 1103 sec. D)
11. Do not use `--Diaries` under nationalities; use `[...] diaries`, e.g. `American diaries`, `Canadian diaries`. (H 1103 secs. D-E)
12. Use `--Antiquities` and its further subdivisions only under groups extant in modern times. (H 1103 sec. D)
13. Do not use `--Languages` to designate a linguistic family for which a separate heading exists (e.g. `Papuan languages`, `Slavic languages`). (H 1103 sec. D)
14. `--Missions` is for missions to the group; missionary activities of the group take headings of the type `Missions, [...]`, e.g. `Missions, Belgian`, `Missions, Tamil`. (H 1103 sec. D)
15. `--Chronology` is a cross-reference: use `--History--Chronology` for lists of events and dates; for the group's own time-reckoning system establish headings of the type `Chronology, [...]`, e.g. `Chronology, Igbo`. (H 1103 sec. D)
16. Specific foreign influences are established in the form `[ethnic group]--[...] influences`. (H 1103 sec. D, Foreign influences)
17. Century subdivisions `--16th century` through `--21st century` are free-floating after `--Economic conditions`, `--History`, `--Intellectual life`, `--Politics and government`, `--Social conditions`, and `--Social life and customs`. (H 1103 sec. D)
18. `--Newspapers` ($v) is used under ethnic groups for newspapers of those groups (e.g. ethnic groups in the U.S. for American ethnic newspapers); works about ethnic newspapers take `[...] newspapers`, e.g. `German American newspapers`. (H 1103 sec. E)
19. `--Biography--Dictionaries--French, [Italian, etc.]` is a multiple formed by combining two authority records. (H 1103 sec. E)
20. [LC practice] LC catalogers no longer use form subdivisions; assign LCGFT (e.g. `Textbooks`) and, for demographic groups, LCDGT. Form subdivisions remain valid LCSH. (H 1103 secs. A, E)

## Full text

### A. Background

The subdivisions listed below may be used on a free-floating basis, as appropriate, under subject headings for ethnic groups, including preliterate groups, historic peoples no longer in existence, races, and ethnic groups in the United States, for example, *Berbers*; *French-Canadians*; *Aboriginal Australians*; *Hmong (Asian people)*; *Dinka (African people)*; *Turkic peoples*; *Minoans*; *Black people*; *Cuban Americans*; *Scandinavian Americans*. The category also includes the heading Jews and headings for Jews of various nationalities, for example, *Jews, Italian*, except for subdivisions noted in the list below and indicated by UF references in the subject authority file. Also included are headings for nationalities such as *Danish*, *Asians*, and *Ukrainians*, when they are used to designate those nationalities or regional inhabitants outside their native countries or regions.

The category includes headings for the Indigenous peoples of the Western Hemisphere: *Indians*; headings for the five major regional groupings of Indians: *Indians of Central America*; *Indians of Mexico*; *Indians of North America*; *Indians of South America*; and *Indians of the West Indies*; as well as groups of tribes and individual tribes established as subject headings. Examples: *Aleuts*; *Eskimos*; *Navajo Indians*; *Spokane Indians*; *Uto-Aztecan Indians*; *Zapotec Indians*. The separate pattern list for groups of Indians, H 1152, was discontinued in February 1994. This list does not cover the general headings Indigenous peoples and Tribes, nor the jurisdictional name headings established to represent the governments of tribes recognized by the United States government, for example, *Spokane Tribe of the Spokane Reservation, Washington*.

For guidelines on subdivisions used under classes of persons, including age and sex groups, social, economic, and political categories, members of religions, occupational groups, etc., see H 1100. Headings for such classes of persons qualified by ethnic or national adjectival qualifiers, such as *African American dentists*, *French students*, or *Foreign workers, Turkish*, are also covered by H 1100 rather than this list.

Nationalities: Do not subdivide headings for nationalities by subdivisions on this list to designate a topic in conjunction with a nationality in its own country. Instead, express the topic by headings of the type: *[topic]--[place]* or *[place]--[topic]*, for example, *Mental health--United States*, not Americans--Mental health; *Spain--Antiquities*, not Spanish--Antiquities. Subdivisions on this list may be used under headings for nationalities only to express topics in conjunction with those nationalities outside their native countries, for example, *Spanish--Florida--Antiquities*; *Turks--Employment--Germany*. For guidelines on the construction and use of headings for nationalities, see H 1919.5.

LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. *Textbooks*. To replace a form subdivision that includes a demographic group, also assign Library of Congress Demographic Groups Terms (LCDGT) to bring out the audience of the resource being cataloged, e.g. *German speakers* or *Arabic, Students of*.

### C. Nationalities

Do not subdivide headings for nationalities by subdivisions on this list to designate a topic in conjunction with a nationality in its own country. Instead, express the topic by headings of the type: *[topic]--[place] or [place]--[topic]*, for example, *Mental health--United States*, not Americans--Mental health; Spain--Antiquities, not Spanish--Antiquities. Subdivisions on this list may be used under headings for nationalities only to express topics in conjunction with those nationalities outside their native countries, for example, *Spanish--Florida--Antiquities*; *Turks--Employment--Germany*. For guidelines on the construction and use of headings for nationalities, see H 1919.5.
