# H 2228 Trials

## Validation rules

1. `--Trials, litigation, etc.` (exact form) is free-floating under names of individual persons, families, corporate bodies, and jurisdictions, for proceedings of civil or criminal actions to which they are parties or works about them, e.g. `Jenrette, John W.--Trials, litigation, etc.`, `United States--Trials, litigation, etc.`, `Reserve Mining Company--Trials, litigation, etc.`. (H 2228 background, sec. 3, 4)
2. `--Trials, litigation, etc.` may be coded `$v` (proceedings) or `$x` (works about). (H 2228 examples)
3. Exception: under `Jesus Christ` use `--Trial`, not `--Trials, litigation, etc.`. (H 2228 sec. 3)
4. `Trials` (May Subd Geog) is for non-topical collections and general works on various trials, e.g. `Trials--United States`. (H 2228 sec. 1)
5. Particular types of trials take `Trials ([topic])` (May Subd Geog), with the topic in parentheses and geographic subdivision following, e.g. `Trials (Witchcraft)--Connecticut`, `Trials (Fraud)--Washington (D.C.)`, `Trials (Libel)--England--London`, `Trials (Divorce)`. (H 2228 sec. 2, 3, 4)
6. Individual criminal trials: first heading is `[defendant]--Trials, litigation, etc.` or the established trial heading, plus `Trials ([topic])--[place]` and other topical headings. (H 2228 sec. 3)
7. Individual civil trials: `--Trials, litigation, etc.` under the initiating party and each other major party, plus `Trials ([topic])--[place]` if such a heading exists or can be established; otherwise appropriate legal headings. (H 2228 sec. 4)
8. War crime trials additionally take `War crime trials--[place]`, e.g. `War crime trials--Germany--Nuremberg`. (H 2228 sec. 5)
9. Establish a heading for an individual trial only when it is known by a popular name other than a principal party's name. (H 2228 sec. 6)
10. Established trial headings take the form `[...] Trial, [city, larger geographical entity], [date(s)]`, e.g. `Watergate Trial, Washington, D.C., 1973`, `Milch Trial, Nuremberg, Germany, 1946-1947`, `Black Panthers Trial, New York, N.Y., 1970-1971` (per H 1592). (H 2228 sec. 6)
11. Include the city qualifier even if the city appears in the trial name, e.g. `Chicago Seven Trial, Chicago, Ill., 1969-1970`. (H 2228 sec. 6 note)
12. The 550 broader term for a trial heading is `Trials ([topic])--[place]` or `War crime trials--[place]`, divided only to the country (or first-order political division for the United States, Canada, Great Britain), e.g. `Trials (Conspiracy)--New York (State)`. (H 2228 sec. 6)

## Full text

BACKGROUND: The free-floating subdivision --Trials, litigation, etc. is used under names of individual persons, families, corporate bodies, or jurisdictions for the proceedings of civil or criminal actions to which they are parties, or for works about such proceedings, as described below. As a general rule, the same practices are followed in assigning subject headings to the actual proceedings of a trial as to works about a trial. The procedures described below therefore apply to both categories of material.

### 1. General collections of trials

Assign the heading Trials (May Subd Geog) to non-topical collections of proceedings of trials, or to general works describing various trials. Examples:

```
Title: Trials that made headlines.
650 #0 $a Trials.

Title: Great American courtroom battles.
650 #0 $a Trials $z United States.
```

### 2. Collections of particular types of trials

Assign headings of the type Trials ([topic]) (May Subd Geog) to collections of proceedings of particular types of civil or criminal trials, or to works describing several trials of a specific type. Example:

```
Title: Witchcraft trials of Connecticut.
650 #0 $a Trials (Witchcraft) $z Connecticut.
```

### 3. Individual criminal trials

Assign as the first heading the name of the defendant with the subdivision --Trials, litigation, etc., or, if the trial has been established as a subject heading, the heading for the name of the trial. Assign one or more additional headings of the type Trials ([topic])--[place], as appropriate, and any other topical headings required for the work in hand.

Exception: Use the subdivision --Trial under the heading Jesus Christ.

Examples:

```
Title: Excerpts from the transcript of the trial proceedings in the case of the United States of America v. John W. Jenrette.
600 10 $a Jenrette, John W. $v Trials, litigation, etc.
650 #0 $a Trials (Fraud) $z Washington (D.C.)
650 #0 $a Trials (Bribery) $z Washington (D.C.)
650 #0 $a Abscam Bribery Scandal, 1980.

Title: To set the record straight : the break-in, the tapes, the conspirators, the pardon.
650 #0 $a Watergate Trial, Washington, D.C., 1973.
650 #0 $a Watergate Affair, 1972-1974.
600 10 $a Nixon, Richard M. $q (Richard Milhous) $d 1913-1994 $x Pardon.
600 10 $a Sirica, John J.
```

### 4. Individual civil trials

Assign as the first heading the name of the party that initiated the civil action, and as additional headings, the names of the other major parties to the litigation. Use the subdivision --Trials, litigation, etc. under the heading for each of these parties. Also assign the heading Trials ([topic])--[place] if the nature of the trial is such that a heading of that type has been or can be established, for example, Trials (Divorce); Trials (Libel). If the trial cannot be described by a heading of that type, assign the appropriate legal headings to bring out the topic(s) of the trial, and any other topical headings required for the work in hand.

Examples:

```
Title: Superior pollutor : a saga of the struggle to stop pollution of the largest fresh water lake in the world by its most egregious polluter--the Reserve Mining Company.
651 #0 $a United States $x Trials, litigation, etc.
651 #0 $a Minnesota $x Trials, litigation, etc.
610 20 $a Reserve Mining Company $x Trials, litigation, etc.
650 #0 $a Water $x Pollution $x Law and legislation $z Minnesota.
650 #0 $a Taconite $x Environmental aspects $z Superior, Lake.
650 #0 $a Iron mines and mining $x Environmental aspects $z Superior, Lake.

Title: Salome's last veil : the libel case of the century.
600 10 $a Billing, Noel Pemberton, $d 1880-1948 $x Trials, litigation, etc.
600 10 $a Allan, Maude $x Trials, litigation, etc.
650 #0 $a Trials (Libel) $z England $z London.

Title: Allan Bakke versus Regents of the University of California.
600 10 $a Bakke, Allan Paul $v Trials, litigation, etc.
610 20 $a University of California (System) $v Trials, litigation, etc.
650 #0 $a Discrimination in medical education $x Law and legislation $z California.
650 #0 $a Medical colleges $z California $x Admission.

Title: The New York Times Company v. United States : a documentary history of the Pentagon Papers litigation.
610 20 $a New York Times Company $x Trials, litigation, etc.
651 #0 $a United States $x Trials, litigation, etc.
630 00 $a Pentagon Papers.
650 #0 $a Freedom of the press $z United States.
650 #0 $a Security classification (Government documents) $z United States.
```

### 5. War crime trials

Assign, in addition to the name of the defendant or the name of the trial, the heading War crime trials--[place] to all proceedings of, or works about, particular war crime trials. Examples:

```
Title: Nuernberg war crimes trials : records of case II, United States of America v. Erhard Milch.
650 #0 $a Milch Trial, Nuremberg, Germany, 1946-1947.
650 #0 $a War crime trials $z Germany $z Nuremberg.

Title: The Tokyo war crimes trial.
650 #0 $a Tokyo Trial, Tokyo, Japan, 1946-1948.
650 #0 $a War crime trials $z Japan $z Tokyo.
```

### 6. Establishing headings for trials

Generally, it is not necessary to establish a subject heading for an individual trial. Instead, the established name headings for the principal parties in a trial, with the subdivision --Trials, litigation, etc., are used as subject headings for a work about the trial. If, however, a trial comes to be known by a popular name other than that of one of the principal parties, a subject heading should be established for the trial. This occurs most commonly in the case of trials connected with a major political event, scandal, etc., or trials involving several defendants. Establish such headings in the following form, according to the provisions of H 1592:

```
150 ## $a [...] Trial, [city, larger geographical entity], [date(s)]
```

Note: Include the name of the city as a qualifier even if it appears as part of the name of the trial itself, for example, Chicago Seven Trial, Chicago, Ill., 1969-1970.

Add 450 fields from alternative names of the trial, if any, and a 550 field (broader term reference) from the heading Trials ([topic])--[place] (or War crime trials--[place]). Divide the broader term reference only to the level of the country (or first order political division in the case of the United States, Canada, and Great Britain). Examples:

```
150 ## $a Black Panthers Trial, New York, N.Y., 1970-1971
450 ## $a Bomb Conspiracy Trial, New York, N.Y., 1970-1971
450 ## $a New York 21 Trial, New York, N.Y., 1970-1971
450 ## $a New York City Bomb Conspiracy Trial, New York, N.Y., 1970-1971
550 ## $w g $a Trials (Conspiracy) $z New York (State)

150 ## $a Red Brigades Trial, Turin, Italy, 1978
450 ## $a Brigate rosse Trial, Turin, Italy, 1978
450 ## $a Processo Brigate rosse, Turin, Italy, 1978
550 ## $w g $a Trials (Political crimes and offenses) $z Italy
550 ## $w g $a Trials (Terrorism) $z Italy
```
