# H 1078 Dates in Subdivisions for Specific Events

## Validation rules

1. Event subdivisions of the type `--[name of event], [date]` (under corporate bodies) and `--History--[name of event], [date]` (under places), e.g. `--Explosion, [date]`, `--Eruption, [date]`, including the date, are free-floating as authorized by H 1105 and H 1140; analogous subdivisions under pattern headings are free-floating under headings covered by the pattern, e.g. `United States. Navy--Cruise, [date]`. (H 1078 Background)
2. The event name and date are separated by a comma and space: `--Fire, 1911`. (H 1078 secs. 1-3 examples)
3. For an event within a single year, give only the year, unless it conflicts with another actual or potential heading, e.g. `Triangle Shirtwaist Company--Fire, 1911`. (H 1078 sec. 1)
4. For an event spanning more than one year, give the full range of years, e.g. `United States. Navy--Cruise, 1907-1909`, even if the work covers only one year or part of the span. (H 1078 sec. 2)
5. To distinguish two or more similar events in the same year, add month and day (or span of days) in parentheses after the year, e.g. `Colima (Mexico : Volcano)--Eruption, 2016 (September 25)`, `Colima (Mexico : Volcano)--Eruption, 2016 (December 18)`. (H 1078 sec. 3)
6. The month and day, or time span, may also be constructed on a free-floating basis. (H 1078 sec. 3)
7. Do not abbreviate names of months in these headings (e.g. `(September 25)`, not `(Sept. 25)`). (H 1078 sec. 3)
8. For subfield coding of these subdivisions see H 620 sec. 1.e(1). (H 1078 Background)

## Full text

### BACKGROUND

Certain headings for specific events are constructed by using subdivisions of the type --[name of event], [date] under corporate bodies, or --History--[name of event], [date] under names of places, for example, --Explosion, [date]; --Eruption, [date]; etc. Subdivisions of this type, including the date element, are free-floating as authorized by H 1105 and H 1140. Similar subdivisions appear under certain pattern headings and are therefore free-floating under individual headings covered by the pattern, for example, United States. Navy--Cruise, [date]. This instruction sheet provides guidelines for formulating the date element in headings of this type. For an explanation of subfield coding of subdivisions of this type, see H 620, sec. 1.e(1).

### 1. Events within a single year

When formulating a heading with a subdivision for an event that occurred in a single year, specify only the year, provided that there is no conflict with another actual or potential heading. In case of conflict, see sec. 3, below. Example:

```
610 20 $a Triangle Shirtwaist Company $x Fire, 1911.
```

### 2. Events spanning more than one year

If the event spanned a period of more than one year, specify the full range of years. Example:

```
610 10 $a United States. $b Navy $x Cruise, 1907-1909.
```

Provide this full span of dates even if the work being cataloged focuses on only one year, or one part, of the span.

### 3. Two or more events within the same year

If it is necessary to specify a month and day, or span of days, in order to distinguish between two or more similar events occurring in the same year, add the additional information in parentheses following the year. Examples:

```
651 #0 $a Colima (Mexico : Volcano) $x Eruption, 2016 (September 25)
651 #0 $a Colima (Mexico : Volcano) $x Eruption, 2016 (December 18)
```

Note: In headings of this type, even the month and day, or time span, may be constructed on a free-floating basis. Do not abbreviate names of months when formulating headings of this type.
