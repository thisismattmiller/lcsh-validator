# H 2020 Research

## Validation rules

1. `--Research` (May Subd Geog) is free-floating under names of places and corporate bodies, classes of persons, ethnic groups, and topical headings. (H 2020 sec. 2)
2. Specific aspects of research are expressed by a topical subdivision after `--Research`, e.g. `--Research--Finance`, `--Research--Management`, `--Research--Methodology`; a geographic subdivision may intervene: `Agriculture--Research--United States--Finance`. (H 2020 sec. 2, 5)
3. The heading `Research` may likewise be subdivided by `--Finance`, `--Management`, `--Methodology`, etc. (H 2020 sec. 1)
4. Do not use `[topic]--Research` if it conflicts with an established heading or UF reference; e.g. `Science--Research` is invalid because it is a UF under `Research`. (H 2020 sec. 3)
5. Establish phrase headings for research (e.g. `Action research`, `Single subject research`) only for a type of research or when `[topic]--Research` cannot be constructed; former phrase headings such as `Historical research`, `Medical research` were converted to `[topic]--Research` where possible. (H 2020 background, sec. 4)
6. `--Legal research` is a separate free-floating subdivision governed by H 1710. (H 2020 background)

## Full text

BACKGROUND: Until 1973 subject headings for research in various fields were established in the form [topic]--Research (May Subd Geog) or as phrase headings such as Historical research or Medical research. In 1973 the subdivision --Research (May Subd Geog) was established as a free-floating subdivision and phrase headings were later converted to [topic]--Research wherever possible.

For instructions on the use of the free-floating subdivision --Legal research, see H 1710.

### 1. The subject heading Research

Assign the heading to general works that discuss comprehensively all aspects of research, such as proposals, finance, goals, management, personnel, special projects, etc., in all fields, and to works on general scientific research. Do not assign the heading to works that discuss the results of research. For works that are limited to a specific aspect of research assign the heading Research with the appropriate topical subdivision, such as --Finance, --Management, or --Methodology.

### 2. The free-floating subdivision --Research

Assign --Research (May Subd Geog) as a free-floating subdivision under names of places and corporate bodies, classes of persons, ethnic groups, and topical headings, for general works that discuss comprehensively all aspects of research, such as proposals, finance, goals, etc., as applied to the topic. Do not assign the subdivision to works that discuss the results of research in a particular field. For works that are limited to a specific aspect of research assign the subdivision --Research further subdivided by the appropriate topical subdivision, such as --Finance, --Management, or --Methodology.

### 3. Conflict

Before assigning the subdivision, determine whether the use under consideration conflicts with a previously established heading in the subject authority file. For example, the heading Science--Research may not be assigned because it appears as a UF under the heading Research.

### 4. Phrase headings for research

Establish a phrase heading for research only to designate a type of research or when it is not possible to construct a heading of the type [topic]--Research. Examples:

```
150 ## $a Action research
150 ## $a Single subject research
```

### 5. Special aspects of research

For works that are limited to a special aspect of research, assign the appropriate heading for the topic. Examples:

```
650 #0 $a Agriculture $x Research $z United States $x Finance.
650 #0 $a Education $x Research $x Management.
650 #0 $a History $x Methodology.
650 #0 $a Medicine $x Research grants $z United States.
```
