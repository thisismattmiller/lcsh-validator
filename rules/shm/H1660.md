# H 1660 Imprints

## Validation rules

1. `--Imprints` is a free-floating form subdivision ($v) used under names of individual regions, countries, cities, etc., e.g. `United States--Imprints`, `India--Imprints`. (H 1660 sec. C)
2. Lists of works published in a particular language take the phrase heading `[language] imprints`, subdivided geographically by place of origin, e.g. `English imprints--India`. (H 1660 sec. D)
3. Where a single language predominates in a place, assign only `[place]--Imprints`; e.g. do not assign `English imprints--United States`. (H 1660 sec. E)
4. When `[language] imprints--[place]` is assigned, also assign `[place]--Imprints`. (H 1660 sec. F)
5. Catalogs of imprints take `[place]--Imprints--Catalogs` or `[language] imprints--[place]--Catalogs`; the place refers to the place of origin. (H 1660 sec. G)
6. Do not subdivide `[language] imprints` or `[place]--Imprints` by `--Bibliography`. (H 1660 sec. H)
7. Lists of imprints on a specific topic also take `[topic]--Bibliography`, e.g. `Medicine--Bibliography`. (H 1660 sec. I)
8. [LC practice] LC does not use `--Imprints`; it assigns `Publications--[place]` (e.g. `Publications--India`) with LCGFT `Bibliographies` (and `Catalogs`/`Library catalogs` as appropriate); LC still uses `[language] imprints--[place]`. `--Imprints` remains valid LCSH. (H 1660 sec. C, F, G)

## Full text

### A. Background

LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. *Catalogs*.

For guidelines on catalogs, see H 1360. For guidelines on catalogs of library materials, see H 1361. For guidelines on collections of objects, see H 1427. For guidelines on publishers' catalogs, see H 1965.

### B. Recent revisions

2026-02: Updated template. Added instructions relating to LCGFT implementation.

### C. The subdivision --Imprints

Use the free-floating form subdivision *--Imprints* under individual regions, countries, cities, etc., for lists of works published in those places without regard to the language in which they were published, for example, *United States--Imprints*.

LC practice: To bring out the place of imprints, assign *Publications--[place]*. Also assign LCGFT *Bibliographies* if appropriate to the resource in hand.

### D. Phrase headings

Use phrase headings of the type *[language] imprints* for lists of works published in a particular language. Use geographic subdivision to refer to the place of origin, for example, *English imprints--India*.

### E. Imprints from places where a single language predominates

As a general rule, assign only the heading *[place]--Imprints* for lists of works published in places where a single language is predominant, for example, the United States. Since English is the predominant language in the United States, do not assign an additional heading of the type *English imprints--United States*.

### F. Assignment of additional heading with [language] imprints

When assigning a heading of the type *[language] imprints* subdivided by place, assign an additional heading of the type *[place]--Imprints*.

LC practice: To bring out the place of imprints, assign *Publications--[place]*. Also assign LCGFT *Bibliographies*.

Example:

```
Title: A list of books in English published in India.
650 #0 $a English imprints $z India.
651 #0 $a India $v Imprints.
LC practice:
650 #0 $a English imprints $z India.
650 #0 $a Publications $z India.
655 #7 $a Bibliographies. $2 lcgft
```

### G. Catalogs

For catalogs listing imprints existing at designated localities or available from specified sources, assign as appropriate either *[place]--Imprints--Catalogs* or *[language] imprints--Catalogs*.

Note: The place used with either heading refers to place of origin.

LC practice: To bring out the place of imprints, assign *Publications--[place]*. Also assign LCGFT *Bibliographies* and *Catalogs*, or the more specific term.

Example:

```
Title: The Rajasthan University Library catalog of Indian imprints in English.
650 #0 $a English imprints $z India $v Catalogs.
651 #0 $a India $v Imprints $v Catalogs.
610 20 $a Rajasthan University Library $v Catalogs.
LC practice:
650 #0 $a English imprints $z India.
650 #0 $a Publications $z India.
610 20 $a Rajasthan University Library.
655 #7 $a Bibliographies. $2 lcgft
655 #7 $a Library catalogs. $2 lcgft
```

### H. Bibliography not used

Do not further subdivide headings of the type *[language] imprints* and *[place]--Imprints* by the subdivision *--Bibliography*.

LC practice: Assign LCGFT *Bibliographies*.

### I. Imprints on a specific topic

For lists of imprints on a specific topic, also assign a heading of the type *[topic]--Bibliography*.

LC practice: Assign a subject heading for the topic. Also assign LCGFT *Bibliographies*.

Example:

```
Title: A list of English-language books on medicine published in India.
650 #0 $a Medicine $v Bibliography.
650 #0 $a English imprints $z India.
651 #0 $a India $v Imprints.
LC practice:
650 #0 $a Medicine.
650 #0 $a English imprints $z India.
650 #0 $a Publications $z India.
655 #7 $a Bibliographies. $2 lcgft
```
