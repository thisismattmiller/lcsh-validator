# H 2021 Reviews

## Validation rules

1. `Books--Reviews` is used for collections of reviews of books on many subjects, only when too broad for a more specific heading. (H 2021 sec. 1.a)
2. `--Reviews` is free-floating under headings for types of books, e.g. `Reference books--Reviews`. (H 2021 sec. 1.a)
3. `--Book reviews` (not `--Reviews`) is the free-floating form subdivision under topical headings and names of places for collections of appraisals of books on those subjects, e.g. `Art--Book reviews`, `California--Book reviews`, `United States--History--Civil War, 1861-1865--Book reviews`. (H 2021 sec. 1.b)
4. `--Reviews` and `--Book reviews` are form subdivisions (`$v`) and may be followed by further form subdivisions, e.g. `Books--Reviews--Periodicals`. (H 2021 background, sec. 1)
5. Works on the technique of writing book reviews take the topical heading `Book reviewing` (May Subd Geog). (H 2021 sec. 1.a note)
6. Newspaper review columns: `Newspapers--Sections, columns, etc.--Reviews` and `[...] newspapers--Sections, columns, etc.--Reviews`, e.g. `Canadian newspapers--Sections, columns, etc.--Reviews` (here `--Reviews` is coded `$x`). (H 2021 sec. 2)
7. `--Reviews` is free-floating under headings for non-book materials, e.g. `Compact discs--Reviews`, `Audiotapes--Reviews`. (H 2021 sec. 3)
8. `--Reviews` is free-floating under headings for mass media and the performing arts, and may follow a geographic subdivision, e.g. `Television programs--United States--Reviews`, `Performing arts--England--London--Reviews`, `Theater--Reviews`. (H 2021 sec. 4)

## Full text

BACKGROUND: The subdivisions --Reviews and --Book reviews are used as form subdivisions for collections of explanatory or critical accounts of artistic productions or performances, such as books, plays, concerts, etc. This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning these subdivisions.

### 1. Books

#### a. Books--Reviews

Use the free-floating subdivision --Reviews under the heading Books for collections of descriptive and evaluative accounts of books on many subjects. Example:

```
Title: Book review digest.
650 #0 $a Books $v Reviews $v Periodicals.
```

Assign this heading only to collections too broad to be assigned a more specific heading.

Note: For works about the technique of writing book reviews, assign the topical heading Book reviewing (May Subd Geog).

Also use the free-floating subdivision --Reviews under headings for types of books, for example, Reference books--Reviews.

#### b. [topic]--Book reviews

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Book reviews under topical headings and names of places for collections of appraisals of books on these topics. Examples:

```
650 #0 $a Art $v Book reviews.
651 #0 $a California $v Book reviews.
650 #0 $a English literature $y l9th century $x History and criticism $v Book reviews.
650 #0 $a French imprints $v Book reviews.
650 #0 $a Medicine $v Book reviews.
651 #0 $a United States $x History $y Civil War, 1861-1865 $v Book reviews.
```

### 2. Newspapers

Assign the heading Newspapers--Sections, columns, etc.--Reviews and headings of the type [...] newspapers--Sections, columns, etc.--Reviews for works on the writing and editing of reviews of books, music, theater, etc., and for collections of these writings. Example:

```
650 #0 $a Canadian newspapers $x Sections, columns, etc. $x Reviews.
```

### 3. Non-book materials

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Reviews under headings for non-book materials for collections of descriptive and evaluative accounts of these materials. Examples:

```
650 #0 $a Compact discs $v Reviews.
650 #0 $a Audiotapes $v Reviews.
```

### 4. Mass media and performing arts

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Reviews under headings for mass media and the performing arts for collections of critical writings about programs, performances, etc. Examples:

```
650 #0 $a Performing arts $z England $z London $v Reviews.
650 #0 $a Television programs $z United States $v Reviews.
650 #0 $a Theater $v Reviews.
```
