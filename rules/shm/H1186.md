# H 1186 Pattern Headings: Religious and Monastic Orders

## Validation rules

1. Subdivisions on this list are free-floating under names of individual religious and monastic orders (male and female, all religions) established as corporate bodies in the name authority file, e.g., Benedictines; Carmelite Nuns; Sisters of Mercy (Buffalo, N.Y.); Communauté de Taizé, if appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. (H 1186, Types of headings; Conflicts)
2. Do not apply this list to headings for types of religious orders, e.g., `Military religious orders`. (H 1186, Types of headings)
3. Subdivisions on the H 1105 list (names of individual corporate bodies) are also free-floating under individual religious and monastic orders where there is no conflict. (H 1186, Relation to H 1105)
4. Where a variant phrase or subdivision equivalent to a list subdivision is established in the subject authority file, the established variant takes precedence. (H 1186, Conflicts)
5. Most `$v` form subdivisions on the list may also be used as `$x` topical subdivisions for works about the form. (H 1186, Note; H 1075 sec. 1.d)
6. Only these list subdivisions may be followed by a geographic subdivision: --Education, --Missions, --Nazi persecution. (H 1186, list)
7. --Liturgy is coded `$x`; its form subdivision is `$x Liturgy $v Texts`. (H 1186, list)
8. [LC practice] If an exceptional variant form is retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating form (H 195); otherwise propose changing the variant (H 193). (H 1186, LC practice)

## Full text

PATTERN: *Jesuits*

### Types of headings covered by the pattern

Headings for names of individual religious and monastic orders established by as corporate bodies in the name authority file. The category includes names of individual male and female groups in all religions. Examples: Benedictines; Carmelite Nuns; Sisters of Mercy (Buffalo, N.Y.); Communauté de Taizé. Headings for types of religious orders, for example, *Military religious orders*, are not included.

### Relation to H 1105

Subdivisions on the list of free-floating subdivisions used under names of individual corporate bodies, H 1105, are also free-floating under individual religious and monastic orders where there is no conflict. This list contains additional subdivisions appropriate for religious and monastic orders.

### Conflicts

Any subdivision listed here can be used as a free-floating subdivision under any heading belonging to the category if it is appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. Subject authority records may exist for headings employing variant phrases or subdivisions equivalent to subdivisions on this list.

LC practice: If an exceptional variant form is to be retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form following the procedures in H 195 if the reference does not yet exist. Otherwise, submit a proposal to change the variant form along with all bibliographic records requiring correction following the procedures in H 193.

Note: Most form subdivisions coded $v in this list may also be used as topical subdivisions coded $x when assigned to works about the form (see H 1075, sec. 1.d.).
