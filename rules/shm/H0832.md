# H 832 Geographic Subdivision to the City Level

## Validation rules

1. If a heading is authorized for geographic subdivision, divide it geographically to the city level for a topic in a specific city: `Fountains--California--Los Angeles` (not the former city-flip form `Los Angeles (Calif.)--Fountains`). (H 832 sec. 1)
2. The city flip, discontinued in 1985, formerly applied to the topics listed in the Background; strings such as `[city]--Fountains`, `[city]--Bridges`, `[city]--Parks` that rely on it are obsolete unless the subdivision is otherwise valid under city names (H 1140). (H 832 Background)
3. For local history and genealogy works, an additional `[city]--[topic]` heading may also be assigned (H 1845). (H 832 sec. 2)
4. For categories of structures (buildings, monuments, bridges, etc.), also assign `[city]--Buildings, structures, etc.` within the limits of H 1334 and H 1334.5. (H 832 sec. 3)
5. If the city is an important aspect of the work, assign an additional `[city]--[topic]` heading using a subdivision valid under city names (e.g. `--Race relations`, `--Social life and customs`; H 1140). (H 832 sec. 4)
6. For disasters, riots, demonstrations, etc. in cities, assign `[category of event]--[place]`, and for historical treatments `[city]--History--[period or century subdivision, if appropriate]` (H 1560). (H 832 sec. 5)

## Full text

**BACKGROUND:** Until early 1985, certain topical headings had provision for geographic subdivision, but were not divided geographically to the city level. Instead, in order to express these topics in conjunction with the name of a city, a subdivision was used under the heading for the city. Topics that received this treatment could be identified in Library of Congress Subject Headings by the presence of a general see also reference of the following type:

```
Fountains (Indirect)
  sa subdivision Fountains under names of cities
```

The term "city flip" was used informally to refer to this practice. Following the provisions of the city flip, the heading for a general work about fountains in California would be *Fountains--California*, whereas the heading for a work about fountains in Los Angeles would have been *Los Angeles (Calif.)--Fountains*. The city flip was discontinued in 1985. The general see also references of the type described above were cancelled from the subject authority file.

### 1. General rule

If a work discusses a topic in a specific city, and the heading for the topic is authorized for geographic subdivision, divide the heading geographically to the city level, for example, *Fountains--California--Los Angeles*.

### 2. Local history materials

For works of interest to local historians and genealogists, also assign an appropriate [city]--[topic] heading, as described in H 1845.

### 3. Structures

If the topic of the work is a category of structures, such as buildings, monuments, bridges, etc., also assign the heading [city]--Buildings, structures, etc., within the limitations of H 1334 and H 1334.5.

### 4. City as topic of work

If the city is judged to be an important aspect of the work in its own right, assign an additional heading of the type [city]--[topic], using one of the topical subdivisions valid for use under city names, for example, --Race relations, --Social life and customs, etc. (cf. H 1140).

### 5. Works about disasters, riots, demonstrations, etc., in cities

As a general rule, assign a heading of the type [category of event]--[place], and for historical treatments, a heading of the type [city]--History--[period or century subdivision, if appropriate] (see H 1560).
