# H 1188 Pattern Headings: Sacred Works

## Validation rules

1. Subdivisions on this list are free-floating under uniform titles of individual sacred works or scriptures from all religions, including their individual parts and apocryphal works (e.g., Book of Mormon; Qur'an; Talmud; Tripitaka; Vedas; Bible. Wisdom of Solomon; Bible. Gospels; Sermon on the mount; Beatitudes; Epistle of Barnabas), if appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. (H 1188, Types of headings; Conflicts)
2. Do not apply this list to headings for individual non-sacred liturgical or theological works, or to form headings for collections of sacred or religious works; use H 1155.8 for uniform titles of secular classics. (H 1188, Types of headings)
3. Where a variant phrase or subdivision equivalent to a list subdivision is established in the subject authority file, the established variant takes precedence. (H 1188, Conflicts)
4. Most `$v` form subdivisions on the list may also be used as `$x` topical subdivisions for works about the form. (H 1188, Note; H 1075 sec. 1.d)
5. --Accents and accentuation, --Criticism, interpretation, etc., Jewish, --Quotations in rabbinical literature, and --Quotations in the New Testament are used only under `Bible. Old Testament` or individual parts or books of the Old Testament. (H 1188, note 1)
6. --Relation to Matthew, [Jeremiah, etc.] is used only under individual books or groups of books of the Bible, and requires a duplicate entry under the reverse, e.g., `630 00 $a Bible. $p Psalms $x Relation to Jeremiah.` and `630 00 $a Bible. $p Jeremiah $x Relation to Psalms.` (H 1188, note 2)
7. --Relation to the Old Testament is used only under the New Testament or individual books or groups of books of the New Testament, with a duplicate reverse entry, e.g., `Bible. Matthew--Relation to the Old Testament` and `Bible. Old Testament--Relation to Matthew`. (H 1188, note 3)
8. --Versions is governed by H 1300. (H 1188, note 4)
9. --Versions, African, [Indic, Slavic, etc.] takes an adjectival qualifier for groups of languages only; for translations into an individual language use `Bible. [language]--Versions`, and for a particular translation `Bible. [language]--Versions--[name of version]`, e.g., `Bible. English--Versions--Authorized` [not `Bible--Versions, English`]. (H 1188, note 5)
10. Chronological subdivisions under --Criticism, interpretation, etc.--History are limited on the list to: `Early church, ca. 30-600`; `Middle Ages, 600-1500`; `Modern period, 1500-`; `16th century`; `17th century`; `18th century`; `19th century`; `20th century`; `21st century`. (H 1188, list)
11. Only the subdivisions marked (May Subd Geog) may be followed by a place: --Abstracting and indexing, --Appreciation, --Children's use, --Criticism, interpretation, etc., --Feminist criticism, --Liturgical use, --Postcolonial criticism, --Publication and distribution, --Reading, --Social scientific criticism, --Socio-rhetorical criticism, --Structuralist criticism, --Study and teaching, --Teenagers' use, --Translating. (H 1188, list)
12. Multiples on the list are completed with a language, version, or name as indicated, e.g., `--Concordances, French, [German, etc.]`, `--Harmonies, English, [French, German, etc.]`, `--Interlinear translations, English, [French, etc.]`, `--Liturgical lessons, Dutch, [English, etc.]`, `--Manuscripts, English, [Latin, Aramaic, etc.]`, `--Parallel versions, English, [French, etc.]`, `--Paraphrases, English, [French, German, etc.]`, `--Concordances, English--Authorized, [Living Bible, Revised Standard, etc.]`, `--Versions--Authorized, [Living Bible, Revised Standard, etc.]`, `--Study and teaching--Baptists, [Catholic Church, etc.]`, `--Prophecies--[subject of prophecy]`. (H 1188, list)
13. Inverted subdivision forms are used as printed: --Copies, Curious; --Criticism, Form; --Criticism, Narrative; --Criticism, Redaction; --Criticism, Textual; --Editions, Curious; --Quotations, Early; --Manuscripts (Papyri). (H 1188, list)
14. --Influence may be further subdivided by the civilization headings listed: --Medieval civilization, --Modern civilization, --Slavic civilization, --Western civilization. (H 1188, list)
15. [LC practice] If an exceptional variant form is retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating form (H 195); otherwise propose changing the variant (H 193). (H 1188, LC practice)

## Full text

PATTERN: *Bible*

### Types of headings covered by the pattern

Headings for the uniform titles of individual sacred works or scriptures from all religions, including their individual parts. Also included are uniform titles for apocryphal works. Examples: Book of Mormon; Qur'an; Talmud; Tripitaka; Vedas; Bible. Wisdom of Solomon; Bible. Gospels; Sermon on the mount; Beatitudes; Epistle of Barnabas. The category does not include headings for individual non-sacred liturgical or theological works, nor form headings for collections of sacred or religious works. For subdivisions used under the uniform titles of secular classics, see H 1155.8.

### Conflicts

Any subdivision listed here can be used as a free-floating subdivision under any heading belonging to the category if it is appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. Subject authority records may exist for headings employing variant phrases or subdivisions equivalent to subdivisions on this list.

LC practice: If an exceptional variant form is to be retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form following the procedures in H 195 if the reference does not yet exist. Otherwise, submit a proposal to change the variant form along with all bibliographic records requiring correction following the procedures in H 193.

Note: Most form subdivisions coded $v in this list may also be used as topical subdivisions coded $x when assigned to works about the form (see H 1075, sec. 1.d.).

### Notes

1. Use only under Bible. Old Testament or individual parts or books of the Old Testament.
2. Use only under individual books or groups of books of the Bible. Make a duplicate entry under the reverse. Example:

```
630 00 $a Bible. $p Psalms $x Relation to Jeremiah.
630 00 $a Bible. $p Jeremiah $x Relation to Psalms.
```

3. Use only under the New Testament or under individual books or groups of books of the New Testament. Make a duplicate entry under the reverse. Example:

```
630 00 $a Bible. $p Matthew $x Relation to the Old Testament.
630 00 $a Bible. $p Old Testament $x Relation to Matthew.
```

4. See H 1300 for instructions on the use of the subdivision --Versions.
5. Assign the adjectival qualifier for groups of languages only. For works on translations of the Bible into individual languages, assign: Bible. [language]--Versions. For works on particular translations, use Bible. [language]--Versions--[name of version], for example, *Bible. English--Versions--Authorized*.
