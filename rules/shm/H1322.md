# H 1322 Bibliographies about Individual Persons

## Validation rules

1. `--Bibliography` is a free-floating form subdivision ($v) under names of individuals for lists of works by or about those persons (e.g. `Freud, Sigmund, 1856-1939--Bibliography`). (H 1322 sec. C)
2. For lists of a person's musical works that include musical notation for the opening measures of works or sections, use the free-floating form subdivision `--Thematic catalogs` under the person's name (rather than `--Bibliography`). (H 1322 sec. C)
3. If a bibliography about a person has a topical orientation, add a heading `[topic]--Bibliography` (e.g. `Psychoanalysis--Bibliography`). (H 1322 sec. D)
4. [LC practice] LC catalogers no longer use the form subdivisions `--Bibliography` and `--Thematic catalogs`; they assign the unsubdivided name (and topic) headings plus LCGFT *Bibliographies* and/or *Thematic catalogs (Music)*. The subdivided forms remain valid LCSH. (H 1322 sec. A, C, D)

## Full text

### A. Background

LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. *Bibliographies*.

For guidelines on abstracts, see H 1205. For guidelines on bio-bibliographies, see H 1328. For guidelines on bibliographies of bibliographies, see H 1325. For catalogs of library materials, see H 1361. For guidelines on digests of legal materials, see H 1550.

### B. Recent revisions

2026-01: Updated template. Added instructions related to LCGFT implementation.

### C. General rule

Use the free-floating subdivision *--Bibliography* under names of individuals for lists of works by or about those persons.

For lists of musical works by a person that include musical notation for the opening measures of individual works or sections of works, use the free-floating form subdivision *--Thematic catalogs*.

LC practice: Assign the names of individuals for lists of works by or about those persons. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Bibliographies* and/or a more specific term, e.g. *Thematic catalogs (Music)*.

### D. Special topics

If the bibliography has a topical orientation, assign an additional heading of the type *[topic]--Bibliography* to bring out the topic.

Example:

```
600 10 $a Freud, Sigmund, $d 1856-1939 $v Bibliography
650 #0 $a Psychoanalysis $v Bibliography
655 #7 $a Bibliographies. $2 lcgft
```

LC practice: If the bibliography has a topical orientation, assign a heading to bring out the topic in addition to the name of the person or persons. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Bibliographies* and/or a more specific term, e.g. *Thematic catalogs (Music)*.
