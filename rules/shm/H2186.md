# H 2186 Testing

## Validation rules

1. `Testing` is the heading for general works on processes or methods of testing. (H 2186 sec. 1)
2. `Psychological tests` (May Subd Geog) is the heading for general works on psychological tests and testing. (H 2186 sec. 2.a)
3. `--Testing` is free-floating under specific psychological topics, traits, etc., e.g. `Motivation (Psychology)--Testing`. (H 2186 sec. 2.b)
4. `--Psychological testing` (May Subd Geog) is free-floating under classes of persons, ethnic groups, and animals, e.g. `People with mental disabilities--Psychological testing`, `Dogs--Psychological testing`. (H 2186 sec. 2.c)
5. `--Intelligence testing` (May Subd Geog) is free-floating under classes of persons and ethnic groups (not animals), e.g. `Children--Intelligence testing`. (H 2186 sec. 2.c)
6. `Ability--Testing` is the heading for general works on testing ability, aptitude, or proficiency. (H 2186 sec. 3.a)
7. `--Ability testing` (May Subd Geog) is free-floating under topical headings, e.g. `Reading--Ability testing`, `Soccer--Ability testing`. (H 2186 sec. 3.b)
8. Where a phrase heading for a type of ability exists (e.g. `Athletic ability`, `Creative ability`, `Mathematical ability`), use `[type of ability]--Testing` instead of `[topic]--Ability testing`: `Athletic ability--Testing`, not `Athletics--Ability testing`. (H 2186 sec. 3.c)
9. `--Testing` is free-floating under `Drugs` and individual drugs, e.g. `Aspirin--Testing`. (H 2186 sec. 4)
10. Under other chemicals tested for use as drugs use `--Therapeutic use--Testing`, e.g. `Lithium--Therapeutic use--Testing`. (H 2186 sec. 4)
11. Testing for personal drug use: `Drug testing` (May Subd Geog) or `[class of persons]--Drug testing` (May Subd Geog), e.g. `Employees--Drug testing`. (H 2186 sec. 4)
12. `--Testing` is free-floating under materials, instruments, equipment, products, chemicals, domestic animals, etc., e.g. `Automobiles--Testing`, `Concrete--Testing`, `Corn--Varieties--Testing`. (H 2186 sec. 5)
13. Do not use `--Testing` for examinations on a subject or given by an institution; use `[topic or individual corporate body]--Examinations`. (H 2186 sec. 5)
14. If a phrase heading exists for the concept (e.g. `Intelligence tests`, `Psychological tests for children`, `Toxicity testing`), use it instead of `[topic]--Testing` or `[class of persons or ethnic group]--Psychological testing`. (H 2186 sec. 6)
15. `--Evaluation` is governed by H 1591. (H 2186 background)

## Full text

BACKGROUND: Testing is a process or method of trying or demonstrating something to determine its quality, genuineness, or suitability, or the use of questions, problems, or tasks to measure and evaluate skills, abilities, traits, capacities, etc. This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning the headings Testing and Psychological tests and the subdivisions --Testing, --Psychological testing, --Intelligence testing, and --Ability testing. For instructions on assigning the heading and subdivision Evaluation, see H 1591.

### 1. General works

Assign the heading Testing to general works on processes or methods of testing.

### 2. Psychology

#### a. General

Assign the heading Psychological tests (May Subd Geog) to general works on psychological tests and testing, including methods of test construction and application.

#### b. Psychological topics

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Testing under specific psychological topics, traits, etc., for works on the construction, application, and results of testing pertaining to these topics, for example, Motivation (Psychology)--Testing.

#### c. Classes of persons, ethnic groups, and animals

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Psychological testing (May Subd Geog) under classes of persons, ethnic groups, and animals to works on methods and results of conducting psychological tests of these groups, for example, People with mental disabilities--Psychological testing.

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Intelligence testing (May Subd Geog) under classes of persons and ethnic groups for works on methods and results of conducting intelligence tests of these groups, for example, Children--Intelligence testing.

### 3. Ability

#### a. General

Assign the heading Ability--Testing to general works on tests and testing of ability, aptitude, or proficiency.

#### b. --Ability testing

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Ability testing (May Subd Geog) under topical headings for works on tests and testing of native aptitude or acquired proficiency in a particular topic, for example, Reading--Ability testing; Soccer--Ability testing.

#### c. Phrase headings for ability

Where phrase headings have been established for particular types of ability, for example, Athletic ability, Creative ability, Mathematical ability, use the free-floating subdivision --Testing under the heading for type of ability for works on tests and testing of these abilities instead of the subdivision --Ability testing under the heading for the corresponding topic. Example:

```
650 #0 $a Athletic ability $x Testing.
  [not 650 #0 $a Athletics $x Ability testing.]
```

### 4. Drugs

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Testing under Drugs and individual drugs to works on testing their effectiveness and/or safety, for example, Aspirin--Testing.

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Therapeutic use--Testing under other chemicals when their use as drugs is being tested, for example, Lithium--Therapeutic use--Testing.

To works on testing to identify the personal use or misuse of drugs, assign the general heading Drug testing (May Subd Geog) or the free-floating subdivision --Drug testing (May Subd Geog) under classes of persons being tested, for example, Employees--Drug testing.

### 5. Other topics

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Testing under headings for materials, instruments, equipment, products, chemicals, domestic animals, etc., to works on the testing of these items and/or the results of testing. Examples:

```
650 #0 $a Automobiles $x Testing.
650 #0 $a Concrete $x Testing.
650 #0 $a Copper $x Testing.
650 #0 $a Corn $x Varieties $x Testing.
650 #0 $a Road materials $x Testing.
```

Do not use this subdivision for works on examinations given on a particular subject or administered by an institution. To these works, assign a heading of the type [topic or individual corporate body]--Examinations.

### 6. Subdivisions vs. phrase headings

Before assigning a heading of the type [topic]--Testing or [class of persons or ethnic group]--Psychological testing, search the subject authority file to determine whether a phrase heading already exists for the concept. If a phrase heading has been established, assign the heading instead of using the free-floating subdivision --Testing or --Psychological testing under the topic or group. Examples:

```
650 #0 $a Intelligence tests.
650 #0 $a Psychological tests for children.
650 #0 $a Toxicity testing.
```

### 7. Assigning headings

Assign several headings, if appropriate, to bring out the most important aspects of the work being cataloged. Examples:

```
Title: The testing of concrete in structures.
650 #0 $a Concrete $x Testing.
650 #0 $a Concrete construction $x Testing.

Title: Passenger comfort and safety : test tools and procedures.
650 #0 $a Automobiles $x Testing.
650 #0 $a Automobiles $x Safety measures.

Title: Assessment of exceptional students.
650 #0 $a Children with disabilities $x Education $z United States.
650 #0 $a Children with disabilities $x Psychological testing $z United States.
650 #0 $a Educational tests and measurements $z United States.
650 #0 $a Special education $z United States.

Title: How to test your dog's IQ.
650 #0 $a Dogs $x Psychological testing.
650 #0 $a Animal intelligence $x Testing.
```
