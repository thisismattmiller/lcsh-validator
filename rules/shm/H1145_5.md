# H 1145.5 Free-floating Subdivisions: Bodies of Water

## Validation rules

1. The following subdivisions are free-floating only under names of individual bodies of water: `--Alluvial plain`, `--Channelization`, `--Channels`, `--Fertilization`, `--Navigation`, `--Navigation--Law and legislation`, `--Power utilization`, `--Regulation`, `--Water rights`. None is marked May Subd Geog. (H 1145.5 sec. G)
2. The category covers names of individual bodies of water, including oceans, seas, bays, rivers, lakes, man-made lakes, reservoirs, estuaries, and canals, e.g. `North Atlantic Ocean`, `California, Gulf of (Mexico)`, `Colorado River (Colo.-Mexico)`, `Glacier Bay (Alaska)`, `Suez Canal (Egypt)`, `Superior, Lake`, `Tug Fork`, `Wolfgangsee (Austria)`, `Potomac River Estuary`. (H 1145.5 sec. B)
3. Do not use these subdivisions under headings for types of bodies of water (e.g. `Lakes`, `Saline waters`, `Sounds (Geomorphology)`) or under national rivers and wild and scenic rivers (e.g. `New River Gorge National River (W. Va.)`, `Obed Wild and Scenic River (Wash.)`). (H 1145.5 sec. C)
4. Appropriate subdivisions from H 1140 may also be used under names of bodies of water. (H 1145.5 sec. D)
5. A listed subdivision is free-floating under any heading in the category if appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. (H 1145.5 sec. E)
6. Further subdivisions under `--Navigation--Law and legislation` follow H 1154.4 (legal topics). (H 1145.5 sec. G)
7. `Region` may be added to a body-of-water heading to form a free-floating phrase heading; if the heading has a parenthetical qualifier, insert `Region` before the qualifier, e.g. `Tahoe, Lake, Region (Calif. and Nev.)`, `Danube River Region`, `Penobscot Bay Region (Me.)` (cf. H 800 sec. 10, H 760). (H 1145.5 sec. H)
8. `--Delta` is no longer a free-floating subdivision under names of rivers (discontinued 1991); deltas are established per H 800 sec. 6. (H 1145.5 sec. I.1)
9. `Estuary` (discontinued 1991), `Watershed` (discontinued March 1993), and `Valley` (discontinued July 1995) are no longer free-floating terms added to names of rivers/lakes/river systems; establish them per H 800 secs. 7, 9, 8. (H 1145.5 secs. I.2-I.4)
10. [LC practice] An exceptional variant form retained in the authority file gets a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating form (H 195); otherwise propose a change of the variant form (H 193). (H 1145.5 sec. F)

## Full text

### A. Recent revisions

2025-05: Updated template.

### B. Types of headings covered

Names of individual bodies of water, including oceans, seas, bays, rivers, lakes, man-made lakes, reservoirs, estuaries, and canals.

Examples:

```
North Atlantic Ocean
California, Gulf of (Mexico)
Colorado River (Colo.-Mexico)
Glacier Bay (Alaska)
Suez Canal (Egypt)
Superior, Lake
Tug Fork
Wolfgangsee (Austria)
Potomac River Estuary
```

### C. Types of headings not covered

The category does not include headings for types of bodies of water, such as *Lakes*; *Saline waters*; *Sounds (Geomorphology)*. The category also does not include headings for national rivers and wild and scenic rivers, such as *New River Gorge National River (W. Va.)*; *Obed Wild and Scenic River (Wash.)*.

### D. Relation to H 1140

Appropriate subdivisions on the list of subdivisions used under names of places, H 1140, may also be used under names of bodies of water. This list contains the additional subdivisions authorized for use only under individual bodies of water.

### E. Conflicts

Any subdivision listed here can be used as a free-floating subdivision under any heading belonging to the category if it is appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. Subject authority records may exist for headings employing variant phrases or subdivisions equivalent to a subdivision on this list.

### F. LC practice

If an exceptional variant form is to be retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form following the procedures in H 195 if the reference does not yet exist. Otherwise, submit a proposal to change the variant form along with all bibliographic records requiring correction following the procedures in H 193.

### H. Regions based on bodies of water

The term *Region* may be added to existing headings for names of bodies of water to form free-floating phrase headings (cf. H 800, sec. 10). If the existing heading has a parenthetical qualifier, add the term before the qualifier.

Examples:

```
Tahoe, Lake, Region (Calif. and Nev.)
Danube River Region
Penobscot Bay Region (Me.)
```

For complete instructions on the construction and use of headings for regions based on geographic features, see H 760.

### I. Former free-floating terms

#### I.1. --Delta

Use of the free-floating subdivision --Delta under names of rivers was discontinued in 1991. For instructions on establishing deltas, see H 800, sec. 6.

#### I.2. Estuary

The use of "Estuary" as a free-floating term added to names of rivers was discontinued in 1991. For instructions on establishing estuaries, see H 800, sec. 7.

#### I.3. Watershed

The use of "Watershed" as a free-floating term added to names of rivers and lakes was discontinued in March 1993. For instructions on establishing watersheds, see H 800, sec. 9.

#### I.4. Valley

The use of "Valley" as a free-floating term added to names of river systems was discontinued in July 1995. For instructions on establishing valleys, see H 800, sec. 8.
