# H 1325 Bibliography of Bibliographies

## Validation rules

1. For a list of bibliographies not limited to any specific topic, use the heading `Bibliography of bibliographies`. (H 1325 sec. F)
2. For a list of bibliographies on a specific topic, use `[topic]--Bibliography of bibliographies` (free-floating form subdivision, $v), e.g. `Outdoor recreation--Bibliography of bibliographies`; `Latin America--Bibliography of bibliographies`; `Shakespeare, William, 1564-1616--Bibliography of bibliographies`. (H 1325 sec. G)
3. Do not use the obsolete pre-1998 forms `Bibliography--Bibliography` or `Bibliography--Bibliography--[topic]` (e.g. `Bibliography--Bibliography--Botany`); use `Bibliography of bibliographies` or `[topic]--Bibliography of bibliographies`. (H 1325 sec. A)
4. [LC practice] LC catalogers no longer use `--Bibliography of bibliographies`; they assign the unsubdivided topic heading plus LCGFT *Bibliographies*. The subdivided form remains valid LCSH. (H 1325 sec. A, G)

## Full text

### A. Background

Prior to 1998, lists of bibliographies on specific topics were entered under the heading *Bibliography--Bibliography*, further subdivided by the appropriate topic, for example, *Bibliography--Bibliography--Botany*. In 1998, the heading *Bibliography--Bibliography* was changed to *Bibliography of bibliographies* and the free-floating form subdivision *--Bibliography of bibliographies* was established for use under subjects. This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning headings to works that are bibliographies of bibliographies.

LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. *Bibliographies*.

For guidelines on abstracts, see H 1205. For guidelines on bibliographies of individual people, see H 1322. For guidelines on bio-bibliographies, see H 1328. For catalogs of library materials, see H 1361. For guidelines on digests of legal topics, see H 1550.

### B. Recent revisions

### C. Definitions

Bibliography of bibliographies: list of bibliographies

### F. General rule

(Section lettering jumps from C to F in the source.)

Assign the heading *Bibliography of bibliographies* to a work that consists of a list of bibliographies not limited to any specific topic.

### G. Bibliography of bibliographies on specific topics

To a work that consists of a list of bibliographies on a specific topic, assign the appropriate heading for the topic, subdivided by the free-floating form subdivision *--Bibliography of bibliographies*.

LC practice: Assign a subject heading to bring out the topic. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Bibliographies*.

Examples:

```
600 10 $a Shakespeare, William $d 1564-1616 $v Bibliography of bibliographies.
650 #0 $a Outdoor recreation $v Bibliography of bibliographies.
651 #0 $a Latin America $v Bibliography of bibliographies.
LC practice:
6XX #0 $a [topic].
655 #7 $a Bibliographies. $2 lcgft
```
