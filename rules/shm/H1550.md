# H 1550 Digests

## Validation rules

1. `--Digests` is a free-floating form subdivision (`$v Digests`) used under legal headings for monographic or serial works consisting of systematically arranged compilations of brief summaries of individual statutes, regulations, court decisions, or regulatory agency decisions on particular topics, e.g. `Constitutional law--United States--Digests`; `Criminal law--India--Digests`. (H 1550 sec. D)
2. Do not further subdivide `--Digests` by `--Periodicals`; `[heading]--Digests--Periodicals` is invalid. (H 1550 sec. D)
3. `--Digests` follows the legal topic and its geographic subdivision, e.g. `Civil service positions--Classification--Law and legislation--United States--Digests`. (H 1550 sec. E)
4. [LC practice] LC catalogers no longer use `--Digests`; assign subject headings for the topic of the digest and `655 #7 $a Law digests. $2 lcgft`. (H 1550 sec. A, D)

## Full text

### A. Background

LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. *Law digests*.

For guidelines on abstracts, see H 1205. For guidelines on bibliographies of individual persons, see H 1322. For guidelines on bio-bibliographies, see H 1328. For guidelines on bibliographies of bibliographies, see H 1325. For catalogs of library materials, see H 1361.

### B. Recent revisions

2026-02: Updated template. Added instructions related to LCGFT implementation.

### C. Definitions

Digests: systematically arranged compilations of brief summaries of individual statutes, regulations, court decisions, or regulatory agency decisions on particular topics

### D. General rule

Assign the subdivision *--Digests* as a free-floating form subdivision under legal headings for monographic or serial works consisting of systematically arranged compilations of brief summaries of individual statutes, regulations, court decisions, or regulatory agency decisions on particular topics. Do not further subdivide *--Digests* by *--Periodicals*.

LC practice: Assign subject headings for the topic of the digest. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Law digests*.

### E. Examples
