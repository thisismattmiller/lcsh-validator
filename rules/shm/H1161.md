# H 1161 Pattern Headings: Musical Instruments

## Validation rules

1. The category covers headings for individual instruments, including brands and models of instruments, and families of instruments (e.g. `Flute`, `Fender guitar`, `Viols`, `Wind instruments`). The general heading `Musical instruments` is not in the category. (H 1161, Types of headings)
2. Any subdivision on the list is free-floating under any heading in the category if appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file; established variant phrases/subdivisions may exist. (H 1161, Conflicts)
3. Only `--Construction`, `--Customizing`, `--Instruction and study`, `--Performance`, `--Tuning`, and the form subdivision `--Catalogs and collections` are marked (May Subd Geog); the other listed subdivisions are not geographically subdivided. (H 1161 list)
4. `--Methods` and `--Studies and exercises` may be qualified by a style of music only with the styles given on the list, in the exact form shown, e.g. `--Methods (Bluegrass)`, `--Studies and exercises (Rock)`, `--Methods (Popular music)`. (H 1161 list)
5. `--Group instruction` and `--Self-instruction` may follow `--Methods (style)` only for the style combinations listed; `--Methods (Barrelhouse)` takes only `--Group instruction`; `--Methods (Tangos)` takes neither. Unqualified `--Methods` takes `--Group instruction`, `--Juvenile`, `--Self-instruction`. (H 1161 list)
6. `--Studies and exercises` qualified by style is not further subdivided on the list; unqualified `--Studies and exercises` may take `--Juvenile`. `--Studies and exercises (Left hand)` and `--Studies and exercises (Right hand)` are also valid. There is no `--Studies and exercises (Barrelhouse)` or `--Studies and exercises (Tangos)` on the list. (H 1161 list)
7. When `--Methods` or `--Studies and exercises` qualified by a style of music is used, assign an additional heading for the style of music subdivided by `--Instruction and study`, e.g. `Banjo--Methods (Bluegrass)` plus `Bluegrass music--Instruction and study`. (H 1161 note 1)
8. `--Religious aspects` may be further subdivided by a religion or Christian denomination; each heading of the type `[instrument]--Religious aspects--[name of religion or denomination]` must be editorially established. See H 1998. (H 1161 note 2)
9. Most `$v` form subdivisions on this list may also be used as `$x` topical subdivisions for works about the form. (H 1161, list note)
10. [LC practice] An exceptional variant form to be retained gets a UF reference from the free-floating form (H 195); otherwise propose a change (H 193). (H 1161, Conflicts)

## Full text

PATTERN: *Piano*; *Clarinet*; *Violin*

TYPES OF HEADINGS COVERED BY THE PATTERN: Headings for individual instruments, including brands and models of instruments, and families of instruments. Examples: Flute; Fender guitar; Viols; Wind instruments. This category does not include the general heading Musical instruments.

CONFLICTS: Any subdivision listed here can be used as a free-floating subdivision under any heading belonging to the category if it is appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. Subject authority records may exist for headings employing variant phrases or subdivisions equivalent to subdivisions on this list.

LC practice:
If an exceptional variant form is to be retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form following the procedures in H 195 if the reference does not yet exist. Otherwise, submit a proposal to change the variant form along with all bibliographic records requiring correction following the procedures in H 193.

### NOTE

1. When using the subdivisions –Methods or –Studies and exercises qualified by a style of music, assign an additional heading for the style of music subdivided by –Instruction and study. Examples:

```
650 #0 $a Banjo $v Methods (Bluegrass)
650 #0 $a Bluegrass music $x Instruction and study.

650 #0 $a Guitar $v Studies and exercises (Rock)
650 #0 $a Rock music $x Instruction and study.
```

2. May be subdivided topically by a religion or Christian denomination. Editorially establish each heading of the type [instrument]–Religious aspects–[name of religion or denomination]. For instructions, see H 1998.
