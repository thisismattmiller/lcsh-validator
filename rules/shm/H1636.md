# H 1636 Gods of Greek and Roman Mythology

## Validation rules

1. Broader terms for Greek and Roman gods take the inverted forms `Gods, Greek`, `Gods, Roman`, `Goddesses, Greek`, `Goddesses, Roman` (`Gods, [Greek or Roman]` / `Goddesses, [Greek or Roman]`). (H 1636 Background)
2. Since July 2013, individual Greek and Roman gods and goddesses are established as RDA name authority records, not LCSH; groups of gods and goddesses continue to be established in LCSH (see H 1635). (H 1636 Background)

## Full text

BACKGROUND: Until July 2013, headings for individual Greek and Roman gods or goddesses were established in LCSH. Where equivalencies between Greek and Roman gods could be determined, a 550 (related term) field was added to the record for each heading, and each had a BT in the form:

```
550 ## $w g $a Gods, [Greek or Roman]
   or 550 ## $w g $a Goddesses, [Greek or Roman]
```

After July 2013, headings for individual gods and goddesses are established as RDA name authority records. Groups of gods and goddesses continue to be established in LCSH.

See H 1635 for guidelines on establishing groups of gods and goddesses, and the procedures for cancelling subject headings for individual named gods and goddesses when name authority records have been created.
