# H 1710 Legal Materials: Legal Research

## Validation rules

1. `--Legal research` is used under inherently legal headings, including topical headings with `--Law and legislation` or `--Legal status, laws, etc.`, for works on using legal research tools (court reports, codes, digests, citators, etc.). (H 1710 sec. 1)
2. `--Legal research` is free-floating when so used, being controlled by the pattern heading for legal topics, `Labor laws and legislation`. (H 1710 sec. 1)
3. `--Legal research` is not further subdivided by place; the geographic subdivision precedes it: `Taxation--Law and legislation--United States--Legal research`. (H 1710 sec. 1)
4. `--Research` under legal topics follows normal topical practice (descriptions of proposed research) and may be further subdivided by place: `Consumer protection--Law and legislation--Research--United States`, `Consumer credit--Law and legislation--Research--United States`. (H 1710 sec. 2)

## Full text

BACKGROUND: This instruction sheet describes the use of the subdivisions --Legal research and --Research under legal topics.

### 1. --Legal research.

Use this subdivision under inherently legal headings, including topical headings with the subdivision --Law and legislation or --Legal status, laws, etc., for works which discuss the use of legal research tools such as court reports, codes, digests, citators, etc., in determining the status of statutory, regulatory, or case law on a topic. Example:

```
Title: How to find current tax law.
650 #0 $a Taxation $x Law and legislation $z United States $x Legal research.
```

--Legal research is controlled by the pattern heading for legal topics, Labor laws and legislation, and is therefore free-floating when used according to these guidelines. As illustrated in the above example, the subdivision is not further subdivided by place.

### 2. --Research.

Use this subdivision, further subdivided by place when appropriate, under legal topics in accordance with the normal practice for its use under other topical headings, i.e. for descriptions of proposed research, including such details as management, finance, personnel, special projects, methodology, goals, etc.. Example:

```
Title: Threats to validity in quasi-experimental evaluations of consumer protection reforms : a critical review of extant research.
650 #0 $a Consumer protection $x Law and legislation $x Research $z United States.
650 #0 $a Consumer credit $x Law and legislation $x Research $z United States.
```
