# H 1895 Miniature Books

## Validation rules

1. `Miniature books` (May Subd Geog) is the topical heading for works discussing books 10 centimeters (100 millimeters) or less in both height and width, e.g. `Miniature books--Russia--History`; `Miniature books--United States--Bibliography`. (H 1895 sec. 1, 3)
2. `Miniature books--Specimens` is the form heading for actual miniature books and is assigned without geographic subdivision (no `Miniature books--[place]--Specimens`). (H 1895 sec. 1)
3. Assign `Miniature books--Specimens` to a miniature book in addition to its other topical or form headings. (H 1895 sec. 2)
4. Do not use the obsolete headings `Bibliography--Microscopic and miniature editions` or `Bibliography--Miniature and microscopic editions--Specimens`; use `Miniature books` and `Miniature books--Specimens`. (H 1895 Background)

## Full text

BACKGROUND: In 1985 the topical heading *Miniature books* replaced the obsolete heading *Bibliography--Microscopic and miniature editions*; the form heading *Miniature books--Specimens*, assigned to actual specimens of such books, replaced *Bibliography--Miniature and microscopic editions--Specimens*. This instruction sheet presents guidelines on the use of the topical subject heading *Miniature books* and the form heading *Miniature books--Specimens*.

### 1. Scope of the headings.

The topical heading *Miniature books* (May Subd Geog) is used for works that discuss books 10 centimeters (i.e. 100 millimeters) or less in both height and width.

The form heading *Miniature books--Specimens* is used for actual specimens of such books. This form heading is assigned without geographic subdivision.

### 2. Assignment of the form heading.

In addition to the appropriate topical or form headings, assign to a miniature book the form heading *Miniature books--Specimens*.

### 3. Examples.

```
Title: Russian miniature books : a history.
(29 cm.)
650 #0 $a Miniature books $z Russia $x History.

Title: American miniature books : a bibliography.
(48 mm.)
650 #0 $a Miniature books $z United States $v Bibliography.
650 #0 $a Miniature books $v Specimens.

Title: Romanian painting 1921-1971.
(50 mm.)
650 #0 $a Painting, Romanian $y 20th century.
650 #0 $a Miniature books $v Specimens.

Title: The selected poems of Longfellow.
(10 cm.)
650 #0 $a Miniature books $v Specimens.

Title: A bibliography of miniature book bibliographies.
(10 cm.)
650 #0 $a Miniature books $v Bibliography of bibliographies.
650 #0 $a Miniature books $v Specimens.
```
