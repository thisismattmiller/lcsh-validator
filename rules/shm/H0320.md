# H 320 Headings Qualified by Nationality, Ethnic Group, Language, etc.

## Validation rules

1. A heading with an adjectival qualifier for nationality, ethnic group, language, etc. is valid only if that specific qualified heading is established (e.g. `Investments, American`, `Art, American`, `Mythology, Armenian`, `Short stories, English`, `Mexican American children`); the existence of the unqualified heading does not authorize arbitrary qualification. (H 320 sec. 1)
2. Exception: an inverted subdivision with an adjectival qualifier may take any appropriate adjective without individual establishment when a multiple subdivision exists in the authority file or on a free-floating list, e.g. `World War, 1939-1945--Personal narratives, American`; `United States--Foreign public opinion, British`. (H 320 sec. 1; H 1090)
3. An uninverted subdivision with an adjectival qualifier (e.g. `--American influences`) for which no multiple exists must be individually established under each heading, e.g. `France--Civilization--American influences`. (H 320 sec. 1)
4. Qualified headings are inverted: `[Heading], [Adjective]` (e.g. `Art, French`, `College and school drama, French`). (H 320 sec. 2)
5. Exceptions in uninverted (direct) form: headings qualified by ethnic groups of the United States or by Indian groups (`African American children`, `Mexican American art`); major literary forms (H 306 sec. 2.a.(1), H 351) (`English poetry`, `French drama`); a few other well-established patterns (`French students`, `Indian women`, `American property`, `German imprints`); and headings with adjectives for religious groups including `Jewish` and `Islamic`. (H 320 sec. 2)
6. The alternate-order form of a qualified heading is a UF reference, not a valid heading: `French art` (for `Art, French`), `Art, Mexican American` (for `Mexican American art`), `French college and school drama` (for `College and school drama, French`). (H 320 sec. 3)
7. Use only the established form of a national or ethnic adjective; non-used variant adjectival forms are handled by general see references (H 374) and are invalid in headings. (H 320 sec. 3)
8. A subdivision established under the unqualified heading may also appear under the qualified heading, e.g. `Corporations--Investor relations` and `Corporations, American--Investor relations`. (H 320 sec. 4)

## Full text

### 1. General provision.

Establish editorially each instance of a subject heading with an adjectival qualifier for nationality, ethnic group, language, etc. Examples:

```
150 ## $a Investments, American
150 ## $a Art, American
150 ## $a Mythology, Armenian
150 ## $a Short stories, English
150 ## $a Mexican American children
```

Exception: In the case of inverted subdivisions with adjectival qualifiers, individual adjectives may be applied without establishing each usage if a multiple subdivision has been provided in the subject authority file or on a free-floating list. For instructions on multiple subdivisions, see H 1090. Examples:

```
650 #0 $a World War, 1939-1945 $v Personal narratives, American.
651 #0 $a United States $x Foreign public opinion, British.
```

If the subdivision is uninverted and no multiple has been provided, establish each instance as needed. Example:

```
151 ## $a France $x Civilization $x American influences.
```

### 2. Inverted or uninverted headings.

Establish all qualified headings in the inverted form except for those qualified by ethnic groups of the United States or by Indian groups, major literary forms (see H 306, sec. 2.a.(1); H 351), and a few other well-established patterns.

Note: Headings qualified by adjectives for religious groups, including the adjectives Jewish and Islamic, are established in uninverted form (cf. H 306, sec. 1).

Examples:

```
150 ## $a English poetry
150 ## $a African American children
150 ## $a French students
150 ## $a Indian women
150 ## $a American property
150 ## $a German imprints
```

### 3. References.

Add as a standard reference to a qualified heading of this type a 450 field with the alternative form of the heading. If the heading is inverted, make the reference from the straight form; if straight, make the reference from the inverted form. Examples:

```
150 ## $a Art, French
450 ## $a French art

150 ## $a Mexican American art
450 ## $a Art, Mexican American
```

Do not add references from non-used variant forms of national and ethnic adjectival qualifiers. Instead make general see references from non-used adjectival variants according to the procedures in H 374.

Add additional 4XX or 5XX fields as appropriate. Example:

```
150 ## $a College and school drama, French
450 ## $a French college and school drama
550 ## $w g $a French drama
```

### 4. References for subdivisions used under qualified headings.

When a subdivision established under the unqualified form of a heading has been provided with a complete reference structure, that reference structure should not normally be duplicated if the same subdivision is established under the qualified heading. Examples:

```
150 ## $a Corporations $x Investor relations
[unqualified heading with subdivision]
450 ## $a Investor relations (Corporations)
550 ## $w g $a Public relations $x Corporations
550 ## $w g $a Stockholders

150 ## $a Corporations, American $x Investor relations
[qualified heading with subdivision; no additional references needed]
```
