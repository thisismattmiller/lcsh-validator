# H 1206.5 Acronyms

## Validation rules

1. Use the heading `Acronyms` for general works about acronyms and for works about English acronyms; do not use `English language--Acronyms`. (H 1206.5 sec. 1, sec. 2.a)
2. Use `Acronyms--Dictionaries` for alphabetical lists of acronyms in multiple languages, and for lists of English-language acronyms. (H 1206.5 sec. 1, sec. 2.a)
3. For acronyms in a language other than English, use `[name of language or language group]--Acronyms`; for dictionaries, `[name of language or language group]--Acronyms--Dictionaries`. (H 1206.5 sec. 2.b)
4. --Acronyms is a free-floating topical subdivision ($x) under topical headings for works about acronyms pertaining to the topic, e.g. `Administrative agencies--United States--Acronyms`. (H 1206.5 sec. 3)
5. For dictionaries of acronyms on a topic, the order is `[topic]--Acronyms--Dictionaries` (--Dictionaries as $v after $x Acronyms). (H 1206.5 sec. 3)
6. For works about topical acronyms in a language other than English, also assign `[language]--Acronyms`. (H 1206.5 sec. 3)
7. For topical dictionaries of acronyms in a language other than English, further divide `[topic]--Acronyms--Dictionaries` by the name of the language (e.g. `[topic]--Acronyms--Dictionaries--French`) and assign an additional heading `[language]--Acronyms--Dictionaries`. (H 1206.5 sec. 3)

## Full text

### Background

An acronym is a word formed from the first, or first few, letters of a series of words. This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning the heading *Acronyms* and the free-floating subdivision --Acronyms.

### 1. General

Assign the heading *Acronyms* to general works about acronyms. Assign the heading *Acronyms--Dictionaries* to works that consist of alphabetical lists of acronyms in multiple languages, together with their meanings.

### 2. By language

#### a. English

Assign the heading *Acronyms* to works about English acronyms. Assign the heading *Acronyms--Dictionaries* to works that consist of alphabetical lists of acronyms in the English language.

#### b. Other languages

Assign the heading *[name of language or language group]--Acronyms* to works about acronyms in that language or language group. Assign the heading *[name of language or language group]--Acronyms--Dictionaries* to works that consist of alphabetical lists of acronyms in that language or language group, together with their meanings.

### 3. By topic

Assign the heading for the appropriate topic subdivided by the free-floating subdivision --Acronyms to works about acronyms pertaining to the topic. For works that consist of alphabetical lists of acronyms pertaining to a topic, further subdivide *[topic]--Acronyms* by the free-floating subdivision --Dictionaries.

For works about acronyms in a language other than English, assign an additional heading for *[language]--Acronyms*. For dictionaries of acronyms in a language other than English, further divide the heading *[topic]--Acronyms--Dictionaries* by the name of the language and assign an additional heading *[language]--Acronyms--Dictionaries*.

Examples:

```
Title:  Guide to federal government acronyms.
650 #0 $a Administrative agencies $z United States $x Acronyms.
650 #0 $a Executive departments $z United States $x Acronyms.

Title:  Dictionary of medical and health acronyms.
650 #0 $a Medicine $x Acronyms $v Dictionaries.
650 #0 $a Health $x Acronyms $v Dictionaries.
```
