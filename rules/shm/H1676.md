# H 1676 Inquisition

## Validation rules

1. Use the topical heading `Inquisition` (May Subd Geog) for works on any aspect of the Inquisition, subdivided geographically as needed, e.g. `Inquisition--Canary Islands`. (H 1676 sec. 1)
2. The former corporate name form `Inquisition. [place]` (AACR1) is replaced and should not be used. (H 1676 Background)
3. Do not subdivide `Inquisition` by `--History` or by `--History--[...] century`, and do not establish period subdivisions under it. (H 1676 sec. 2)
4. Bring out other aspects with additional headings, e.g. a defendant's name or `[place]--Church history` (`Canary Islands--Church history`). (H 1676 sec. 3)
