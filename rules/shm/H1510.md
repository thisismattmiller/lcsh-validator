# H 1510 Cross-cultural Studies

## Validation rules

1. `--Cross-cultural studies` is a free-floating subdivision under topical headings: form (`$v`) for works reporting results of cross-cultural studies, e.g. `Suicide--Cross-cultural studies`; topical (`$x`) for works on methods of conducting them, e.g. `Family--Cross-cultural studies`. (H 1510 sec. D)
2. Use it only for topics that are cultural traits or culturally modifiable phenomena (e.g. `Breastfeeding`, `Childbirth`, `Memory in children`, `Visual perception`); physiological topics qualify only if modifiable by the social environment or socialization. (H 1510 sec. E.1)
3. Do not use `--Cross-cultural studies` under names of individual ethnic groups or for works about a single ethnic group; at least two cultures or societies must be compared. (H 1510 sec. E.2)
4. [LC practice] LC does not use `$v Cross-cultural studies`; it assigns the topical heading and LCGFT `Cross-cultural studies`. The topical `$x Cross-cultural studies` (methods) is unchanged. (H 1510 sec. A, D, E.1)

## Full text

### A. Background

Cross-cultural studies are studies in which comparable data on cultural traits or behavior from two or more cultures or societies are presented. These studies may assess similarities or diversities occurring in those cultures or test hypotheses concerning individual or group behavior. This instruction sheet provides guidelines on the usage of the free-floating subdivision *--Cross-cultural studies*.

LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. For resources that would use form subdivisions, assign the LCGFT term appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand.

### B. Recent revisions

2025-02: Updated template. Added alternative instructions for LCGFT implementation.

### C. Definitions

Cross-cultural studies: works that report the results of cross-cultural studies or discuss the methods and techniques of conducting such studies.

### D. General rule

Use the free-floating subdivision *--Cross-cultural studies* as a form subdivision under topical headings for works that report the results of cross-cultural studies on those topics or as a topical subdivision for works that discuss the methods and techniques of conducting such studies.

LC practice: Assign the appropriate subject headings for the topical focus of the resource. Do not use the free-floating subdivision *--Cross-cultural studies* as a form subdivision for works that report the results of cross-cultural studies. Instead, assign the LCGFT term appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Cross-cultural studies*.

Example:

```
Title: Suicide in different cultures.
650 $0 $a Suicide $v Cross-cultural studies.
LC practice:
650 #0 $a Suicide.
655 #7 $a Cross-cultural studies. $2 lcgft

Title: Studying in the family cross-culturally.
650 #0 $a Family $x Cross-cultural studies.
LC practice:
No change.
```

### E. Restrictions on usage

### E.1. General

Reserve use of the subdivision *--Cross-cultural studies* to designate works that compare cultural traits or culturally modifiable phenomena.

Topics such as physiological processes may be compared, but the topic in question should be one that is modifiable by the social environment or through the socialization process.

LC practice: Assign the appropriate subject headings for the topical focus of the resource. Also assign the LCGFT term appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Cross-cultural studies*.

Examples:

```
650 #0 $a Breastfeeding $v Cross-cultural studies.
LC practice:
650 #0 $a Breastfeeding.
655 #7 Cross-cultural studies. $2 lcgft

650 #0 $a Childbirth $v Cross-cultural studies.
LC practice:
650 #0 $a Childbirth.
655 #7 Cross-cultural studies. $2 lcgft

650 #0 $a Memory in children $v Cross-cultural studies.
LC practice:
650 #0 $a Memory in children.
655 #7 Cross-cultural studies. $2 lcgft

650 #0 $a Visual perception $v Cross-cultural studies.
LC practice:
650 #0 $a Visual perception.
655 #7 Cross-cultural studies. $2 lcgft
```

### E.2. Ethnic groups

Do not use the subdivision *--Cross-cultural studies* under names of individual ethnic groups or for works that discuss a single ethnic group. The work must compare traits in at least two or more cultures or societies in order to designate it a cross-cultural study.
