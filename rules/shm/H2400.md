# H 2400 Yearbooks

## Validation rules

1. The form subdivision `--Yearbooks` was discontinued in 1986 in favor of `--Periodicals`. Do not use `--Yearbooks` under headings assigned to any new work, except as `--Students--Yearbooks` (rule 4). A string such as `Agriculture--Yearbooks` is invalid; use `Agriculture--Periodicals`. (H 2400 Background; sec. 1)
2. To all serials issued indefinitely at regular or stated intervals (weekly, monthly, yearly, biennially, etc.), assign headings with the free-floating form subdivision `--Periodicals` (see H 1927). Annual publications therefore take `--Periodicals`, not `--Yearbooks`. (H 2400 sec. 1)
3. `--Yearbooks` may still be found on older existing records; it is obsolete there, not a currently valid subdivision. When encountered during revision for other reasons, it should be changed to `--Periodicals`. (H 2400 secs. 2–3)
4. `--Students--Yearbooks` is a valid free-floating subdivision, used only under names of individual educational institutions for student yearbooks from those institutions, e.g., `Bowdoin College--Students--Yearbooks`. `--Yearbooks` in this sense must follow `--Students`. (H 2400 sec. 4)
5. [LC practice] Do not make any systematic attempt to correct `--Yearbooks` on existing bibliographic records; but when the subject headings on an individual record are being revised or updated for other reasons, change `--Yearbooks` to `--Periodicals`. (H 2400 sec. 2)
6. [LC practice] When cataloging a continuation of a serial whose earlier titles were assigned headings with `--Yearbooks`, update the MARC records of the earlier titles so that the headings on all titles match (i.e., all use `--Periodicals`). (H 2400 sec. 3)

## Full text

BACKGROUND: Until 1986, the subdivision --Yearbooks was used as a form subdivision under headings assigned to publications appearing annually, each volume of which summarizes the accomplishments or events of a particular year within a specific discipline or area of endeavor. This subdivision was discontinued in 1986 in favor of the subdivision --Periodicals.

For classification of yearbooks, see F 230.

### 1. General rule

Do not use the subdivision --Yearbooks under headings assigned to any new work being cataloged, except as described in sec. 4, below. To all serials issued indefinitely at regular or stated intervals, whether weekly, monthly, yearly, biennially, etc., assign headings with the free-floating form subdivision --Periodicals. For instructions on using the subdivision --Periodicals, see H 1927.

### 2. Correcting existing bibliographic records

LC practice: Do not make any systematic attempt to correct the subdivision on existing bibliographic records. However, if the subject headings on an individual record are being revised or updated for other reasons, change --Yearbooks to --Periodicals whenever it is encountered.

### 3. Serial continuations

LC practice: If a continuation of a serial is being cataloged, and earlier titles have been assigned headings with the subdivision --Yearbooks, update MARC records of the earlier titles so that the headings on all titles match.

### 4. Student yearbooks

Use the free-floating subdivision --Students--Yearbooks under names of individual educational institutions for student yearbooks from those institutions, for example, Bowdoin College--Students--Yearbooks.
