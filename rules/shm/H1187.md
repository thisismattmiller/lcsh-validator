# H 1187 Pattern Headings: Christian Denominations

## Validation rules

1. Subdivisions on this list are free-floating under headings for any named Christian denomination or sect, whether a corporate body in the name authority file (e.g., `United Methodist Church (U.S.)`) or a generic subject heading for a larger grouping (e.g., `Methodist Church`), e.g., African Methodist Episcopal Church; Baptists; Congregational churches; Orthodox Eastern Church, if appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. (H 1187, Types of headings; Conflicts)
2. The list also applies under headings for individual rites, e.g., `Catholic Church--Ambrosian rite`; `Catholic Church--Byzantine rite`. (H 1187, Types of headings)
3. Do not apply this list under the general headings `Christian sects` and `Protestant churches`, or under name headings for individual or local churches, congregations, dioceses, etc. (H 1187, Types of headings)
4. Religious and monastic orders use H 1186; religions in general use H 1185; names of denominations used as subdivisions are covered by H 2015. (H 1187, Types of headings)
5. Where a variant phrase or subdivision equivalent to a list subdivision is established in the subject authority file, the established variant takes precedence. (H 1187, Conflicts)
6. Most `$v` form subdivisions on the list may also be used as `$x` topical subdivisions for works about the form. (H 1187, Note; H 1075 sec. 1.d)
7. Chronological subdivisions under --History are limited on this list to: `$y 16th century`, `$y Modern period, 1500-`, `$y 17th century`, `$y 18th century`, `$y 19th century`, `$y 20th century`, `$y 1965-`, `$y 21st century`; under --Doctrines--History only `$y Modern period, 1500-` is listed. (H 1187, list)
8. Only the subdivisions marked (May Subd Geog) may be followed by a place: --Adult education, --Benefices, --Clergy--Deprivation of the clerical garb, --Clergy--Installation, --Clergy--Secular employment, --Dioceses, --Education, --Foreign relations, --Liturgical objects, --Missions, --Museums, --Political activity, --Publishing. (H 1187, list)
9. --Bishops, --Clergy, and --Employees are classes of persons; further subdivisions from H 1100 may be used after them. (H 1187, note 1)
10. --Relations is used per H 1996 secs. 1 and 3. (H 1187, note 2)
11. `--Catechisms--English, [French, German, etc.]` is coded `$v Catechisms $x English, [French, German, etc.]` as printed; works about catechisms use `$x Catechisms $x History and criticism`. (H 1187, list)
12. Fixed subfield codings under --Liturgy: `$x Liturgy $v Calendar`, `$x Liturgy $v Texts`, `$x Liturgy $x Texts $v Concordances`, `$x Liturgy $x Texts $x History and criticism`, `$x Liturgy $x Texts $v Illustrations`, `$x Liturgy $v Texts $v Manuscripts`, `$x Liturgy $x Texts $v Rubrics`, `$x Liturgy $x Theology`; and the separate inverted subdivision `--Liturgy, Experimental`. (H 1187, list)
13. `--Hymns--Texts` is coded `$v Hymns $v Texts`; `--In art` is coded `$v`, while `--In comics`, `--In literature`, `--In motion pictures` are `$x`. (H 1187, list)
14. [LC practice] If an exceptional variant form is retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating form (H 195); otherwise propose changing the variant (H 193). (H 1187, LC practice)

## Full text

PATTERN: *Catholic Church*

### Types of headings covered by the pattern

Headings for all named Christian denominations and sects. These headings may be headings for corporate bodies established in the name authority file, for example, *United Methodist Church (U.S.)*, or generic subject headings for larger groupings that are established in the subject authority file, for example, *Methodist Church*. Examples: African Methodist Episcopal Church; Baptists; Congregational churches; Orthodox Eastern Church. Also included are headings for individual rites, for example, *Catholic Church--Ambrosian rite*; *Catholic Church--Byzantine rite*. The category does not include the general headings *Christian sects* and *Protestant churches*; nor does it cover name headings for individual or local churches, congregations, dioceses, etc. For subdivisions used under names of individual religious and monastic orders, see H 1186. For the category representing headings for religions in general, see H 1185. For names of denominations used as subdivisions, see H 2015.

### Conflicts

Any subdivision listed here can be used as a free-floating subdivision under any heading belonging to the category if it is appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. Subject authority records may exist for headings employing variant phrases or subdivisions equivalent to subdivisions on this list.

LC practice: If an exceptional variant form is to be retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form following the procedures in H 195 if the reference does not yet exist. Otherwise, submit a proposal to change the variant form along with all bibliographic records requiring correction following the procedures in H 193.

Note: Most form subdivisions coded $v in this list may also be used as topical subdivisions coded $x when assigned to works about the form (see H 1075, sec. 1.d.).
