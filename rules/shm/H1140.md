# H 1140 Free-Floating Subdivisions: Names of Places

## Validation rules

1. The subdivisions on sec. E (topical), sec. F (form), and sec. G (areas and regions) may be used, within the limits of the usage notes, as free-floating subdivisions under geographic names: continents; regions; islands; countries; states, provinces, and equivalent jurisdictions; counties and other local jurisdictions larger than cities; metropolitan areas, suburban areas, and regions based on city names. (H 1140 sec. C)
2. Except as noted, the list may also be used under names of cities established as valid RDA name headings, extinct cities established as subject headings (H 715), and city sections, districts, or quarters (H 720). (H 1140 sec. C)
3. Appropriate subdivisions may also be used under geographic features and designated areas (parks, reserves, gardens, streets and roads, refugee camps, etc.), regions based on geographic features (H 760), and names of bodies of water (additional body-of-water subdivisions in H 1145.5). (H 1140 sec. C)
4. The list covers jurisdictional name headings for U.S.-recognized tribal governments, e.g. `Spokane Tribe of the Spokane Reservation, Washington`. (H 1140 sec. C)
5. Do not apply this list to names of celestial bodies and astronomical features; to international organizations and non-jurisdictional corporate bodies (e.g. `Commonwealth of Independent States`); or to corporate headings for agencies controlling parks (e.g. `Yosemite National Park (Agency : U.S.)`). (H 1140 sec. C)
6. Century subdivisions `--16th century`, `--17th century`, `--18th century`, `--19th century`, `--20th century`, `--21st century` are free-floating at any geographic level after `--Civilization`, `--Intellectual life`, `--Economic conditions`, `--History`, `--History, Military`, `--History, Naval`, `--Politics and government`, `--Social conditions`, and `--Social life and customs` (the list also gives them after `--Church history`, `--Environmental conditions`, and `--Religion`). (H 1140 secs. D, E)
7. Use these century subdivisions with caution under countries and other entities with established specific period subdivisions; assign them only where they do not conflict with chronological subdivisions established in the subject authority file. (H 1140 sec. D)
8. A subdivision may be followed by a geographic subdivision only if marked "Yes" on the list: `--Abstracting and indexing`, `--Antiquities--Collection and preservation`, `--Antiquities--Collectors and collecting`, `--Armed Forces`, `--Boundaries`, `--Commerce`, `--Foreign economic relations`, `--Foreign relations`, `--Kings and rulers--Travel`, `--Military relations`, `--Officials and employees`, `--Officials and employees--Accidents`, `--Officials and employees--Salaries, etc.`, `--Press coverage`, `--Relations`, `--Research`, `--Study and teaching`, `--Study and teaching--Law and legislation`. (H 1140 sec. E)
9. Use only under countries and regions larger than countries: `--Armed Forces`, `--Commercial policy`, `--Defenses` (and its subdivisions), `--Dependency on [place]`, `--Dependency on foreign countries`, `--Foreign public opinion` (and `--Foreign public opinion, Austrian, [British, etc.]`), `--Foreign relations` (and its subdivisions), `--Foreign relations administration`, `--Military policy` (and `--Religious aspects`), `--National Guard`, `--Territorial expansion`, `--Territories and possessions`, `--Commercial treaties` ($v). `--Economic integration` only under regions larger than countries. (H 1140 secs. E-F)
10. Do not use under cities: `--Aerial exploration`, `--Altitudes`, `--Annexation to [...]`, `--Capital and capitol`, `--Economic conditions--Regional disparities` (and with period subdivision), `--Gold discoveries`, `--History--Autonomy and independence movements`, `--History, Local`, `--History, Local--Collectibles`, `--Militia`, `--Naval militia`, `--Officials and employees--Salaries, etc.--Regional disparities`, `--Rural conditions`. (H 1140 sec. E)
11. Do not use `--Antiquities`, `--Antiquities--Collection and preservation`, `--Antiquities--Collectors and collecting`, or `--Antiquities, Byzantine [Celtic, Germanic, Phoenician, Roman, Slavic, Turkish]` under names of extinct cities. (H 1140 sec. E)
12. Use `--Buildings, structures, etc.` only under cities and city sections. (H 1140 sec. E)
13. Use `--Charters` (topical and form) only under states, counties, cities, etc., of the United States and Indigenous jurisdictions of the United States; under countries, etc., and cities other than those of the United States use `--Charters, grants, and privileges` (as printed in the topical list, $x) / `--Charters, grants, privileges` (as printed in the form list, $v). (H 1140 secs. E-F)
13a. Note the source inconsistency for `--Commercial treaties`: the topical ($x) entry says "Use only under states, counties, cities, etc., of the United States and under indigenous jurisdictions of the United States", while the form ($v) entry says "Use only under countries and regions larger than countries." (H 1140 secs. E-F)
14. Use `--Scheduled tribes` only under `India` and names of states, regions, cities, etc., of India. (H 1140 sec. E)
15. `--Eruption, [date]` and `--Eruptions` are used under names of volcanoes; format the date per H 1078. (H 1140 sec. E)
16. `--History, Local` is for collective histories of several local units under regions, countries, states, etc.; for a single locality use `[locality]--History`. (H 1140 sec. E)
17. Reciprocal trade between places takes paired headings `[Place A]--Commerce--[Place B]` and `[Place B]--Commerce--[Place A]`. (H 1140 sec. E)
18. `[Name of country]--Colonies` and `[Name of country]--Territories and possessions` may also be used under topical headings, e.g. `Education--Great Britain--Colonies`, `Taxation--United States--Territories and possessions` (further subdivisions per H 1149.5). (H 1140 sec. E)
19. `--Emigration and immigration--Religious aspects` and `--History--Religious aspects` may be subdivided by a religion or Christian denomination; each `[Place]--...--Religious aspects--[Name of religion or denomination]` heading is editorially established. (H 1140 sec. E)
20. For folklore originating in a place use `Folklore--[Place]`; `[Place]--Folklore` is for the place as a folklore theme ($x) or collections of folklore texts about it ($v). (H 1140 secs. E-F)
21. `--Photographs from space` ($v) is not used for cartographic materials. (H 1140 sec. F)
22. Free-floating area headings based on city names: `[city]...Metropolitan Area ([geographic qualifier])` and `...Suburban Area ([geographic qualifier])`; based on city names and geographic features: `...Region ([geographic qualifier])` (consult H 790). (H 1140 sec. G)
23. `--Biography--Dictionaries--French, [Italian, etc.]` is a multiple: substitute the language. (H 1140 sec. F)
24. [LC practice] LC catalogers no longer use form subdivisions; assign LCGFT (e.g. `Textbooks`) and, for demographic groups, LCDGT. Form subdivisions remain valid LCSH. (H 1140 secs. A, F)

## Full text

### A. Background

Until 1985, the Subject Cataloging Manual and Library of Congress Subject Headings provided two separate lists of subdivisions used under places, one for cities (H 1135) and one for regions, countries, etc. (H 1145). When the "city flip" was discontinued (cf. H 832), it was possible to resolve the few remaining discrepancies between the two lists and to consolidate them into a single list of subdivisions used, as applicable, under regions, countries, cities, etc. This instruction sheet superseded and replaced H 1135 and H 1145.

LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. *Textbooks*. To replace a form subdivision that includes a demographic group, also assign Library of Congress Demographic Groups Terms (LCDGT) to bring out the audience of the resource being cataloged, e.g. *German speakers* or *Arabic, Students of*.

### B. Recent revisions

2026-02: Corrected scope note under Charters, grants, privileges.

### C. Types of headings covered

The subdivisions listed below may be used, within the limitations of the usage notes, as free-floating subdivisions under headings for geographic place names including continents; regions; islands; countries; states, provinces, and equivalent jurisdictions; counties and other local jurisdictions larger than cities; and headings for metropolitan areas, suburban areas, and regions based on names of cities. They may also be used, except as noted, under names of cities established as valid RDA name headings, under names of extinct cities established as subject headings (in accordance with the provisions of H 715), and under names of city sections, districts, or quarters (in accordance with the provisions of H 720). Appropriate subdivisions may also be used under headings for geographic features and designated areas, including parks, reserves, gardens, streets and roads, refugee camps, etc., or regions based on geographic features following the guidelines for the assignment of such headings given in H 760. Appropriate subdivisions may also be used under names of bodies of water. For additional subdivisions used only under names of bodies of water, streams, etc., see H 1145.5. This list also covers the jurisdictional name headings established to represent the governments of tribes recognized by the United States government, for example, *Spokane Tribe of the Spokane Reservation, Washington*. This list does not cover names of celestial bodies and other astronomical features. This list also does not cover international organizations and corporate bodies that are not jurisdictions, for example, *Commonwealth of Independent States*, nor the corporate name headings that represent government agencies that control parks, etc., for example, *Yosemite National Park (Agency : U.S.)*.

### D. Chronological subdivisions

In 1997 restrictions were lifted on use of subdivisions of the type *--16th*, [*17th*, *18th*, *19th*, *20th*, *21st*] century under *--Civilization* and *--Intellectual life*, thereby allowing their use under geographic headings at any level. At the same time, subdivisions of the type *--16th*, [*17th*, *18th*, *19th*, *20th*, *21st*] century were added under the subdivisions *--Economic conditions*; *--History*; *--History, Military*; *--History, Naval*; *--Politics and government*; *--Social conditions*; and *--Social life and customs*. These subdivisions were added to facilitate subject access by time period, primarily by authorizing their free-floating use under places smaller than countries, where chronological subdivisions were previously not established or used. These free-floating century subdivisions should be used with caution under names of countries and other geographic entities where chronological subdivisions for specific time periods have been established. Assign these century subdivisions only where there is no conflict with chronological subdivisions established in the subject authority file.
