# H 1154.5 Pattern Headings: Legal Topics

## Validation rules

1. Subdivisions on this list are free-floating under headings for legal topics (e.g. *Torts*; *Civil procedure*; *Domestic relations*; *Airspace (International law)*), legal phrase headings (e.g. *Agricultural laws and legislation*; *Criminal law*; *Trade regulation*), and legal systems (e.g. *Islamic law*; *International law*). (H 1154.5, Types of headings covered)
2. The list may also follow topical headings subdivided by `--Law and legislation`, `--Legal status, laws, etc.`, or `--Safety regulations`, and place names subdivided by legal subdivisions (e.g. *[place]--Foreign relations--Law and legislation*). (H 1154.5, Types of headings covered)
3. New uses of `--Law and legislation` are established editorially (not free-floating); see H 1705 for usage of `--Law and legislation`, `--Legal status, laws, etc.`, and `--Safety regulations` under topical headings. (H 1154.5, Note)
4. A listed subdivision may be used free-floating if appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. (H 1154.5, Conflicts)
5. Only `--Compliance costs`, `--Environmental aspects`, and `--Research` on this list may be subdivided geographically. (H 1154.5, list)
6. Do not use `--Interpretation and construction` under the heading *Constitutional law*. (H 1154.5, note 1)
7. Distinguish `--Legal research` from `--Research` per H 1710. (H 1154.5, note 2)
8. `--Legislative history` may also be used free-floating under names of individual legislative enactments (see H 1715, sec. 3.c). (H 1154.5, note 3)
9. Form subdivisions on this list (`--Cases`, `--Digests`, `--Forms`, `--Popular works`, coded $v) are valid LCSH; most may also be used as $x for works about the form. (H 1154.5, Note; H 1075 sec. 1.d)
10. [LC practice] If an exceptional variant form is retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form (H 195); otherwise propose a change (H 193). (H 1154.5, Conflicts)

## Full text

May 2021

PATTERN: *Labor laws and legislation*

### Types of headings covered by the pattern

Headings for legal topics, for example, *Torts*; *Civil procedure*; *Domestic relations*; *Airspace (International law)*; legal phrase headings, for example, *Agricultural laws and legislation*; *Criminal law*; *Trade regulation*; headings for legal systems, for example, *Islamic law*; *International law*; topical headings subdivided by the legal subdivisions --Law and legislation; --Legal status, laws, etc.; and --Safety regulations; place names subdivided by legal subdivisions, for example, --Foreign relations--Law and legislation.

Note: Establish editorially new uses of the subdivision --Law and legislation. For guidelines on the usage of the subdivisions --Law and legislation; --Legal status, laws, etc.; and --Safety regulations under topical headings, see H 1705.

### Conflicts

Any subdivision listed here can be used as a free-floating subdivision under any heading belonging to this category if it is appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. Subject authority records may exist for headings employing variant phrases or subdivisions equivalent to subdivisions on this list.

LC practice: If an exceptional variant form is to be retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form following the procedures in H 195 if the reference does not yet exist. Otherwise, submit a proposal to change the variant form along with all bibliographic records requiring correction following the procedures in H 193.

Note: Most form subdivisions coded $v in this list may also be used as topical subdivisions coded $x when assigned to works about the form (see H 1075, sec. 1.d.).

### List of subdivisions

- `$v Cases`
- `$x Codification`
- `$x Compliance costs` (May Subd Geog)
- `$x Criminal provisions`
- `$v Digests`
- `$x Environmental aspects` (May Subd Geog)
- `$v Forms`
- `$x International unification`
- `$x Interpretation and construction`[^1]
- `$x Language`
- `$x Legal research`[^2]
- `$x Legislative history`[^3]
- `$v Popular works`
- `$x Research`[^2] (May Subd Geog)
- `$x Trial practice`

### Notes

[^1]: Do not use under the heading Constitutional law.
[^2]: For the distinction in usage between the subdivisions --Legal research and --Research, see H 1710.
[^3]: The subdivision --Legislative history may also be used as a free-floating subdivision under names of individual legislative enactments (see H 1715, sec. 3.c.).
