# H 1210 Addresses, Essays, Lectures

## Validation rules

1. The subdivision --Addresses, essays, lectures is discontinued (since early 1986) and is invalid in any newly assigned heading string under any heading type. (H 1210 Background, sec. 1)
2. Do not substitute another form subdivision for it: assign the appropriate headings with no form subdivision. (H 1210 sec. 1)
3. [LC practice] Do not systematically correct existing bibliographic records, but delete --Addresses, essays, lectures whenever it is encountered on a record being revised for other reasons. (H 1210 sec. 2.a)
4. [LC practice] When cataloging a new edition, update the 6XX fields of earlier editions that carry --Addresses, essays, lectures so headings match across all editions. (H 1210 sec. 2.b)

## Full text

### Background

Until early 1986, the subdivision --Addresses, essays, lectures was used as a form subdivision to distinguish works that consist of collected papers, miscellaneous articles, anthologies of readings, etc., from works that consist of continuous text. Because this distinction was determined not to be sufficiently useful, the subdivision was discontinued. References to the subdivision were removed from the subject authority file. For classification of addresses, essays, and lectures, see F 300.

### 1. General rule

Do not use the subdivision --Addresses, essays, lectures under headings assigned to any new work being cataloged. To any work that would formerly have been assigned this subdivision, assign the appropriate headings with no form subdivision.

LC practice:

### 2. Bibliographic file maintenance

#### a. General

Do not make any systematic attempt to correct the subdivision on existing bibliographic records. However, if the subject headings on an individual record are being revised or updated for other reasons, delete --Addresses, essays, lectures whenever it is encountered.

#### b. Editions

If a new edition of a work is being cataloged, earlier editions of which have been assigned headings with the subdivision --Addresses, essays, lectures, update MARC records of the earlier editions so that the 6XX fields in the records for all editions match.
