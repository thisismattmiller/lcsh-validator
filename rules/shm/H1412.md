# H 1412 Classical and Ancient Works

## Validation rules

1. Assign topical headings to topically oriented ancient works, original or translation, e.g. `Political science--Early works to 1800` for Aristotle's Politics. (H 1412 sec. 1)
2. Assign a literary form heading to individual works of belles lettres on an identifiable topic, e.g. `Oedipus (Greek mythology)--Drama`. (H 1412 sec. 2)
3. The pre-1950 LC policy of assigning no topical headings to classical and ancient works is no longer followed. (H 1412 Background)

## Full text

BACKGROUND: This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning topical headings to classical and ancient works. Although pre-1950 Library of Congress cataloging reflects a policy of not assigning topical headings to these works, that policy is no longer followed.

### 1. Topical works

Assign topical headings to any ancient work, original text or translation, that has a topical orientation. Example:

```
Title: Aristotle's Politics.
[a translation of the work by Aristotle]
650 #0 $a Political science $v Early works to 1800.
```

### 2. Works of belles lettres

Assign a literary form heading to any individual work of belles lettres, original text or translation, on an identifiable topic. Example:

```
650 #0 $a Oedipus (Greek mythology) $v Drama.
```
