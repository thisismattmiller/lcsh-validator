# H 1969 Quotations and Maxims

## Validation rules

1. `--Quotations` is free-floating under classes of persons, ethnic groups, names of individual persons (including literary authors), families, and uniform titles of individual sacred works and their parts. (H 1969 background, sec. 1)
2. `--Quotations, maxims, etc.` (exact wording, with commas and "etc.") is free-floating under names of places and topical headings; do not use `--Quotations` under places or topics, nor `--Quotations, maxims, etc.` under persons, families, classes of persons, ethnic groups, or sacred works. (H 1969 background, sec. 1-2)
3. Code these subdivisions `$v` for collections of quotations and `$x` for discussions of quotations. (H 1969 background)
4. A topical or geographic subdivision may be interposed before `--Quotations` under classes of persons/ethnic groups, e.g. `African Americans--Civil rights--Quotations`, `Teachers--Great Britain--Quotations`. (H 1969 sec. 1.a)
5. A topical subdivision may be interposed before `--Quotations` under names of persons, e.g. `Lincoln, Abraham, 1809-1865--Political and social views--Quotations`. (H 1969 sec. 1.b)
6. Under individual religions use `--Sacred books--Quotations`, e.g. `Buddhism--Sacred books--Quotations`. (H 1969 sec. 1.c)
7. `[language or language group]--Quotations, maxims, etc.` is only for quotations *about* the language; general collections of quotations in a language take `Quotations, [name of language]` or `Maxims, [name of language]`. (H 1969 sec. 2 note)

## Full text

BACKGROUND: The subdivision --Quotations is used under classes of persons, ethnic groups, and names of individual persons and families for collections or discussions of quotations by or about the group, person, or family. It is also used under the uniform titles of individual sacred works for collections or discussions of quotations from or about these works. The subdivision --Quotations, maxims, etc., is used under names of places and under topical headings for collections or discussions of quotations, maxims, etc., on these subjects. These subdivisions are coded as $v subfields for collections of quotations or as $x subfields for discussions of quotations. This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning these subdivisions.

### 1. --Quotations

#### a. Classes of persons and ethnic groups

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Quotations under classes of persons and ethnic groups for collections and/or discussions of quotations of, or about, the group. Examples:

```
650 #0 $a African Americans $v Quotations.
650 #0 $a Statesmen $x Quotations.
```

If appropriate for the work, interpose a topical or geographic subdivision between the heading for the class of persons or ethnic group and the subdivision --Quotations. Examples:

```
650 #0 $a African Americans $x Civil rights $x Quotations.
650 #0 $a Teachers $x Great Britain $v Quotations.
```

#### b. Individual persons and families

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Quotations under names of individual persons, including literary authors, and families, for collections and/or discussions of quotations of, or about, the person or family. Examples:

```
600 10 $a Lincoln, Abraham, $d 1809-1865 $x Quotations.
600 30 $a Kennedy family $v Quotations.
```

If appropriate for the work, interpose a topical subdivision between the heading for the person and the subdivision --Quotations. Example:

```
600 10 $a Lincoln, Abraham, 1809-1865 $x Political and social views $v Quotations.
```

#### c. Sacred works

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Quotations under uniform titles of individual sacred works, including their individual parts, for collections or discussions of quotations from or about these works. Example:

```
630 00 $a Bible $v Quotations.
```

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Sacred books--Quotations under individual religions for collections or discussions of quotations from or about these works. Example:

```
650 #0 $a Buddhism $x Sacred books $x Quotations.
```

### 2. --Quotations, maxims, etc.

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Quotations, maxims, etc., under names of places or topical headings for collections and/or discussions of quotations about these places or topics. Examples:

```
651 #0 $a Hawaii $x Quotations, maxims, etc.
651 #0 $a Louisiana $x Social life and customs $v Quotations, maxims, etc.
650 #0 $a Love $x Quotations, maxims, etc.
650 #0 $a Medicine $x Quotations, maxims, etc.
650 #0 $a Spanish language $v Quotations, maxims, etc.
```

Note: Assign headings of the type [name of language or language group]--Quotations, maxims, etc. to collections of quotations, maxims, etc., about the language or languages. Do not assign headings of this type to general collections of quotations, etc., in a particular language. To such collections, assign Quotations, [name of language]; Maxims, [name of language], and similar headings.
