# H 1255 Artistic Photography

## Validation rules

1. Do not subdivide *Photography, Artistic* geographically; `Photography, Artistic--[place]` is invalid. Express the place of origin with a separate heading `Photography--[place]` (e.g. `Photography--Australia`). (H 1255 sec. E)
2. *Photography, Artistic* may be subdivided directly by period subdivisions from the free-floating list in H 1148 (e.g. `Photography, Artistic--20th century`, `Photography, Artistic--21st century`). (H 1255 sec. F)
3. Do not interpose `--History` between *Photography, Artistic* and a period subdivision; `Photography, Artistic--History--20th century` is not the prescribed form. (H 1255 sec. F)
4. For special types of photography use the specific established heading (e.g. *Landscape photography*, *Fashion photography*, *Photography, Humorous*) rather than *Photography, Artistic*. (H 1255 sec. G)
5. For works whose primary focus is artistic photography of a topic, use the heading of the type `Photography of [topic]` (e.g. *Photography of mountains*, *Photography of cats*, *Dance photography*), with an additional heading `[topic]--Pictorial works` (e.g. `Cats--Pictorial works`, `Families--United States--Pictorial works`). (H 1255 sec. H)
6. For portrait photography use *Portrait photography*; if a specific class of persons is represented, add `[class of persons]--Portraits` (e.g. `Children--Portraits`). (H 1255 sec. I)
7. If an established heading of the type `Photography of [class of persons]` exists (e.g. *Photography of women*, *Photography of children*), use it instead of *Portrait photography*; add `[class of persons]--Portraits` only if the visual identity of the individuals is emphasized. (H 1255 sec. I)
8. *Portrait photography* may be subdivided geographically (e.g. `Portrait photography--United States`). (H 1255 sec. J, example)
9. For collections by one photographer, assign *Photography, Artistic* (or the type/theme heading) plus a heading for the photographer's name. (H 1255 sec. J)
10. For exhibition catalogs of artistic photographs not limited to a theme or type, the prescribed heading is `Photography, Artistic--Exhibitions`; for a theme or type, `[theme or type heading]--Exhibitions` (e.g. `Photography of clouds--Exhibitions`, `Glamour photography--Exhibitions`). (H 1255 sec. K)
11. For an exhibition of one photographer's works, add `[name of photographer]--Exhibitions`. (H 1255 sec. K.1)
12. For photographs from an institution's permanent collection, add `[name of institution]--Exhibitions` per H 1593. (H 1255 sec. K.2)
13. For photographs from a private collection, follow H 1427: e.g. `Photography, Artistic--Private collections--Exhibitions`, `[name of collector]--Photograph collections--Exhibitions`, and `Photograph collections--[place]--Exhibitions`. (H 1255 sec. K.2, examples)
14. [LC practice] LC catalogers no longer use form subdivisions (--Pictorial works, --Portraits, --Exhibitions); they assign LCGFT terms instead (e.g. *Illustrated works*, *Portraits*, *Exhibition catalogs*). These subdivided forms remain valid LCSH. (H 1255 sec. A, D–K)
15. [LC practice] For artistic photographs of a class of persons, LC assigns the unsubdivided class-of-persons heading (e.g. `Children`, `Celebrities--United States`) plus *Portrait photography* or *Photography of [class of persons]*, with LCGFT *Portraits*. (H 1255 sec. I)

## Full text

### A. Background

LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. *Textbooks*. To replace a form subdivision that includes a demographic group, also assign Library of Congress Demographic Groups Terms (LCDGT) to bring out the audience of the resource being cataloged, e.g. *German speakers* or *Arabic, Students of*.

For guidelines on works about art, see H 1250. For guidelines on works about illustrations, see H 1659. For guidelines on works about pictorial works, see H 1935. For guidelines on works on exhibitions, see H 1593.

### B. Recent revisions

2026-07: Updated template. Added alternative instructions for LCGFT implementation.

### C. Definitions

Artistic photography: photography used for creative expression and treated as a fine art.

### D. General works on artistic photography

Assign the heading *Photography, Artistic* to works that consist chiefly of photographs whose main purpose is photography as a fine art. Assign the heading also to general discussions of artistic photography.

LC practice: Assign the subject heading *Photography, Artistic* for works that are chiefly collections of or reproductions of photographs whose main purpose is photography as a fine art. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Illustrated works*.

Example:

```
Title: Great unknowns : an exquisite collection of black and white photography.
650 #0 $a Photography, Artistic.
LC practice:
650 #0 $a Photography, Artistic.
655 #7 $a Illustrated works. $2 lcgft
```

### E. Artistic photography from a particular place

Do not divide the heading *Photography, Artistic* by place. To express the geographic origin of the photographs, assign an additional subject heading *Photography--[place]*.

Example:

```
Title: Visual instincts : contemporary Australian photography.
650 #0 $a Photography, Artistic.
650 #0 $a Photography $z Australia.
LC practice:
650 #0 $a Photography, Artistic.
650 #0 $a Photography $z Australia.
655 #7 $a Illustrated works. $2 lcgft
```

### F. Artistic photography from a particular time period

Assign the heading *Photography, Artistic*, subdivided by period subdivisions from the free-floating list in H 1148. Do not interpose the subdivision *--History*.

Example:

```
Title: Present perfect.
650 #0 $a Photography, Artistic $y 20th century.
650 #0 $a Photography, Artistic $y 21st century.
LC practice:
650 #0 $a Photography, Artistic $y 20th century.
650 #0 $a Photography, Artistic $y 21st century.
655 #7 $a Illustrated works. $2 lcgft
```

### G. Special types of photography

For works on or photographic collections of special types of photography, assign the specific heading that best represents that type of photography, for example, *Landscape photography*; *Fashion photography*; *Photography, Humorous*. For portrait photography, see Sec. I, below.

LC practice: Assign the subject heading for the type of artistic photography for works that are chiefly collections of or reproductions of photographs with a specific focus. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Illustrated works*.

Example:

```
Title: The field guide of photographing landscapes.
650 #0 $a Landscape photography.
LC practice:
650 #0 $a Landscape photography.
655 #7 $a Illustrated works. $2 lcgft

Title: Vogue book of fashion photography, 1919-1979.
650 #0 $a Fashion photography.
LC practice:
650 #0 $a Fashion photography.
655 #7 $a Illustrated works. $2 lcgft
```

### H. Photography of specific items, places, etc.

Determine whether the focus of the piece is on artistic photography or on the actual items, places, etc. If the primary focus is artistic photography, assign a heading for photography of the topic, for example, *Photography of mountains; Dance photography*. Assign additional headings for the topic with the form subdivision *--Pictorial works*. For works that merely contain photographs depicting a topic or place and are of interest primarily for the pictorial information presented, see H 1935.

LC practice: For works that are chiefly collections of or reproductions of photographs with a specific focus, first determine if the focus of the work is on artistic photography. If so, assign the subject heading for the topical focus, for example, *Photography of mountains; Dance photography*. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Illustrated works*.

LC practice: For guidelines on works that merely contain photographs depicting a topic or place and are of interest primarily for the pictorial information presented, see H 1935.

Example:

```
Title: City cats.
650 #0 $a Photography of cats.
650 #0 $a Cats $v Pictorial works.
LC practice:
650 #0 $a Photography of cats.
650 #0 $a Cats.
655 #7 $a Illustrated works. $2 lcgft

Title: The American family : a pictorial celebration.
650 #0 $a Photography of families.
650 #0 $a Families $z United States $v Pictorial works.
LC practice:
650 #0 $a Photography of families.
650 #0 $a Families $z United States.
655 #7 $a Illustrated works. $2 lcgft
```

### I. Portrait photography

For works on or general collections of portrait photographs, assign the heading *Portrait photography*. If a specific class of persons is represented, assign a heading for that class of persons subdivided by *--Portraits*, with *Portrait photography* as an additional heading. If there is an existing heading of the type *Photography of [class of persons]* assign that heading instead of *Portrait photography* and, if the visual identity of the individuals is emphasized, assign an additional heading for class of persons with subdivision *--Portraits*. For works that contain portraits primarily as visual representations of persons without emphasis on photography, see H 1935.

LC practice: For works that are chiefly collections of or reproductions of photographs that are portraits, assign of subject heading for the focus of the portraits. If a specific class of persons is represented, assign a heading for that class of persons, with *Portrait photography* as an additional heading. If there is an existing heading of the type *Photography of [class of persons]* assign that heading instead of *Portrait photography*. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Illustrated works*.

LC practice: For guidelines on works that contain portraits primarily as visual representations of persons without emphasis on photography, see H 1935.

### K. Exhibition catalogs

For an exhibition catalog of artistic photographs, not limited to a particular theme or type of photography, assign the subject heading *Photography, Artistic--Exhibitions*. For an exhibition catalog of photographs on a particular theme or photography of a particular type, assign a heading for the theme or type, for example, *Photography of clouds--Exhibitions*; *Glamour photography--Exhibitions*.

LC practice: For an exhibition catalog of artistic photographs, not limited to a particular theme or type of photography, assign the subject heading *Photography, Artistic*. For an exhibition catalog of photographs on a particular theme or photography of a particular type, assign a heading for the theme or type, for example, *Photography of clouds*. Also assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Exhibition catalogs*.

### K.1. Works of one photographer

If the exhibition consists of works by one photographer, assign an additional heading for the name of the photographer with subdivision *--Exhibitions*.

LC practice: If the exhibition consists of works by one photographer, assign an additional heading for the name of the photographer.

### K.2. Works belongs to an institution or private collection

If the photographs are a part of an institution's permanent collection, assign an additional heading for the institution with subdivision *--Exhibitions* in accordance with H 1593. If the photographs are identified as coming from an individual private collection, including a formerly private collection now belonging to a public institution, assign additional headings as specified in H 1427.

LC practice: If the photographs are a part of an institution's permanent collection, assign an additional heading for the institution, in accordance with H 1593. If the photographs are identified as coming from an individual private collection, including a formerly private collection now belonging to a public institution, assign additional headings as specified in H 1427.
