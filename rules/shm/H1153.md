# H 1153 Pattern Headings: Industries

## Validation rules

1. Subdivisions on this list are free-floating under headings for types of industries, including service industries and industries based on commodities, manufactured goods, or natural resources (e.g. *Animal industry*; *Cobalt industry*; *Electronic industries*; *Food industry and trade*; *Lumber trade*; *Wedding supplies and services industry*). (H 1153, Types of headings covered)
2. Headings that represent industries without the word industry (e.g. *Aeronautics, Commercial*; *Tourism*) also take this list. (H 1153, Types of headings covered)
3. Do not use this list under names of individual businesses or corporations (use H 1105) or under the general heading *Industries*. (H 1153, Types of headings covered)
4. A listed subdivision may be used free-floating if appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. (H 1153, Conflicts)
5. Only subdivisions marked (May Subd Geog) may be followed by a geographic subdivision. (H 1153, list)
6. `--Equipment and supplies` is not valid under *Construction industry*. (H 1153, note 3)
7. `--Estimates` is not established under *Construction industry* or *Retail trade*; use under other industries for cost estimates (e.g. *Aerospace industries--Estimates*). (H 1153, note 4)
8. `--Inventories` is not valid under *Retail trade*. (H 1153, note 5)
9. `--Military aspects` is not valid under military-related industries (e.g. *Defense industries*; *Military fiber optics industry*). (H 1153, note 6)
10. `--Prices`, `--Prices--Government policy`, and `--Prices--Law and legislation` are not valid under *Retail trade*; use `--Prices` under headings for products/merchandise, and under an industry only when no general heading for the industry's products exists. (H 1153, note 7)
11. `--Specifications` ($v) is used under an industry only when specifications are not limited to a single product; for single products use it under the product heading. (H 1153, note 8)
12. Legal subdivisions (e.g. `--Law and legislation`, `--Safety regulations`, `--Employees--Legal status, laws etc.` [sic; printed without the comma before etc.; the standard LCSH form is `Legal status, laws, etc.`], `--Finance--Law and legislation`) may be further subdivided per H 1154.5. (H 1153, note 1)
13. `--Employees` may be further subdivided by subdivisions for classes of persons (H 1100). (H 1153, note 2)
14. Form subdivisions on this list ($v `--Inventories`, `--Records and correspondence`, `--Specifications`, `--Telephone directories`, `--Trademarks`, and `--Employees--Job descriptions`) are valid LCSH; most may also be used as $x for works about the form. (H 1153, Note; H 1075 sec. 1.d)
15. [LC practice] Establish new subdivisions under *Construction industry* if possible, otherwise under *Retail trade*. (H 1153, Types of headings covered)
16. [LC practice] If an exceptional variant form is retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form (H 195); otherwise propose a change (H 193). (H 1153, Conflicts)

## Full text

June 2013

PATTERNS: *Construction industry*; *Retail trade*

### Types of headings covered by the pattern

Headings for types of industries, including service industries as well as those based on commodities, manufactured goods, or natural resources. Examples: *Animal industry*; *Cobalt industry*; *Electronic industries*; *Food industry and trade*; *Gardening equipment industry*; *High technology industries*; *Laundry industry*; *Lumber trade*; *Macadamia nut industry*; *Wedding supplies and services industry*. Also included are headings which represent industries but do not explicitly include the term industry, for example, *Aeronautics, Commercial* and *Tourism*. The category does not include names of individual businesses or corporations, nor the general heading *Industries*. For subdivisions used under the names of individual businesses or corporations, see H 1105. Establish new subdivisions under *Construction industry*, if possible. If that is not possible, establish them under *Retail trade*.

### Conflicts

Any subdivision listed here can be used as a free-floating subdivision under any heading belonging to the category if it is appropriate and no conflict exists in the subject authority file. Subject authority records may exist for headings employing variant phrases or subdivisions equivalent to subdivisions on this list.

LC practice: If an exceptional variant form is to be retained, make a UF reference from the equivalent free-floating subdivision form following the procedures in H 195 if the reference does not yet exist. Otherwise, submit a proposal to change the variant form along with all bibliographic records requiring correction following the procedures in H 193.

Note: Most form subdivisions coded $v in this list may also be used as topical subdivisions coded $x when assigned to works about the form (see H 1075, sec. 1.d.).
