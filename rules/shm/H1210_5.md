# H 1210.5 Aerial and Space Photography

## Validation rules

1. Use the heading `Aerial photography` for technical works on methods of taking photographs from conventional aircraft. (H 1210.5 sec. D.1)
2. Use headings of the form `Aerial photography in [topic]` (e.g. `Aerial photography in agriculture`) for methods of taking and using aerial photographs in a particular field. (H 1210.5 sec. D.2)
3. Use the heading `Aerial photographs` for general works on the handling, maintenance, cataloging, etc., of collections of aerial photographs. (H 1210.5 sec. E.1)
4. --Aerial photographs is free-floating under topical headings and under names of countries, cities, etc., for collections or discussions of aerial photographs (including reproductions) of the topic or place. As a form ($v) it marks collections; as topical ($x) it marks discussions. (H 1210.5 sec. E.2, E.3)
5. --Aerial views is a free-floating form subdivision ($v) used under names of countries, cities, etc., or under individual educational institutions, for maps or atlases depicting those places observed from the air. (H 1210.5 sec. F)
6. Do not further subdivide --Aerial views by --Maps (i.e. `--Aerial views--Maps` is invalid). (H 1210.5 sec. F)
7. --Photographs from space is a free-floating form subdivision ($v) under topical headings and names of countries, cities, etc., for collections of photographs (or reproductions) taken from outer space. (H 1210.5 sec. G)
8. Use the heading `Space photography` (not --Photographs from space) for technical works on methods of taking photographs from outer space. (H 1210.5 sec. G)
9. [LC practice] LC catalogers no longer use form subdivisions; assign LCGFT terms instead (e.g. `Aerial photographs`, `Photomaps`, `Photographs`), and LCDGT where a form subdivision would have expressed a demographic group. (H 1210.5 sec. A)
10. [LC practice] For collections of aerial photographs of a topic or place, assign the topic or place heading without `$v Aerial photographs` plus LCGFT `Aerial photographs`; use `$x Aerial photographs` only for discussions. (H 1210.5 sec. E.2, E.3)
11. [LC practice] For aerial views of a place, assign the place heading plus LCGFT (e.g. `Photomaps`) instead of --Aerial views. (H 1210.5 sec. F)
12. [LC practice] For photographs from space of a place, assign the place heading plus LCGFT (e.g. `Photographs`) instead of --Photographs from space. (H 1210.5 sec. G)

## Full text

### A. Background

The heading *Aerial photography* is assigned to technical works on methods of taking photographs from conventional aircraft. Headings of the type *Aerial photography in [topic]* are assigned to works that discuss methods of taking aerial photographs and using them to carry out work or research in a particular field. The heading *Aerial photographs* is assigned to general works on the handling, maintenance, cataloging, etc., of collections of aerial photographs. The subdivision *--Aerial photographs* is used under topical headings and under names of countries, cities, etc., for discussions or collections of aerial photographs, or reproductions of them, of a subject or place. The form subdivision *--Aerial views* is used for maps depicting areas observed from the air. The form subdivision *--Photographs from space* is used under topical headings and under names of countries, cities, etc., for collections of photographs, or reproductions of them, taken from outer space. This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning these headings and subdivisions.

LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. *Aerial photographs*. To replace a form subdivision that includes a demographic group, also assign Library of Congress Demographic Groups Terms (LCDGT) to bring out the audience of the resource being cataloged, e.g. *German speakers* or *Arabic, Students of*.

### C. Definitions

Aerial photographs: Photographs taken from the air.

### D. Aerial photography

### D.1. General rule

Assign the heading *Aerial photography* to technical works on methods of taking photographs from conventional aircraft.

### D.2. By subject

Assign headings of the type *Aerial photography in [topic]* to works that discuss methods of taking aerial photographs and using them as a tool in carrying out work or research in a particular field.

Example:

```
Title: Color aerial photography in the plant sciences and related fields.
650 #0 $a Aerial photography in agriculture.
650 #0 $a Aerial photography in forestry.
650 #0 $a Aerial photography in botany.
650 #0 $a Color photography.
```

### E. Aerial photographs

### E.1. General

Assign the heading *Aerial photographs* to general works on the handling, maintenance, cataloging, etc., of collections of aerial photographs.

### E.2. By subject

Assign the free-floating subdivision *--Aerial photographs* under topical headings to collections or discussions of aerial photographs on these topics.

LC practice: Assign the subject heading for the topic for collections of aerial photographs or reproductions of the photographs on the topic. Add the free-floating topical subdivision for discussions of aerial photographs of the topic. Do not use the form subdivision. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Aerial photographs*.

### E.3. By place

Assign the free-floating subdivision *--Aerial photographs* under names of countries, cities, etc., to discussions or collections of aerial photographs, including reproductions, of these places.

LC practice: Assign the subject heading for the place for collections of aerial photographs or reproductions of the photographs of the place. Add the free-floating topical subdivision for discussions of aerial photographs of the place. Do not use the form subdivision. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Aerial photographs*.

Example:

```
Title: Above Baton Rouge : a pilot's view then and now.
651 #0 $a Baton Rouge (La.) $v Aerial photographs.
651 #0 $a Baton Rouge (La.) $x History $v Sources.
650 #0 $a Repeat photography.

LC practice:
651 #0 $a Baton Rouge (La.) $x History.
650 #0 $a Repeat photography.
655 #7 $a Aerial photographs $2 lcgft
```

### F. Aerial photographs

(Section heading as printed in the source; the section's content concerns the subdivision --Aerial views.)

Assign the free-floating form subdivision *--Aerial views* under names of countries, cities, etc., or under individual educational institutions, to maps or atlases depicting those places observed from the air. Do not further subdivide by *--Maps*.

LC practice: Assign the subject heading for the place for collections of aerial photography of the named place. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Photomaps*.

### G. Photographs from space

Assign the free-floating form subdivision *--Photographs from space* under topical headings and under names of countries, cities, etc., for collections of photographs, or reproductions of them, taken from outer space. Use the heading *Space photography* for technical works on the methods of taking photographs from outer space.

LC practice: Assign the subject heading for the place for collections of photographs from space or reproductions of the photographs of the named place. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Photographs*.
