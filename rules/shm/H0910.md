# H 910 Place as a Theme in Art, Literature, Motion Pictures, etc.

## Validation rules

1. `--In art` is a free-floating subdivision under names of places; it may be coded `$v` (form) for reproductions of art works with the place as theme, e.g. `651 #0 $a New York (N.Y.) $v In art.` (H 910 sec. C)
2. `--In art` is coded `$x` (topical) for works that discuss a place as a theme in art, e.g. `651 #0 $a Paris (France) $x In art.` (H 910 sec. C)
3. [LC practice] For collections of art depicting places, LC assigns `[Place] $x In art` (not `$v`) plus LCGFT terms (J 210). (H 910 sec. C)
4. Photographs of a place, or pre-photography paintings made merely to give views, take `[place]--Pictorial works`, not `--In art`. (H 910 sec. C)
5. The phrase heading `[name of place] in art` is invalid (discontinued January 1997; earlier 1997 change to `[name of place]--In art`); `[name of place]--Description and travel--Views` and `[name of place]--Description--Views` for art reproductions were discontinued in 1973. (H 910 sec. A, C)
6. `--In literature` is a free-floating topical subdivision (`$x`) under place names for the treatment of a place in literature collectively or in any genre: `Paris (France)--In literature`. The phrase heading `[name of place] in literature` is invalid (discontinued August 1993). (H 910 sec. D)
7. Collections or individual literary works featuring a place as theme take the literary form subdivisions `--Literary collections`, `--Fiction`, `--Drama`, or `--Poetry` under the place name. (H 910 sec. D Note)
8. [LC practice] LC assigns LCGFT terms instead of the form subdivisions `--Literary collections`, `--Fiction`, `--Drama`, `--Poetry` (and in general no longer uses form subdivisions, per H 1075). (H 910 sec. A, D)
9. `--In motion pictures` is a free-floating topical subdivision (`$x`) under place names: `Canada--In motion pictures`. The phrase heading `[name of place] in motion pictures` is invalid (discontinued December 1997). (H 910 sec. E)
10. Headings of the type `[place]--In the Bible`, `[place]--In the Vedas`, etc. are not free-floating; they must be established. Phrase headings `[name of place] in the Bible` are discontinued (June 2013). These are assigned only as topical headings. (H 910 sec. A, F)
11. Headings of the type `[place] in textbooks`, `[place] in the performing arts`, etc. are established (not free-floating) topical phrase headings, e.g. `Rome in textbooks`. (H 910 sec. G)
12. Places as topics of musical works: see H 2075. (H 910 sec. G Note)

## Full text

### A. Background

In 1973, the practice of assigning headings of the type *[name of place]--Description and travel--Views* or *[name of place]--Description--Views* to works consisting of reproductions of art works having a particular place as a theme was discontinued in favor of the use of headings of the type *[name of place] in art* as either form or topical headings. In 1997 headings of this type were changed to *[name of place]--In art*. Phrase headings of the type *[name of place] in the Bible, etc.*, continued to be used until 2013, when they were largely discontinued in favor of headings of the type *[name of place]--In the Bible*. They are assigned only as topical headings.

LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. *Paintings*. To replace a form subdivision that includes a demographic group, also assign Library of Congress Demographic Groups Terms (LCDGT) to bring out the audience of the resource being cataloged, e.g. *German speakers* or *Arabic, Students of*.

For guidelines on place as a theme in art, see H 910. For guidelines on artistic photography, see H 1255. For guidelines on illustrations, see H 1659. For guidelines on pictorial works, see H 1935. For guidelines on exhibitions, see H 1593.

### C. [Place]--In art

Use the free-floating subdivision *--In art* as a form subdivision coded $v for reproductions of art works with a specific place as a theme.

Exception: Assign to works that consist of photographs of a place, or of paintings made before the invention of photography merely to give views, the heading *[place]--Pictorial works*.

LC practice: For collections of art depicting places, assign *[Place] $x In art* as a theme. Also assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource. For guidelines on assigning LCGFT to artistic and visual works, see J 210.

Example:

```
Title: Manhattan observed : selections of drawings, prints, and paintings.
651 #0 $a New York (N.Y.) $v In art.
651 #0 $a Manhattan (New York, N.Y.) $v In art.
LC practice:
651 #0 $a New York (N.Y.) $x In art.
651 #0 $a Manhattan (New York, N.Y.) $x In art.
```

Also use the free-floating subdivision *--In art* as a topical subdivision coded $x for works that discuss a specific place as a theme in art.

Example:

```
Title: Michel Delacroix, eternal Paris.
600 10 $a Delacroix, Michel $x Themes, motives.
651 #0 $a Paris (France) $x In art.
```

Use of the free-floating phrase heading *[name of place] in art* was discontinued in January 1997.

### D. [Place]--In literature

Use the free-floating topical subdivision *--In literature* under names of places for works that discuss the treatment of a specific place in the various literary genres collectively, or in any individual literary genre.

Example:

```
Title: The Paris of the novelists
651 #0 $a Paris (France) $x In literature.
650 #0 $a Fiction $y 19th century $x History and criticism.
```

Note: The standard provisions for literary form headings apply to collections of literature or individual literary works featuring a specific place as a theme, that is, the subdivisions *--Literary collections*, *--Fiction*, *--Drama*, or *--Poetry* are used under the place name.

LC practice: In place of the form subdivisions mentioned in the note, instead, assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource.

Use of the free-floating phrase heading *[name of place] in literature* was discontinued in August 1993.

### E. [Place]--In motion pictures

Use the free-floating topical subdivision *--In motion pictures* under names of places for works that discuss the treatment of a specific place in motion pictures.

Example:

```
Title: Hollywood's Canada : the Americanization of our national image.
651 #0 $a Canada $x In motion pictures.
650 #0 $a Motion pictures $z United States $x History.
```

Use of phrase headings of the type *[name of place] in motion pictures* was discontinued in December 1997.

### F. Places in sacred works

Submit proposals to editorially establish headings of the type *[place]--In the Bible*, *[place]--In the Vedas*, etc. as needed for works that discuss the treatment of a specific place in a sacred work.

Use of phrase headings of the type *[name of place] in the Bible* was discontinued in June 2013.

### G. [Place] in […] headings

Submit proposals to establish editorially headings of the type *[place] in textbooks*, *[place] in the performing arts*, etc. Assign these as topical headings to works that discuss the treatment of the place in textbooks, the performing arts, etc.

Example:

```
Title: Die Römerzeit im Selbstverständnis der Franzosen und Deutschen.
650 #0 $a Rome in textbooks.
```

Note: For instructions on assigning names of places as topics of musical works, see H 2075.
