# H 1460 Congresses

## Validation rules

1. `--Congresses` is a free-floating form subdivision (`$v`) under subjects of all types for collected conference papers, condensations or abstracts of papers, reports of proceedings, preprints, programs, delegate lists, or combinations of these. (H 1460 sec. A, C)
2. Proceedings of a society's meeting on a subject: `[subject]--Congresses`; annual meetings or internal-affairs proceedings: `[name of corporate body]--Congresses`. (H 1460 sec. D)
3. Use `--Congresses` for collections of two or more lectures/papers identified with a meeting; omit it when lectures are later published separately and no longer identified with the meeting. (H 1460 sec. E)
4. Do not use `--Congresses` for hearings of legislative bodies. (H 1460 sec. F)
5. `--Congresses` may be followed only by the form subdivisions `--Calendars`, `--Directories`, or `--Juvenile literature`, e.g. `Authorship--Congresses--Directories`, `Education--Congresses--Calendars`. (H 1460 sec. G)
6. `--Congresses--Abstracts` and `--Congresses--Periodicals` are invalid. (H 1460 sec. G)
7. `--Congresses` is also a topical subdivision (`$x`) under subjects for works about congresses on those subjects. (H 1460 sec. E [second], "--Congresses as a topical subdivision")
8. `--Congresses--Attendance` is a free-floating topical subdivision (both `$x`) for works on attendance at or participation in congresses, e.g. `Physics--Congresses--Attendance`. (H 1460 sec. F [second])
9. [LC practice] LC no longer uses form subdivisions; it assigns the topical or corporate heading without `$v Congresses` and adds LCGFT `Conference papers and proceedings` (published records), `Conference materials` (ephemera), `Lectures`, `Essays`, `Yearbooks`, `Legislative hearings`, `Directories`, `Calendars` as appropriate; the topical `$x Congresses` remains. (H 1460 sec. A, C-G)

## Full text

### A. Background

The free-floating subdivision *--Congresses* is used under subjects for collections of conference proceedings on those subjects as well as for works about congresses on those subjects. This instruction sheet provides guidelines for the use of *--Congresses* as both a form and topical subdivision. For classification of congresses, see F 240.

LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. *Textbooks*. To replace a form subdivision that includes a demographic group, also assign Library of Congress Demographic Groups Terms (LCDGT) to bring out the audience of the resource being cataloged, e.g. *German speakers* or *Arabic, Students of*.

### B. Recent revisions

2026-01: Updated template. Added instructions related to LCGFT implementation.

### C. Congresses as a form subdivision

Use *--Congresses* as a form subdivision under subjects of all types to represent either: (1) the collected papers delivered at, or published on the occasion of, one or several congresses, symposia, conferences, meetings, etc.; (2) condensations or abstracts of such papers; (3) reports of the proceedings or discussions, preprints, program statements, lists of delegates, etc., of such congresses; or (4) combinations of these types of materials.

LC practice: Record subject heading(s) for the topic of the conference(s). Use the LCGFT *Conference papers and proceedings* for published records of conferences, including abstracts, reports, and the papers presented. Use LCGFT *Conference materials* for ephemeral materials such as flyers, program books, daily newsletters, or newspapers that are distributed at conferences.

### D. Meetings of a society or institution or institution

For the proceedings of a meeting or meetings of a society or institution on a specific subject, use the subdivision *--Congresses* under the subject. For the annual meeting of a corporate body or other proceedings dealing with the internal affairs of a society or institution, use the subdivision *--Congresses* under the name of the corporate body, society, etc.

LC practice: Record subject heading(s) for the topic of the meeting or the corporate body that is hosting the conference for its internal affairs. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. *Conference papers and proceedings* and *Yearbooks*.

### E. Lectures published separately

Use the form subdivision *--Congresses* for collections of two or more lectures or papers, or of contributions, essays, etc., that are based on papers, originally presented at a conference or meeting and identified with that meeting. Omit the subdivision *--Congresses* when one or several lectures, originally delivered on the occasion of an individual meeting, are later published under separate cover, and no longer identified with the original meeting.

LC practice: Record subject heading(s) for the topic of the lectures, papers, etc., that are based on papers, originally presented at a conference or meeting and identified with that meeting. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. *Conference papers and proceedings* and *Lectures* and/or *Essays*.

### F. Legislative hearings

Do not use the subdivision *--Congresses* for the hearings of legislative bodies.

LC practice: Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. *Legislative hearings*.

### G. Use of form subdivisions after --Congresses

Further subdivide *--Congresses* by the form subdivisions *--Calendars*, *--Directories*, or *--Juvenile literature*, if appropriate. Do not use the form subdivisions *--Abstracts* or *--Periodicals* after the subdivision *--Congresses*.

LC practice: Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. *Conference papers and proceedings* and *Directories*.

### E. --Congresses as a topical subdivision

Use *--Congresses* as a topical subdivision under subjects for works about congresses, conferences, meetings, etc., on those subjects.

Example:

```
Title: The guide to writers conferences.
650 #0 $a Authorship $x Congresses $v Directories.
LC practice:
650 #0 $a Authorship $x Congresses.
655 #7 $a Directories. $2 lcgft
```

Example:

```
Title: ERIC calendar of education-related conferences.
650 #0 $a Education $x Congresses $v Calendars.
LC practice:
650 #0 $a Education $x Congresses.
655 #7 $a Calendars. $2 lcgft
```

### F. The subdivision --Congresses--Attendance

Use the free-floating topical subdivision *--Congresses--Attendance* under subjects for works on attendance at or participation in congresses on those subjects.

Example:

```
Title: Conference attendance patterns among physicists.
650 #0 $a Physics $x Congresses $x Attendance.
650 #0 $a Physicists $z Great Britain.
```
