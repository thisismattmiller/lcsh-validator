# H 1120 Free-Floating Subdivisions: Names of Families

## Validation rules

1. The subdivisions on the sec. C (topical) and sec. D (form) lists may be used on a free-floating basis under headings for individual families, dynasties, royal houses, etc., e.g. `Smith family`, `Ptolemaic dynasty, 305-30 B.C.`, `Bourbon, House of`, `Normandy, Dukes of`. (H 1120 sec. A)
2. Most form subdivisions coded `$v` on this list may also be used as topical subdivisions coded `$x` for works about the form (H 1075). (H 1120 sec. A)
3. Only `--Homes and haunts`, `--Monuments`, `--Museums`, `--Political activity`, `--Relics`, and `--Tombs` are marked May Subd Geog; other subdivisions on the list are not followed by a geographic subdivision. (H 1120 sec. C)
4. `--Relics` is for disinterred bones, etc.; for graves, interred bones, etc., use `--Tombs` under names of individual families. (H 1120 sec. C)
5. `--Health` covers the state of health of family members, including diseases suffered. (H 1120 sec. C)
6. Collection subdivisions (`--Art collections`, `--Coin collections`, `--Map collections`, `--Photograph collections`, etc.) are used for the family's own collections (consult H 1427). (H 1120 sec. C)
7. [LC practice] LC catalogers no longer use form subdivisions; assign LCGFT (e.g. `Textbooks`) and, for demographic groups, LCDGT. Form subdivisions remain valid LCSH. (H 1120 secs. A, D)

## Full text

### A. Background

The subdivisions listed below may be used on a free-floating basis, as appropriate, under subject headings for individual families, dynasties, royal houses, etc., for example, *Smith family*; *Ptolemaic dynasty, 305-30 B.C.*; *Bourbon, House of*; *Normandy, Dukes of*.

Note: Most form subdivisions coded $v in this list may also be used as topical subdivisions coded $x when assigned to works about the form (see H 1075, sec. 1.d.).

LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. *Textbooks*. To replace a form subdivision that includes a demographic group, also assign Library of Congress Demographic Groups Terms (LCDGT) to bring out the audience of the resource being cataloged, e.g. *German speakers* or *Arabic, Students of*.

### B. Recent revisions

2026-01: Updated template. Separated general and form subdivisions. Added instructions related to LCGFT implementation.
