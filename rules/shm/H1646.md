# H 1646 Handbooks, Manuals, etc.

## Validation rules

1. `--Handbooks, manuals, etc.` is a free-floating form subdivision ($v) usable under subject headings of all types (topical, geographic, corporate, uniform title, personal name), e.g. `Technology--Handbooks, manuals, etc.`, `Middle East--Handbooks, manuals, etc.`, `Library of Congress--Handbooks, manuals, etc.`, `WordPerfect (Computer file)--Handbooks, manuals, etc.`, `Dickinson, Emily, 1830-1886--Handbooks, manuals, etc.` (H 1646 sec. C)
2. The exact wording is `Handbooks, manuals, etc.` (comma after Handbooks, comma before etc., final period part of "etc."). (H 1646 sec. C)
3. Under classes of persons, use `--Handbooks, manuals, etc.` only if a heading for the corresponding field or activity does not exist and cannot be established, e.g. `Nurses' aides--Handbooks, manuals, etc.` (H 1646 sec. D)
4. Prefer more specific free-floating form subdivisions where appropriate: `--Amateurs' manuals` (H 1943.5 sec. 4), `--Guidebooks` (H 1645), `--Laboratory manuals`, `--Observers' manuals`, `--Tables` (H 2160). (H 1646 sec. E)
5. [LC practice] LC no longer uses `--Handbooks, manuals, etc.`; it assigns the topical heading alone plus LCGFT `Handbooks and manuals`. The subdivision remains valid LCSH. (H 1646 sec. A, C)

## Full text

### A. Background

LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. *Textbooks*. To replace a form subdivision that includes a demographic group, also assign Library of Congress Demographic Groups Terms (LCDGT) to bring out the audience of the resource being cataloged, e.g. *German speakers* or *Arabic, Students of*.

### B. Recent revisions

2026-01: Updated template. Added instructions related to LCGFT implementation.

### C. General rule

Use *--Handbooks, manuals, etc.* as a free-floating form subdivision under subject headings of all types for concise reference works in which facts and information pertaining to the topic are arranged for ready reference and consultation rather than for continuous reading and study.

LC practice: Assign a subject heading for the topic of the work. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g., *Handbooks and manuals*.

Example:


### D. Classes of persons

Use the subdivision under classes of persons only if a heading for the corresponding field or activity does not exist and cannot be established, for example, *Nurses' aides--Handbooks, manuals, etc.*

LC practice: Assign a subject heading for the topic of the work. Use a class of persons heading if the field or activity does not exist and cannot be established. Assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g., *Handbooks and manuals*.
