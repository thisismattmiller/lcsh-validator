# H 2083 Specifications

## Validation rules

1. `--Contracts and specifications` was discontinued in 1989; use `--Specifications` or phrase headings for types of contracts (e.g. `Construction contracts`, `Road construction contracts`). (H 2083 background)
2. `Specifications` (May Subd Geog) is the heading for general works on specifications. (H 2083 sec. 1)
3. `--Specifications` (May Subd Geog) is free-floating under headings for types of engineering or construction, kinds of products or merchandise, and types of industries. (H 2083 sec. 2.a, 2.b)
4. Code `--Specifications` `$v` for works containing specifications and `$x` for works discussing specifications. (H 2083 sec. 2.a, 2.b)
5. `--Specifications` may be followed by a geographic subdivision, e.g. `Bus terminals--Specifications--Great Britain`, `Engineering--Specifications--United States`, `Optical materials--Specifications--France`. (H 2083 sec. 2)
6. Place `--Specifications` under the heading for the object designed or constructed, not under the construction/engineering process: `Bridges, Arched--Specifications`, not `Bridges, Arched--Design and construction--Specifications`. (H 2083 sec. 2.a)
7. Use `--Specifications` under an industry only when the discussion is not limited to a single product of the industry. (H 2083 sec. 2.b)

## Full text

BACKGROUND: Specifications are detailed descriptions or assessments of requirements, dimensions, materials, etc., for a proposed building, structure, product, etc. This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning the heading Specifications and the subdivision --Specifications. The free-floating subdivision --Contracts and specifications, previously used under headings for engineering and construction, was discontinued in 1989. It was replaced by the free-floating subdivision --Specifications and by phrase headings for types of contracts, for example, Construction contracts and Road construction contracts.

### 1. General

Assign the heading Specifications (May Subd Geog) to general works on specifications.

### 2. Special topics

#### a. Engineering and construction

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Specifications (May Subd Geog) as a form or topical subdivision under headings for types of engineering or construction to works that contain or discuss detailed descriptions or assessments of requirements for construction or engineering projects. Code the subdivision as a $v subfield for works that contain specifications and as an $x subfield for works that discuss specifications. Examples:

```
650 #0 $a Bridges $v Specifications.
650 #0 $a Bus terminals $x Specifications $z Great Britain.
650 #0 $a Engineering $x Specifications $z United States.
```

Assign the subdivision --Specifications under the heading for the object designed or constructed rather than under the construction or engineering process, if both headings exist. Examples:

```
650 #0 $a Bridges, Arched $v Specifications.
  [not 650 #0 $a Bridges, Arched $x Design and construction $x Specifications.]
```

#### b. Other topics

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Specifications (May Subd Geog) as a form or topical subdivision under kinds of products or merchandise, types of industries, etc., to works that contain or discuss detailed descriptions of the requirements for materials, dimensions, etc., to be used in products manufactured or supplied. Code the subdivision as a $v subfield for works that contain specifications and as an $x subfield for works that discuss specifications. Examples:

```
650 #0 $a Automobiles $x Specifications $z United States.
650 #0 $a Construction industry $x Specifications $z Great Britain.
650 #0 $a Optical materials $v Specifications $z France.
```

Assign the subdivision under an industry only in those cases where the discussion of specifications is not limited to a single product of the industry.
