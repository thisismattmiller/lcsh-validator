# H 2232 Vocational Guidance

## Validation rules

1. `--Vocational guidance` is a free-floating subdivision (topical, `$x`) for works describing careers in a field or organization and/or offering advice on how to prepare for, enter, and succeed in these careers. (H 2232 Background)
2. `--Vocational guidance` is (May Subd Geog): a geographic subdivision may follow it directly, e.g., `Dentistry--Vocational guidance--United States`. (H 2232 sec. 1)
3. Use `--Vocational guidance` under occupations, fields of endeavor, types of industries, trades, etc., e.g., `Bioengineering--Vocational guidance`; `Automobiles--Maintenance and repair--Vocational guidance`. (H 2232 sec. 1)
4. Do not establish or use phrase headings of the type `[...] as a profession` for vocational guidance in professional fields; that practice was discontinued in 1975. Use `[field]--Vocational guidance` instead. (H 2232 sec. 1)
5. Use `--Vocational guidance` under classes of persons or occupational groups only if the heading for the corresponding field or activity does not exist and cannot be established (e.g., `Allied health personnel--Vocational guidance`). If a heading for the field exists, prefer `[field]--Vocational guidance` over `[class of persons]--Vocational guidance`. (H 2232 sec. 2)
6. Use `--Vocational guidance` under names of individual corporate bodies and individual military services for works on careers in those organizations or services, e.g., `United States. National Aeronautics and Space Administration--Vocational guidance`; `United States. Army--Vocational guidance`. (H 2232 sec. 3)

## Full text

BACKGROUND: The free-floating subdivision --Vocational guidance is used under occupations, fields of endeavor, industries, names of corporate bodies, and individual military services, for works describing careers in these fields or organizations and/or offering advice on how to prepare for, enter, and succeed in these careers. This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning this subdivision.

### 1. Professions, fields of knowledge, industries, etc.

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Vocational guidance *(May Subd Geog)* under occupations, fields of endeavor, types of industries, trades, etc., for works on careers in these fields. Examples:

```
650 #0 $a Automobiles $x Maintenance and repair $x Vocational
            guidance.
650 #0 $a Bioengineering $x Vocational guidance.
650 #0 $a Dentistry $x Vocational guidance $z United States.
```

The former practice of establishing phrase headings of the type [...] as a profession for vocational guidance in professional fields was discontinued in 1975.

### 2. Classes of persons

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Vocational guidance *(May Subd Geog)* under classes of persons or occupational groups only if the heading for the corresponding field or activity does not exist and cannot be established. Example:

```
650 #0 $a Allied health personnel $x Vocational guidance.
```

### 3. Corporate bodies and individual military services

Assign the free-floating subdivision --Vocational guidance *(May Subd Geog)* under names of individual corporate bodies and individual military services for works on careers in those organizations or services. Examples:

```
610 10 $a United States. $b National Aeronautics and Space
            Administration $x Vocational guidance.
610 10 $a United States. $b Army $x Vocational guidance.
```
