# H 1965 Publishers' Catalogs

## Validation rules

1. Catalogs of an individual publishing house: `[name of publishing house]--Catalogs` ($v), `Publishers' catalogs--[country]`, and `[country]--Imprints--Catalogs` (both `$v`). (H 1965 sec. 1)
2. If the publishing house is a government agency, replace `[country]--Imprints--Catalogs` with a heading for the type of government publications, e.g. `Government publications--[country]--Bibliography--Catalogs`. (H 1965 sec. 1 note)
3. Publishers' catalogs on a specific topic: `[topic]--Bibliography--Catalogs` (`$v Bibliography $v Catalogs`), plus `[publisher]--Catalogs` and `Publishers' catalogs--[country]`. (H 1965 sec. 2)
4. Microform catalogs: `[topic]--Bibliography--Microform catalogs`, `[publisher]--Microform catalogs`, `Publishers' catalogs--[country]`, `Microforms--Catalogs`. (H 1965 sec. 3)
5. Use `[topic]--Bibliography--Microform catalogs` only if the catalog lists works on a specific topic. (H 1965 sec. 3)
6. Use `Microforms--Catalogs` only for general microform catalogs; for a specific microform use the specific heading, e.g. `Pamphlets in microform--Catalogs`, `Books on microfilm--Catalogs`, `Periodicals on microfiche--Catalogs`. (H 1965 sec. 3)
7. `--Bibliography` precedes `--Catalogs` / `--Microform catalogs`; `--Imprints` precedes `--Catalogs`. (H 1965 sec. 1-3)

## Full text

Note: The order of headings indicated below is not significant since the actual order is determined according to the procedures in H 80.

### 1. General rule

Assign the following combination of headings to catalogs of works issued by an individual publishing house:

```
(1) 610 20 $a [name of publishing house] $v Catalogs.
(2) 650 #0 $a Publishers' catalogs $z [country].
(3) 651 #0 $a [country] $v Imprints $v Catalogs.
```

Note: Consider a government agency or similar organization to be a publishing house in those cases where the body issues a catalog of its publications. If the publishing house is a government agency, assign instead of the third heading a heading for the type of government publications, such as Government publications--[country]--Bibliography--Catalogs.

### 2. Topical catalogs

Assign the following combination of headings to publishers' catalogs of works on a specific topic:

```
(1) 650 #0 $a [topic] $v Bibliography $v Catalogs.
(2) 610 20 $a [name of publishing house] $v Catalogs.
(3) 650 #0 $a Publishers' catalogs $z [country].
```

### 3. Microform catalogs

Assign the following combination of headings if the publishing house issues a catalog of its publications in microform:

```
(1) 650 #0 $a [topic] $v Bibliography $v Microform catalogs.
(2) 610 20 $a [name of publishing house] $v Microform catalogs.
(3) 650 #0 $a Publishers' catalogs $z [country].
(4) 650 #0 $a Microforms $v Catalogs.
```

Assign [topic]--Bibliography--Microform catalogs only if the catalog lists works on a specific topic. Assign Microforms--Catalogs only to general microform catalogs. If the catalog lists works in a specific microform, use the appropriate specific heading. Examples:

```
650 #0 $a Pamphlets in microform $v Catalogs.
650 #0 $a Books on microfilm $v Catalogs.
650 #0 $a Periodicals on microfiche $v Catalogs.
```
