# H 1678 Interviews

## Validation rules

1. The heading `Interviews` (geographically subdivided as needed, e.g. `Interviews--United States`) is for general collections of interviews with persons from a wide variety of groups or undesignated backgrounds. (H 1678 sec. D)
2. `--Interviews` is a free-floating form subdivision ($v) under classes of persons, ethnic groups, individual corporate bodies, and individual persons (including literary authors), e.g. `African Americans--Interviews`, `Children of divorced parents--United States--Interviews`, `Scientists--Hungary--Interviews`, `Women mountaineers--Europe--Interviews`, `Duchamp, Marcel, 1887-1968--Interviews`. (H 1678 sec. A, E.1, E.2)
3. `--Interviews` follows geographic and chronological subdivisions of the class-of-persons heading, e.g. `Authors, Argentine--20th century--Interviews`, `Terminally ill--Sweden--Interviews`. (H 1678 sec. E.1, E.2)
4. `--Interviews` may follow a class-of-persons subdivision under a corporate body, e.g. `United States. Navy--Surgeons--Interviews`, `Enron Corp.--Employees--Interviews`. (H 1678 sec. E.1, F)
5. `--Interviews` under a heading for a field or discipline (used only when no class-of-persons heading exists or can be established) is not free-floating and must be established. (H 1678 sec. E.1 Exception)
6. Under corporate bodies, including performing groups, `--Interviews` is used for interviews with persons associated with the body, e.g. `Minnesota Vikings (Football team)--Interviews`, `Guarneri Quartet--Interviews`; prefer more specific class-of-persons subdivisions such as `--Employees` or `--Presidents` where appropriate. (H 1678 sec. E.1)
7. `--Interviews` is also a free-floating topical subdivision ($x) under the same heading types for works about interviews, e.g. `Nixon, Richard M. (Richard Milhous), 1913-1994--Interviews`, `Presidents--United States--Interviews`. (H 1678 sec. F)
8. Do not subdivide additional topical headings (assigned to bring out the topics discussed) by `--Interviews`, e.g. `Soviet Union--Foreign relations--1953-1975`, not `...--Interviews`. (H 1678 sec. G)
9. [LC practice] LC does not use form subdivision `--Interviews`; it assigns the person/class/body heading without it plus LCGFT `Interviews` (or a more specific term). The $v form remains valid LCSH. (H 1678 sec. A, E)

## Full text

### A. Background

This instruction sheet provides guidelines for the use of the heading *Interviews*, as well as for the use of the free-floating subdivision *--Interviews*. The free-floating subdivision *--Interviews* is used as a form subdivision under headings for classes of persons, ethnic groups, names of individual corporate bodies, and names of individual persons, including literary authors, for transcripts of what was said during the course of interviews or conversations with one or more persons on one or more occasions. The free-floating subdivision *--Interviews* is used as a topical subdivision under the same types of headings for works about those interviews.

LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead assign an appropriate term or terms from Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. *Interviews*. To replace a form subdivision that includes a demographic group, also assign Library of Congress Demographic Groups Terms (LCDGT) to bring out the audience of the resource being cataloged, e.g. *German speakers* or *Arabic, Students of*.

For guidelines on bio-bibliography, see H 1328. For guidelines on biography, see H 1330. For guidelines on diaries, see H 1538. For guidelines on personal narratives of wars or disasters, see H 1928. For guidelines on genealogy and family histories, see H 1631.

### B. Recent revisions

2026-02: Updated template. Added instructions related to LCGFT implementation.

### C. Definitions

Interviews: transcripts of what was said during the course of interviews or conversations with one or more persons on one or more occasions.

### D. The heading Interviews

Assign the heading *Interviews* to general collections of interviews with persons from a wide variety of groups or with undesignated backgrounds.

Example:

```
Title: Talks with prominent Americans.
650 #0 $a Interviews $z United States.
```

### E. --Interviews as a form subdivision

### E.1. Classes of persons or ethnic groups

Assign a heading or headings of the type *[class of persons]--Interviews* or *[ethnic group]--Interviews* to works consisting of interviews with one or more persons from specific fields or ethnic groups.

Examples:

```
650 #0 $a African Americans $v Interviews.
650 #0 $a Children of divorced parents $z United States $v Interviews.
650 #0 $a Scientists $z Hungary $v Interviews.
650 #0 $a Terminally ill $z Sweden $v Interviews.
610 10 $a United States. $b Navy $x Surgeons $v Interviews.
650 #0 $a Women mountaineers $z Europe $v Interviews.
LC practice:
650 #0 $a Children of divorced parents $z United States.
655 #7 $a Interviews. $2 lcgft
```

Exception: If the heading representing the special class of persons does not exist and it is not possible to establish such a heading, use *--Interviews* under the heading for the appropriate field or discipline. In such situations the subdivision is not free floating and must be established. An example of a work requiring such treatment would be a collection of interviews with several persons involved in the field of dance, including dancers, choreographers, impresarios, composers, costume designers, patrons, etc.

Also use the subdivision *--Interviews* under names of individual corporate bodies, including performing groups, for collections of interviews with persons associated with the corporate body, for example, *Minnesota Vikings (Football team)--Interviews*; *Guarneri Quartet--Interviews*. Use more specific subdivisions representing classes of persons, for example, *--Employees* or *--Presidents*, under the corporate body heading, if appropriate.

LC practice: Assign the subject term for the class of persons, ethnic group, or corporate bodies if the group is a focus of the topic. If no appropriate class of persons exists, use the heading for the field or discipline. Also assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Interviews* or the more specific term.

### E.2. Individual persons

Use the free-floating subdivision *--Interviews* under names of individual persons, including literary authors, for works consisting of interviews with them.

LC practice: Assign the subject term for individual person. Also assign LCGFT appropriate to the form and/or genre of the resource in hand, e.g. LCGFT *Interviews* or the more specific term.

Examples:

```
Title: Entretiens avec Marcel Duchamp.
600 10 $a Duchamp, Marcel, $d 1887-1968 $v Interviews.
650 #0 $a Artists $z France $v Interviews.
LC practice:
600 10 $a Duchamp, Marcel, $d 1887-1968.
650 #0 $a Artists $z France.
655 #7 $a Interviews. $2 lcgft

Title: Borges at eighty : conversations.
600 10 $a Borges, Jorge Luis, $d 1899-1986 $v Interviews.
650 #0 $a Authors, Argentine $y 20th century $v Interviews.
LC practice:
600 10 $a Borges, Jorge Luis, $d 1899-1986.
650 #0 $a Authors, Argentine $y 20th century.
655 #7 $a Interviews. $2 lcgft
```

### F. --Interviews as a topical subdivision

For works about interviews or conversations with one or more persons on one or more occasions, use *--Interviews* as a topical subdivision under headings for classes of persons, ethnic groups, names of individual corporate bodies, and names of individual persons, including literary authors.

Examples:

```
Title: Frost/Nixon.
600 10 $a Nixon, Richard M. $q (Richard Milhous), $d 1913-1994 $x Interviews.
650 #0 $a Presidents $z United States $x Interviews.
650 #0 $a Ex-presidents $z United States $x Interviews.
600 10 $a Frost, David, $d 1939 Apr. 7-

Title: Enron 1986-2001.
610 20 $a Enron Corp. $x Employees $x Interviews.
650 #0 $a Energy industries $z United States.
```

### G. Assigning additional headings

Assign additional appropriate headings as needed to bring out the focus of the interviews or specific topics discussed. Do not further subdivide these headings by *--Interviews*.

Examples:

```
Title: Soviet leaders face American journalists.
651 #0 $a Soviet Union $x Foreign relations $y 1953-1975.
651 #0 $a Soviet Union $x Foreign relations $z United States.
651 #0 $a United States $x Foreign relations $z Soviet Union.
650 #0 $a Statesmen $z Soviet Union $v Interviews.
LC practice:
651 #0 $a Soviet Union $x Foreign relations $y 1953-1975.
651 #0 $a Soviet Union $x Foreign relations $z United States.
651 #0 $a United States $x Foreign relations $z Soviet Union.
650 #0 $a Statesmen $z Soviet Union.
655 #7 $a Interviews. $2 lcgft

Title: Ici et maintenant : conversations avec...
600 10 $a Mitterrand, François, $d 1916-1996 $v Interviews.
650 #0 $a Statesmen $z France $v Interviews.
651 #0 $a France $x Politics and government $y 1974-1981.
LC practice:
600 10 $a Mitterrand, François, $d 1916-1996.
650 #0 $a Statesmen $z France.
651 #0 $a France $x Politics and government $y 1974-1981.
655 #7 $a Interviews. $2 lcgft
```
