# H 1720 Legends and Stories about Animals

## Validation rules

1. Do not use `--Legends and stories` under types of animals (discontinued in 1981). (H 1720 sec. A)
2. Fictitious stories about an animal or group of animals take `[type of animal]--Fiction`, e.g. `Dogs--Fiction`; collections involving various types of animals take `Animals--Fiction`. (H 1720 sec. C)
3. Legends about an animal or group of animals take `[type of animal]--Folklore`, e.g. `Dogs--Folklore`; collections involving various animals take `Animals--Folklore`. (H 1720 sec. D)
4. Collections of true-life stories and individual biographies of animals take `[type of animal]--Biography`, with geographic subdivision interposed before `--Biography` if appropriate, e.g. `Dogs--Biography`, `Saint Bernard dog--Switzerland--Biography`, `Pets--England--Yorkshire--Biography--Juvenile literature`. (H 1720 sec. E.1, E.2)
5. A named individual animal (established per H 1332 sec. 9) is an additional name heading, e.g. `Barry (Dog), 1800-1814`, `Obama, Bo`; assign owner's name(s) if appropriate. (H 1720 sec. E.2, E.3)
6. Anecdotal accounts of pets take `[type of animal]--[place]--Anecdotes`, e.g. `Dogs--California--Anecdotes`, `Dogs--Washington (D.C.)--Anecdotes`. (H 1720 sec. E.3)
7. [LC practice] LC does not use `--Fiction`, `--Folklore`, `--Biography`, `--Anecdotes`, or `--Juvenile literature` as form subdivisions; it assigns LCGFT (e.g. `Animal fiction`, `Folk tales`, `Biographies`, `Anecdotes`) and LCDGT (e.g. `Children`). These remain valid LCSH. (H 1720 sec. A, C-E)

## Full text

### A. Background

Use of the subdivision *--Legends and stories* under types of animals was discontinued in 1981. Several subdivisions are now used in place of the discontinued subdivision.

LC practice: As stated in H 1075, LC catalogers will no longer use form subdivisions. Instead assign an appropriate term or terms from the Library of Congress Genre/Form Terms (LCGFT), e.g. *Fiction*, *Folk literature*, *Legends*, etc.

### B. Recent revisions

2026-02: Updated template. Added instructions related to LCGFT implementation.

### C. Fiction

To one or more fictitious stories about an animal or a group of animals, assign the subdivision *--Fiction* under the type of animal (cf. H 1790)

To collections of stories involving various types of animals, assign *Animals--Fiction*.

LC practice: Do not use a form subdivision of the type *--Fiction*. Instead assign an appropriate term or terms from LCGFT, e.g. *Fiction*, *Animal fiction*, etc.

Example:

```
650 #0 $a Dogs $v Fiction.
LC practice:
650 #0 $a Dogs.
655 #7 $a Animal fiction. $2 lcgft
```

### D. Folklore

To one or more legends about an animal or group of animals, assign the subdivision *--Folklore* under the type of animal (cf. H 1627).

To collections of legends involving various types of animals, assign *Animals--Folklore*.

LC practice: Do not use a form subdivision of the type *--Folklore*. Instead assign an appropriate term or terms from LCGFT, e.g. *Folk literature*, *Folk literature*, etc.

Example:

```
650 #0 $a Dogs $v Folklore.
LC practice:
650 #0 $a Dogs.
655 #7 $a Folk tales. $2 lcgft
```

### E. True stories about individual animals and pets

### E.1. Collections

To collections of true-life stories about animals, assign the subdivision *--Biography* under the type of animal. Interpose geographic subdivision if appropriate and assign additional headings as needed.

LC practice: Do not use a form subdivision of the type *--Biography*. Instead, assign an appropriate term or terms from LCGFT, e.g. *Biographies*, etc. To replace a form subdivision that includes a demographic group, also assign LCDGT to bring out the audience of the resource being cataloged, e.g. *Children*, *Teenagers*, etc.

Examples:

```
Title: Great canine heroes of all time.
650 #0 $a Dogs $v Biography.
LC practice:
650 #0 $a Dogs.
655 #7 $a Biographies. $2 lcgft

Title: James Herriot's treasury for children.
650 #0 $a Pets $z England $z Yorkshire $v Biography $v Juvenile literature.
650 #0 $a Domestic animals $z England $z Yorkshire $v Biography $v Juvenile literature.
650 #0 $a Farm life $z England $z Yorkshire $v Biography $v Juvenile literature.
LC practice:
650 #0 $a Pets $z England $z Yorkshire.
650 #0 $a Domestic animals $z England $z Yorkshire.
650 #0 $a Farm life $z England $z Yorkshire.
655 #7 $a Biographies. $2 lcgft
385 ## $a Children $2 lcdgt
```

### E.2. Individual biographies

For individual biographies, use the subdivision *--Biography* under types of animals, with geographic subdivision if appropriate. If the animal has a name that is established as a heading, or if the animal is well known enough for its name to be established, assign it as an additional heading. See H 1332, section 9, for guidelines on establishing headings for names of individual animals. Assign the owner's name(s) if appropriate.

LC practice: Do not use a form subdivision of the type *--Biography*. Instead, assign an appropriate term or terms from LCGFT, e.g. *Biographies*, etc. To replace a form subdivision that includes a demographic group, also assign LCDGT to bring out the audience of the resource being cataloged, e.g. *Children*, *Teenagers*, etc.

Example:

```
Title: Barry, the bravest Saint Bernard.
600 00 $a Barry $c (Dog), $d 1800-1814 $v Juvenile literature.
650 #0 $a Saint Bernard dog $z Switzerland $v Biography $v Juvenile literature.
650 #0 $a Rescue dogs $z Switzerland $v Biography $v Juvenile literature.
LC practice:
600 00 $a Barry $c (Dog), $d 1800-1814.
650 #0 $a Saint Bernard dog $z Switzerland.
650 #0 $a Rescue dogs $z Switzerland.
655 #7 $a Biographies. $2 lcgft
385 ## $a Children $2 lcdgt
```

### E.3. Anecdotes about pets

To anecdotal accounts of pets, assign the subdivision *--Anecdotes* under the type of animal, with local subdivision if appropriate. Assign the owner's name(s) if appropriate.

LC practice: Do not use a form subdivision of the type *--Anecdotes*. Instead assign an appropriate term or terms from LCGFT, e.g. *Anecdotes*, etc.

Example:

```
600 10 $a [name of owner]
650 #0 $a Dogs $z California $v Anecdotes.
LC practice:
600 10 $a [name of owner]
650 #0 $a Dogs $z California.
655 #7 $a Anecdotes. $2 lcgft
```

If the pet has a name that is established as a heading, or if the animal is well known enough for its name to be established, assign the name as an additional heading.

LC practice: Do not use a form subdivision of the type *--Anecdotes*. Instead assign an appropriate term or terms from LCGFT, e.g. *Anecdotes*, etc.

Example:

```
600 10 $a Obama, Bo.
650 #0 $a Dogs $z Washington (D.C.) $v Anecdotes.
LC practice:
600 10 $a Obama, Bo.
650 #0 $a Dogs $z Washington (D.C.)
655 #7 $a Anecdotes. $2 lcgft
```
