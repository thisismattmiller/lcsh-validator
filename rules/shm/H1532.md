# H 1532 Design and Construction

## Validation rules

1. Use `--Design and construction` under types of structures, machines, equipment, etc., for works on their engineering and/or construction, e.g. `Automobiles--Design and construction`; `Cattle--Housing--Design and construction`. (H 1532 sec. 1)
2. Use `--Design and construction` under `Examinations` and under headings for types of tests and individual examinations for works on methods of designing and constructing tests, e.g. `Multiple choice examinations--Design and construction`; `Graduate Record Examination--Design and construction`. (H 1532 sec. 2)
3. Use `--Design` under other topics where `--Design and construction` would not be appropriate, e.g. `Gardens--Design`; `Quilts--Design`. (H 1532 sec. 3)
4. Do not use `--Design` (free-floating) when a phrase heading has been established for the concept; use the phrase heading instead, e.g. `Costume design` not `Costume--Design`; `Magazine design` not `Magazines--Design`. (H 1532 sec. 3)
5. `--Designs and plans` is a form subdivision ($v) used under architectural and landscape headings for works containing architectural drawings, e.g. `Church architecture--Designs and plans`; `Landscape architecture--Designs and plans`. (H 1532 sec. 4)

## Full text

### Background

The free-floating subdivisions --Design and construction; --Design; --Designs and plans are used under certain types of topical headings. This instruction sheet provides guidelines for assigning these subdivisions.

### 1. Structures, machines, equipment

Use the free-floating subdivision --Design and construction under types of structures, machines, equipment, etc., for works on their engineering and/or construction, for example, *Automobiles--Design and construction*; *Cattle--Housing--Design and construction*.

### 2. Examinations

Use the free-floating subdivision --Design and construction under Examinations and under headings for types of tests and individual examinations for works on methods of designing and constructing tests, for example, *Multiple choice examinations--Design and construction*; *Graduate Record Examination--Design and construction*.

### 3. Other topics

Use the free-floating subdivision --Design under other topics where the use of --Design and construction would not be appropriate, for example, *Gardens--Design*; *Quilts--Design*.

Do not use the free-floating subdivision if a phrase heading has been established for the concept, for example, *Costume design*; *Magazine design*. Assign the phrase heading instead.

### 4. Designs and plans

Use the free-floating form subdivision --Designs and plans under architectural and landscape headings for works containing architectural drawings, for example, *Church architecture--Designs and plans*; *Landscape architecture--Designs and plans*.
